## Description

A panel discussion exploring moral dilemmas and human nature through thought experiments about pragmatism and self-interest. The title references the evolutionary argument that practical/self-interested behavior ("snobbery") is what allowed ancestors to survive — framing practical decision-making as morally defensible.

## Key Themes

- Moral philosophy applied to everyday decisions
- Self-interest vs. altruism in Chinese cultural context
- Thought experiments on pragmatism
- Social class, status, and inherited values
