## Gemini 4 Argon

- Google's Gemini 4 Argon went generally available, positioned for coding, knowledge work, enterprise workflows, and cyber defense.
- Introductory API pricing: $2/$10 per million input/output tokens. Artificial Analysis notes pricing doubles after November 30.
- Available in Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and Devin. Cursor reports 52.8% on CursorBench; Cognition reports 60.5% on FrontierCode 1.1.
- Artificial Analysis places Argon level with GPT-6 Astra at ~60% of the cost per task.
- Independent evaluations are mixed — Roboflow reports strong vision results; critics note reliability gaps and high false-claim rates on codebase analysis.

## Meta Muse Wonder

- Meta's Muse Wonder positioned for coding and personal assistant use.
- Early users report expensive subscriptions and rollout problems; Meta says it is fixing them.

## Other releases

- Artificial Analysis launched a Decision Index for decision models.
- Fish Audio released Drama 3, an open-weight text-to-speech model.
- Anthropic reported scaling to over 30,000 daily merges and replacing Git for its monorepo.

## Safety and policy
