# Guest: Anastasis Germanidis, Co-founder & Co-CEO of Runway

## Show Notes

This episode explores Runway's advancement from generative video tools to comprehensive world models, specifically discussing the new GWM Worlds 2 system and its innovative WorldPrompt feature.

### Key Topics Covered

**WorldPrompt Innovation**
WorldPrompt functions as a control mechanism allowing users to "fix some aspects of a simulated environment" while creating timestamped events that can be prompted in real-time, enabling detailed scene control similar to video game mechanics.

**Technical Architecture**
The system converts bidirectional diffusion models into autoregressive generators, producing real-time 720p video at 24fps with synchronized audio. This required developing distillation methods to reduce computational demands while maintaining quality.

**Core Challenges**
- Error accumulation in autoregressive generation
- Maintaining long-term consistency across extended sequences
- Generating counterfactual outcomes accurately
- Managing memory and context efficiently

**Broader Applications Beyond Gaming**
- Robotics simulation and policy evaluation
- Synthetic data generation for AI agents
- Interface design through neural rendering
- Real-world correlation testing for robotic systems

**Company Evolution**
Runway progressed from creative post-production tools through Gen-1 and Gen-2 video models to current world model research, driven by scaling investments and team expansion focused on both technical and creative expertise.
