# Gemini 4 Carbon tops leaderboards, launches for cyber defenders
# URL: https://latent.space/p/ainews-gemini-4-carbon/
# Date: 2026-10-29
# Source: Latent Space (AI News)

Author: swyx (.ai)

**Launch:** Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon, successor to Gemini 4 Argon (launched ~3 weeks prior). Positioned as state of the art on cybersecurity and long-horizon tasks.

**Cyber benchmarks (Google's claims):**
- 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro (vulnerability finding)
- 54.8% on ExploitGym (exploitation)
- 71.0% on CWE-bench (patching)

**Access:** Initially limited to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program; broader developer access "as soon as possible."

**Independent evaluations:**
- Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5: #1 with score 59
- Arena: #1 in Text Arena
- Vals: #2 overall, #1 on Finance and Legal benchmarks

**Pricing:** Disputed — sources cite either $2/$10 per million input/output tokens, or $4/$20 with 50% launch discount.

**Specs:** 1M-token context window, 1M max output.

**Caveats:** Coding-agent results measured only in Antigravity CLI (other harnesses untested). Hallucination rate rose from 4% (Argon) to 11% — still second lowest measured.

**Other news covered:** Open-weight releases, Claude Code usage complaints, Opus 5.5 math claims, research and infrastructure developments.
