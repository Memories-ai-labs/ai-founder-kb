Good Start Labs explores whether AI models can develop transferable skills by training on strategic games. The company was founded by Alex Duffy and Tyler Marques after spinning out from Every with $3.6 million in funding.

## Key Research Finding

The most compelling evidence comes from training a 30-billion parameter model on *1830: The Game of Railroads and Robber Barons*. Significantly, only models trained with a "multi-turn terminal agent" design — which required using tools, planning strategies, and adapting in real time — improved performance on real financial research tasks. The single-turn question-answering approach did not transfer.

## Core Insight

Duffy emphasizes that "How you design that [the harness] totally changes what the model learns." The training environment's structure matters enormously for whether capabilities generalize beyond games.

## Evidence of Transfer

- 1830 railroad game training enhanced financial research abilities
- Diplomacy game training produced better customer support agents

## Products

Good Start Labs primarily sells reinforcement learning data (agent trajectories from games) and customizable learning environments to frontier AI labs.

## Relevance to AI Founders

Training environment design — not just model size or algorithm choice — is a first-class variable in capability generalization. The harness determines what the model actually learns.
