## Google's Empirical Research Assistance (ERA)

Platt's team developed ERA, a system that automates solutions to scientific problems by treating them as optimization tasks. The approach uses Gemini to maintain a tree of experimental notebooks, similar to Monte Carlo Tree Search, selecting promising branches via the Upper Confidence Bound algorithm.

The system proposes mutations at each iteration — "It's almost like having a hyper-eager grad student who doesn't sleep." A significant performance jump occurred between Gemini 2.0 and 2.5, enabling the system to solve multiple outstanding problems, resulting in at least ten published papers.

**Key caution:** Platt emphasizes that ERA is "a power tool. It can slice your fingers off," warning against overfitting and reward hacking. His recommendation for baseline modeling: "Always just fit linear regression."

## Climate Change Solutions

**Contrails:** Airplane condensation trails account for approximately 1% of human-induced global warming. Ice crystals form when exhaust seeds water vapor in ice-supersaturated air, with one gram of exhaust creating ten kilograms of ice. ERA helped solve the counterfactual modeling problem for measuring warming prevention.

**FireSat:** A satellite constellation concept for identifying fires before they become uncontrollable.

## Career Philosophy & Advice

Platt emphasizes developing deep domain expertise and learning through hands-on implementation. He advocates for balancing optimization with preservation of learning opportunities: "You do lose something when you hyper-optimize. It's overfit."

His core guidance echoes Feynman's principle: "You must not fool yourself, and you are the easiest person to fool."
