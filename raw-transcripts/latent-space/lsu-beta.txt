We aim to get you competent and conversant in all major modalities and to have built actual working products with them so you have hands-on experience with the major Software 3.0 advancements of the past year.

The course content is:

GPT API Basics

Prompt Tooling and Retrieval Augmented Generation

Code Generation with GPT

Image Generation with Dall-E, Stability, Lexica, and Midjourney

Speech-to-Text with Whisper

Finetuning & Running Open Models

Build Your First AI Agent

Each lesson is scoped to be ~1hr and will come with video instruction. We’ll offer 2-48 extra hours worth of rabbit holes and tidbits should you want to go further.

We are looking for beta testers. We’ll be sending you our drafts as and when we finish them over the coming weeks.

Course is completely free. Please sign up here and we’ll see you after you confirm! NOTE: this course is not fully live yet, we are running it with a few hundred beta testers for now. Signing up just puts you on the list for when it is done.

Please join our community if you’d like to go through this content with a group in person or online.

We are also interested in having sponsors support our work. We expect thousands of developers to go through this course each month. Please reach out to Noah Hein to coordinate.

For advanced folks, we’d love to expand this to a 30 day version. What else should we add? How do we sequence it? Please let us know.
