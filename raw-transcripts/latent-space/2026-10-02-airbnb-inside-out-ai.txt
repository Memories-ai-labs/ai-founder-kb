## Episode Overview

Ahmad Al-Dahle, Chief Technology Officer at Airbnb (formerly head of generative AI at Meta), describes Airbnb's "inside-out AI" strategy — applying AI both internally (engineering and operations) and externally (guest experience).

## Key Metrics

- 60% of Airbnb's code is now AI-authored
- Company shipped nearly 80% more features and improvements annually after AI adoption
- Engineers show 1.6x increased pull-request throughput
- Roughly half of customer support tickets resolved by AI systems

## Internal Transformation

- Reorganized development process to work "directly with prototypes" rather than traditional team handoffs
- Code is now the artifact for collaboration, replacing lengthy documentation
- Customer support AI systems trained on synthetic data before production deployment
- Human oversight maintained for sensitive issues

## Everest (Internal Tool)

Airbnb's internal context graph called Everest leverages LLMs and embeddings to connect company knowledge:
- Enabled grocery delivery launch in 8 months
- Enabled airport pickups launch in just 6 weeks

## Engineering Philosophy

Al-Dahle emphasizes that maintaining engineering craftsmanship remains critical — engineers must be able to explain AI-generated work thoroughly. AI acceleration does not remove the need for deep technical understanding.

## Signal

★★★ [AI-Native Engineering]: Airbnb's metrics (60% AI-authored code, 80% more features, 1.6x PR throughput) provide concrete benchmarks for AI-augmented engineering teams. The "inside-out" framing — internal transformation before external product — is a repeatable playbook.
