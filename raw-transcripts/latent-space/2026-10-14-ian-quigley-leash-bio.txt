**Guest:** Ian Quigley, Leash Bio
**Byline:** Brandon Anderson

Leash Bio specializes in DNA-encoded libraries (DELs), a high-throughput method for measuring protein-small molecule interactions. The episode covers how the team made this notoriously difficult assay produce clean, reproducible data suitable for large-scale modeling.

Key topics:
- Their Kaggle competition, which showed that models struggled to generalize to unseen chemical libraries, and that models can exploit hidden patterns such as who made a molecule
- Hermes model: trained only on Leash Bio's own data
- Minos: an agent sandbox where LLMs reason about structure-activity relationships using the company's lab-in-the-loop data
- Minos preprint, blog post, and open dataset were released alongside the episode

The lab-in-the-loop approach integrates AI agents into the physical lab workflow, running experiments at compute speed and feeding results back into model training in tight iteration cycles.
