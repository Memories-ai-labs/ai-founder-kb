[NOTE] AI News briefing covering October 29-30, 2026 events.

MAIN STORY: Google Search revenue hit by AI Mode
Google's Chief Business Officer Philipp Schindler admitted that strong growth in AI Mode queries hurt results because AI Mode monetizes at a lower rate than traditional Search. Combined with other weak earnings, this triggered steep tech stock declines (-15%, ~$500B drop, worst since 2004 IPO). AI summaries reduce the need to visit source pages, straining Google's relationship with publishers.

OpenAI reportedly raised at a $1.25T valuation rather than $1.4T — seen as a negative signal ahead of a major tech IPO.

SIGNIFICANCE: First major documented case of an AI-native product (AI Mode) cannibalizing its parent company's (Google's) core revenue stream. Signals the structural tension between AI search and ad-supported search business models.
