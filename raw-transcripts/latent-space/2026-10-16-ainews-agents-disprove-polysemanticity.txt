The lead story covers Goodfire researcher Nick Cammarata's essay "Neural Networks Are Already Clean." He argues that many cases of polysemanticity and superposition, where neurons appear to encode tangled mixtures of concepts, dissolve under further agentic analysis. Commentators cited in the piece, including Deedy, summarized the claim as "neural networks are not tangled knots," and roon called it a possible major moment in science. Neel Nanda said it doesn't change his core views, and Leo Gao, Jake Mendel, and others raised methodological critiques. The paper itself wasn't yet public at the time of writing.
