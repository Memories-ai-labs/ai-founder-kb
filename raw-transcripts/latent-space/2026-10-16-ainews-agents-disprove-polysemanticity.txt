## Title Story: "Neural Networks Are Already Clean"

- Nick Cammarata (Goodfire) published essay arguing many cases of polysemanticity and superposition mostly disappear under further agentic analysis
- Claim: most neurons, even in models up to trillion parameters, are legible
- Many well-known polysemantic examples were misread
- His 2023 automated-interpretability work at OpenAI found ~2% of neurons legible — new work reverses this picture
- Reactions: roon and others call it a major moment for alignment and P(doom) reduction
- Neel Nanda: "doesn't change my core views"
- Critics (Leo Gao, Jake Mendel): methodological concerns raised; paper not yet public
