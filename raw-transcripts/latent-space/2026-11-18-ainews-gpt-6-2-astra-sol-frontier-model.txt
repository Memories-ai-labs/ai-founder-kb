## AI News Summary

**Main story: GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol**

OpenAI released two models on the same day Anthropic's first trading day was expected.

**Model comparison:**
- Sol matches September Astra at ~15% of the cost per task
- New Astra scores 62 on Intelligence Index, behind Anthropic's Fable 5.2 at 65
- Conclusion: Anthropic still has the best model; OpenAI has the most cost-efficient one

**API pricing:**
- Astra: $8 input / $40 output per million tokens
- Sol: $2 input / $10 output per million tokens
- Both: 1M-token context window, 128K output limit

**Rollout:** ChatGPT and Codex paid tiers and developer API. Sol expected to be the main workhorse.

**Mixed early reception:**
- Matt Shumer called models "essentially unusable" and said agents rejected Astra's subagent work
- Pietro Schirano attributed problems to the harness rather than the model
- OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux defended the launch, acknowledging some RL artifacts

**Benchmarks and safety:**
- Astra led Vals' multi-turn Vibe Code Bench and Artificial Analysis 3D/robotics previews
- OpenAI rated Astra "High" for AI self-improvement — first for an externally released OpenAI model
