## Summary

Anthropic completed its IPO, priced at $83/share (ticker: $ANT), making it the largest IPO in history.

**IPO performance:**
- Priced at $83/share
- Opened at $111.50
- Closed at $97.17 (~17% above offering price)
- After-hours: $92.60

**Offering size:** $81.3B raise, overtaking SpaceX's June IPO as the largest in history.

**Governance:** Class F shares carry 50.1% of voting power on most shareholder matters.

**Lockups:** ~$1T of shares become tradable over the next year as lockup expirations hit.

**Safety commitment:** Dario and Daniela Amodei stated Anthropic would pace training and deployment based on whether its systems can stay safe and under human control.

**Financials/investors:**
- Google reportedly owns 13.7%
- AMD agreed to invest $2.5B
- Low cash burn relative to capital raised

**Competitive context:** Google's Gemini 4 Neon launch and OpenAI's GPT-6.2 release happened around the same time.

**Opinion:** Reactions ranged from celebratory to skeptical, including concerns about whether $2T valuation can be supported by revenue.
