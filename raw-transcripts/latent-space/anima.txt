## Summary

Anima Anandkumar discusses her two-decade journey through AI, from classical mathematics to deep learning, now focusing on modeling physical systems. Developed FourCastNet, an open-source AI weather model competitive with physics-based simulations, enabling accurate weather predictions on consumer-grade GPUs.

## Key Points

**Physics vs language modeling distinction:** Unlike language models that scale with more tokens, physics simulation requires structural inductive biases due to limited datasets (tens to hundreds of thousands of examples) and extreme context lengths (hundreds of billions tokens for industrial-scale resolution). "We have foundation models for language, not for physics."

**Neural Operators:** Anandkumar pioneered Neural Operators, enabling AI to combine data with physical laws for multi-scale modeling. The Fourier Neural Operator learns in frequency domains, with spherical variants powering FourCastNet 3 for stable global weather forecasting.

**FourCastNet:** Open-source AI weather model competitive with physics-based simulations. Enables accurate weather predictions on consumer-grade GPUs.

**TorchLean:** Framework integrating PyTorch-style networks with the Lean proof assistant for formal neural network verification.

**Fusion plasma disruption prediction:** Applied AI to predict and prevent plasma disruptions in fusion reactors.

**UN Scientific Advisory Board:** Anandkumar recently appointed to the UN Scientific Advisory Board.

**Core thesis:** Progress in physical AI "comes from building in structure and inductive biases" rather than pure scaling approaches. This is a direct challenge to the scaling-only paradigm dominant in language model development.
