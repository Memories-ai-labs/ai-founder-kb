## Key Topics

**Modal's Business**
- AI infrastructure platform with compute and storage products
- Customers include Suno, Runway, and Cognition
- Over $1B in annualized run rate; $750M Series D at $16B valuation

**Founding Story**
- Erik's path: Spotify recommendation systems → CTO at Better.com → founding Modal
- Early traction came when Stable Diffusion created demand for GPU inference

**GPU Capacity Economics**
- Demand is "not rectangular" unlike fixed-term capacity contracts
- Modal pools demand across thousands of customers to smooth it out
- Moved from mostly spot/1-year reservations to multi-year deals with ~25 clouds
- Runs a dedicated procurement team

**Renting vs. Owning**
- Renting has capital-efficiency benefits; ownership brings financial/operational risk
- More worried about GPU prices collapsing (leaving reserved buyers with expensive supply) than rising

**Alternative Accelerators**
- Alternative accelerators increasingly interesting
- Disaggregated inference setups mentioned as promising

**Sandboxes**
- Launched 2023; now ~1/3 of revenue
- Driven by coding agents, background agents, and reinforcement learning
- Key development areas: snapshotting, forking, fast startup
- Scaling to 1M concurrent sandboxes

**Security**
- Addressed Hugging Face incident (vulnerable app on Modal, not a Modal breach)
- Exploring safer defaults; using AI agents for internal security testing

**Products**
- Modal Functions: ~2/3 of revenue, mostly inference
- Modal Sandboxes: ~1/3 of revenue
- Other bets: training for small/mid workloads, batch processing, low-latency voice

**Team & Culture**
- ~200 employees, growing to ~250 by year-end, possibly 700 next year
- Mostly systems engineers
- Code review is optional; relies on strong CI/CD and testing

**Cloud vs. On-Prem**
- Favors multi-tenant cloud; skeptical of on-prem and BYOC
- Would consider BYOC at the right price
