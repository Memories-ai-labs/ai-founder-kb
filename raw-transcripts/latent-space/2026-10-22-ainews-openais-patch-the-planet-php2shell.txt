# [AINews] OpenAI's Patch the Planet program is 1 of 2 parties to discover PHP2Shell vulnerabilities in core PHP affecting 70% of the web, using GPT-5.6 Sol; coordinated response over 2 months
# URL: https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-openais-patch-the-planet-program
# Date: 2026-10-22
# Source: Latent Space (AINews)

## Summary

Two independent parties discovered two PHP-engine memory-corruption bugs (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619), dubbed PHP2Shell. One party was Calif, a security firm working with OpenAI's "Patch the Planet" program using GPT-5.6 Sol. The second was Wiz, recently acquired by Google Cloud, crediting Gemini 4 Argon and its own tooling.

## Key Points

- **PHP2Shell vulnerabilities:** Two remote code execution bugs in core PHP affecting WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla. Researchers demonstrated memory disclosure and RCE. Estimates suggest 70% of the web runs PHP.

- **OpenAI's Patch the Planet:** This is OpenAI's AI-assisted security research program. Used GPT-5.6 Sol to discover one set of the vulnerabilities over approximately two months of coordinated remediation with maintainers, security providers, and governments.

- **Parallel discovery:** Wiz (Google Cloud) independently found the same CVEs using Gemini 4 Argon and their own tooling — a significant moment where two AI systems from competing labs converged on the same zero-day.

- **Coordinated response:** PHP Foundation helped with patches. Cloudflare deployed WAF mitigations as a stopgap before scheduled patch release. NSA advised immediate updates or taking systems offline.

- **Credit and attribution debate:** The article covers the ethics and logistics of credit among human researchers, AI models, and vendor organizations when AI-assisted discovery is involved.

- **Remediation nuance:** "Updating closes a vulnerability; it does not establish that credentials or data were never exposed." — the article's key warning to system operators.

- **Evidence gaps:** The article notes: exact fixed versions not fully confirmed, successful exploitation vs. scanning not always distinguished, validated exposure counts lacking.

- **Broader implication for AI security:** This is one of the clearest public examples of AI models being used as offensive security research tools at production scale, by multiple major labs simultaneously.
