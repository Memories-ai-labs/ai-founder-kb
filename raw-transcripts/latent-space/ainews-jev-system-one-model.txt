TypeSafe launched Jev, a "System One Model" focused exclusively on decision-making tasks rather than text generation.

## Key Claims

- 20–200x faster than competing frontier models
- 40–400x cheaper with free output tokens
- Trained using RLCD (reinforcement learning for calibrated decisions)

## Functionality

Jev is optimized for "decides/classifies/routes/scores" operations. It cannot produce free-form text and requires predefined output formats, functioning as a "structured classifier / judge / routing policy" rather than a general language model.

## Design Philosophy

Jev complements slower "System Two" LLMs by offering parallel sampling, reduced hallucination, and calibration — sacrificing reasoning and coding capabilities for speed and cost efficiency.

## Reception

Topped Hacker News and generated millions of social media views. Community discussion compared it to DSPy-style structured prediction abstractions.

## Relevance to AI Founders

Specialization of model roles (inference vs. classification vs. routing) may allow significant cost and latency reduction in agentic architectures. The "System One / System Two" framing of model specialization is gaining traction as a product design pattern.
