## Emerging Model Category: "System One" Decision Models

**TypeSafe AI's Jev** represents a new classification of models focused on structured decision-making:
- Returns typed decisions with probabilities rather than reasoning text
- 277× cheaper than GPT-6 for judgment tasks
- ~$0.044 per 1K judgments

Alternative approaches including CLM (Contrastive Language Model) and specialized classifiers showed competitive performance, with community debate on whether Jev is fundamentally new vs. scaled classification.
