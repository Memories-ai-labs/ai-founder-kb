Length: 84 minutes

## Key Points

**Agents reshaping Linear's core:**
- Linear launched its MCP server in May 2025
- Agents created ~3% of issues a year ago; now create more than 50%
- Product repositioned as a "product development system built around agent orchestration" (after CEO Karri Saarinen's March post: "Issue tracking is dead")
- Pre-AI companies like Ramp and Coinbase wanted APIs to plug in their own internal agents

**Enterprise timing:**
- Three weeks after joining in 2023, Cordova removed "move upmarket to enterprise" from company goals (Linear had one salesperson)
- Enterprise sales started in 2025, but agent features took priority over planned enterprise roadmap

**Go-to-market principles:**
- Never hired SDRs or forward deployed engineers
- AEs handle outbound; solutions engineers support enterprise deals
- Support stays in-house; AI drafts replies but humans decide what gets sent

**Pricing:**
- Seats remain
- Linear Agent chat is free
- Coding sessions pass through API costs with small markup
- Loops use AI credits
- New AI features start free in beta; prices set high and lowered over time

**Headcount philosophy:**
- Stripe-derived rule: keep at least 40% of company in engineering, product, design, and data
- Hiring uses work trials

**Stats:**
- August employee stock sale at $2.5B valuation
- 40,000+ paying customers
- 177% net revenue retention

**Key caution:** Some AI companies reaching $100M ARR quickly rely on a few very large contracts — not the same as broad adoption.
