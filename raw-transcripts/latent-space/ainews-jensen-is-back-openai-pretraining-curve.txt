[AI News Daily Digest — November 23, 2026]

## Top Stories

### 1. Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

Eleven days after announcing his resignation, Jensen Huang returned as CEO, president, and board member. The Nvidia board voted unanimously, citing input from senior U.S. officials. Key details:
- Colette Kress remains CFO
- Independent directors' investigation continues
- Huang has a Senate hearing in approximately eight days
- Nvidia formed a board committee on national security and export control

### 2. OpenAI Publishes Its Pretraining Loss Curve

Chief scientist Jakub Pachocki's essay "Perplexity and Intelligence" plots base-model log-perplexity from GPT-3.5 onward on an internal held-out coding dataset. He proposes that labs agree on a shared first limit on AI development, with defined criteria for crossing it. Extrapolation suggests another capability jump around 0.68–0.70 on this metric.

### 3. Microsoft Joins the Transparency Proposal

Mustafa Suleyman's Microsoft AI reported 0.819 for its MAI-2 Base model on OpenAI's evaluation set. Critics raised concerns about:
- Tokenizer bias
- Share of compute spent on post-training
- Synthetic training data
Note: Anthropic has not joined the initiative and has redacted similar pretraining metrics.
