# [AINews] Modal's $1B ARR, $16B Series D makes it NYC's newest AI Infra Decacorn
# URL: https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-modals-1b-arr-16b-series-d
# Date: 2026-10-15
# Source: Latent Space

[NOTE] Partial content — article is behind paywall after summary section.

Modal crossed $1B in annualized run rate and raised a $750M Series D at a $15.75B valuation, led by Accel. Usage has tripled since its Series C, and revenue grew more than 15x year over year. This moves Modal from the "infra unicorn" category to "infra decacorn."

Modal's Sandboxes product, launched in 2023 after noticing people using LLMs to generate code, is now a roughly $300M run-rate business on its own, with close to 3 billion sandboxes spun up and up to a million running concurrently. Use cases include background agents, reinforcement learning, and vibecoding platforms. CEO Erik Bernhardsson credited the AI agent wave as the primary growth driver.

The broader AINews roundup covers model launches including Google's Gemini 4 Argon GA, independent evaluations, inference infrastructure, safety incidents, and industry funding. Those sections are paywalled.
