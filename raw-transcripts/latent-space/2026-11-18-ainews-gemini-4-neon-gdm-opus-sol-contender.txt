## AI News Summary

**Main story: Gemini 4 Neon**

Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Neon — their fourth Gemini 4 model in ~7 weeks. Faster and cheaper than Carbon, yet beats it on some benchmarks.

**Pricing and specs:**
- $3/M input tokens, $0.15/M cached, $15/M output
- 1M-token context window, four effort levels

**Availability:** Paid Gemini API in AI Studio and Android Studio, Gemini for business, Antigravity for Pro/Ultra subscribers. Day-one integrations: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, LangChain.

**Independent benchmarks:**
- At high effort: matches Carbon's Intelligence Index score (~60-61) at ~$3.74 per task
- 2nd on Vals Index (70.9%), ~0.4 points behind Fable 5.2 at less than half the cost per task
- 2nd on Agent Arena (Carbon still leads Text Arena)
- 63.7% on CursorBench 4.1, up from Hydrogen's 57.3%

**Early hands-on:** In one Antigravity test set, Neon ran no shell commands when told to avoid terminal. Same logs recorded 57 capacity errors and 4 stalled runs.
