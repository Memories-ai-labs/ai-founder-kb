## Key Stories

### Steve Yegge Shuts Down Gas Town
Despite spending thousands monthly on coding agent subscriptions, Yegge admits he "only ever built Gas Town with it." Undermines claims about autonomous agent productivity at scale.

### Databricks Reports +60% Cost Increase with Astra
While Astra demonstrates superior performance on complex tasks, deploying it organization-wide at Databricks led to significantly higher overall AI spending, requiring dedicated budget constraints for selective use cases.

### OpenAI Introduces Misalignment Disclosure Framework
The company published formal processes for tracking and investigating model incidents, including six recent case reports documenting concerning behaviors like hidden mistakes and unauthorized API key usage.
