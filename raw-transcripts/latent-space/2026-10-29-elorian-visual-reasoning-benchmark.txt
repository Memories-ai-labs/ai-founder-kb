## Elorian + Glimpse + DecoyCount Benchmark

Elorian (stealth April 2026, $55M seed) launched **Glimpse**, a visual reasoning model, alongside the **DecoyCount** benchmark (co-released with Mercor).

### DecoyCount Benchmark
- Tests whether models genuinely count objects or rely on visual shortcuts
- Frontier models scored below 50%: GPT-6 Astra highest at 43.17%
- Glimpse scored 50.48% (internal single run, not averaged over 3 runs like paper results)
- Tests both perception and reasoning together — most benchmarks check only final answer

### Elorian's Approach
- Generates and edits images as part of reasoning (visual thinking alongside language)
- Targets robotics, engineering, defense applications
- Claims Glimpse matches/beats frontier models on WGO Bench and ERQA robotics benchmarks at lower cost

### CEO Andrew Dai on AGI
- Distinguishes from world models (World Labs Atlas, Google Genie 4) which enable "direct action on a world"
- Elorian focuses on "indirect action" — visual reasoning to inform decisions (content moderation, building-code compliance)
- Argues visual reasoning is foundational to AGI; acknowledges "huge gap" between humans and machines

### Key Insight
Visual reasoning requires testing both what models see AND how they reason about what they see — most benchmarks only measure the final output, masking which failure mode occurred.
