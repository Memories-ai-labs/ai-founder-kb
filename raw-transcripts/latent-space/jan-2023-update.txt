Hello to new latent spacenauts joining our journey!

Capture the Prompt - a Reverse Prompt Engineering game

Since the success of Reverse Prompt Engineering for Fun and (no) Profit I’ve been thinking about more ways to test the assumptions of prompt injection fears, particularly claims that it is unsolvable. There was one very obvious “unpwnable” strategy that I had not seen anyone attempt yet, so yesterday I coded up a quick concept to demonstrate it. Some friends beta tested, and two actually managed to pwn it. So I am downgrading my claim from “this is literally unpwnable” to “hey here’s a fun Capture the Prompt game!”

Try it now: https://unpwnable.netlify.app/

And try not to spoil it for others on social media, but you can leave guesses and successful prompt injection strategies below in the comments!

Podcast

You know what the world really needs? Yet another AI podcast. 

Alessio, a good friend from Decibel.vc and I are starting one. We’ve been surveying the existing landscape of AI podcasts (shoutouts to Lukas Biewald’s Gradient Dissent, The Gradient, Michael Trazzi’s The Inside View, TalkRL, Sam Charrington’s TWIML, Papers Read, Robot Brains, AI Pub’s Deep Papers and Elad Gil/Sarah Guo’s upcoming No Priors). There are still voices we wanted to hear from that aren’t being covered.

We’re teasing (but not releasing) ours today. Get in the comments to tell us who you want to hear from!
