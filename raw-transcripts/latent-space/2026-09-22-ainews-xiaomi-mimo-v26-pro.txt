## Summary

Xiaomi has emerged as a frontier AI lab with the release of MiMo-V2.6-Pro, an open-weights omnimodal model trained for approximately $3 million. The newsletter documents the accelerating release cadence from Chinese AI labs.

## Key Developments

**Xiaomi Model Release:**
The MiMo-V2.6 series includes two natively omnimodal models: MiMo-V2.6-Pro (top capability) and MiMo-V2.6-Flash (efficiency/capability balance). The Pro variant achieved top ranking on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index at competitive cost efficiency.

**Technical Approach:**
Reinforcement learning scaled across three dimensions:
- Larger batch processing (1,568 samples per update)
- Diverse multi-task training environments
- Enhanced grading compute for reward signals

Training consumed approximately $2.6 million and 130 hours.

**Open-Source Contribution:**
Xiaomi committed to open-sourcing training environments, code, and recipes covering:
- Coding, cybersecurity, general knowledge work, visual design, music generation
- 7,000+ task datasets remain unreleased (only code/recipes open-sourced)
