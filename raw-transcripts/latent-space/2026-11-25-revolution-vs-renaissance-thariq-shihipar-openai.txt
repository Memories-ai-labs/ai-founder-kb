## Summary

Thariq Shihipar explores how AI makes software cheap to produce in volume while still leaving room to perfect one thing — the "revolution vs renaissance" framing.

Key topics:
- Where agents surprised him (unexpected capability jumps in coding agents)
- The `/human` folder: a demo where he edits a rock's size in a human-editable folder and immediately tests the throw in a fighting game
- Software as craft: what it means when software can be generated cheaply but mastery still matters
- Game balance and design models: how decision models choose from actions a designer defines
- The land/water test: heuristic for evaluating what AI can and cannot do
- Personal software: building tools for yourself with AI
- Autoresearch and visibility of work
- Built a Mac Dock that switched modes, then found he valued noticing when he got distracted more than using it
