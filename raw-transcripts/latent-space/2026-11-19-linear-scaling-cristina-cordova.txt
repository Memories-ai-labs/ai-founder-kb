# Scaling Linear's GTM to $100M ARR — Cristina Cordova, COO
# URL: https://www.latent.space/p/linear-scaling/
# Date: 2026-11-19
# Source: Latent Space

## Guest

Cristina Cordova, COO of Linear. Previously led partnerships and platform at Stripe.

## Key topics

### Agents as the new users
- Linear's MCP server launched May 2025; now more than half of issues are created by agents
- Key tension: a product that is *only* a database behind an MCP server gives users no reason to open the app
- Test: agent usage and app usage should both keep growing — not trade off against each other

### Repositioning: "Issue tracking is dead"
- When customers asked what they'd be buying once manual tracking disappeared, Linear repositioned as a product development system built around agent orchestration
- Cristina's thesis: Linear is small enough to cannibalize itself; a large incumbent like Google is not

### AI budgets and deal structure
- New AI budgets speed up purchases
- Buyers shifting toward 1-year deals and paid pilots vs. multi-year contracts
- Warning sign: some fast-growing AI companies depend on 1-2 very large contracts — Linear has a broad base instead

### Enterprise GTM
- Dropped "move upmarket" goal in Cristina's first weeks, then pursued enterprise in 2025 after agent features became the priority
- Some planned enterprise work never completed due to agent feature priority

### Go-to-market model
- No SDRs, no forward deployed engineers
- Account executives do their own outbound
- Solutions engineers support enterprise deals
- "Linear should be easy enough to adopt that nobody needs access to your systems"

### Support
- Support kept in-house; AI drafts replies, human decides what gets sent

### Pricing
- Seats remain the base pricing unit
- AI features launch free in beta with advance notice before charges
- Coding sessions: pass-through API cost + small markup; Loops use AI credits
- Principle: "prices start high and come down rather than the other way around"

### Hiring and headcount
- Linear runs leaner than peers at its stage
- Stripe benchmark: keep at least 40% of staff in engineering/product/design/data (Linear has no formal version)
- Hiring uses work trials on real problems, not just interviews

## Notable quotes

- "They want a demo, not questions about their budget" — on why Linear skips qualification calls
- "AI drafts replies and a human decides what goes out" — on support
- "Prices start high and come down rather than the other way around" — on pricing
- "Linear should be easy enough to adopt that nobody needs access to your systems" — on no forward deployed engineers
