## Key Topics

**Background**
- Anekha studied mathematics, operational research, statistics, and economics at Warwick
- Worked in energy derivatives at JPMorgan, then moved to buy side
- Came to the US on a Fulbright scholarship before founding Moshi

**What Moshi Does**
- Real-time insights product for financial traders
- Focuses on commodity, energy, and macro markets
- Processes structured data (futures time series, technical indicators) and unstructured data (news, social media, podcasts, users' private data like internal analyst notes)
- Surfaces what matters to each user's portfolio

**Recommendation Systems**
- Uses a connected graph to surface second- and third-order market effects
- Goes beyond what a standard news feed would show

**Trusted Sources and Signal**
- Users often follow specific industry voices
- Moshi attaches confidence scores
- Treats disagreement between sources (e.g., Reuters vs. WSJ) as a data point

**Build vs. Buy**
- Built a custom vision-language model to parse messy customer PDFs
- Data privacy is the key deciding factor for using third-party tools like Reducto
- Also depends on team's engineering capacity

**UI and Adoption**
- Building a useful tool isn't enough — adoption into user workflow is a separate UI problem

**Team Priorities**
- Current focus: meeting clients before hiring
- Heavy inbound applications; wants better filtering
