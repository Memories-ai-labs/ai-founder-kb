# Cloudflare Joins the Jevolution and Labels Itself a Neolab
# URL: https://latent.space/p/cloudflare/
# Date: 2026-11-17
# Source: Latent Space (swyx & Alessio Fanelli)

## Summary

Cloudflare is building its own AI models and calling itself a "neolab." Michelle Chen (Cloudflare AI Platform) presented at the AI Engineer Code Summit, announcing Cloudflare has launched two System One models:
- **Clef:** A decision model modeled on TypeSafe AI's Jev
- **Clairic:** An "extract" model that pulls typed values from unstructured input in a single forward pass

## Key Points

**Neolab context:** The term was coined by Stephanie Palazzolo of The Information for research-focused AI startups spending heavily on compute with little revenue. Cloudflare doesn't fit that profile ($3B+ annualized revenue, public company) — but they're using the label anyway.

**Natural progression:** Model-building follows Cloudflare's earlier moves into compute, storage, and serving open-weight models.

**Jev as catalyst:** Clef took ~2 weeks to build (Jev took TypeSafe ~2 years). The speed gap illustrates how rapidly model building has become accessible.

**Clairic's purpose:** Addresses LLM hallucination and malformed JSON by extracting typed values in a single forward pass.

**Future direction:** Cloudflare may build reasoning models and other LLMs, but focuses on post-training rather than pretraining from scratch.

**Risk management thesis:** Owning models reduces exposure to closed-source dependencies, regulatory uncertainty, and costly licenses.

## Notable Quotes

- "We don't have to send every request to an LLM."
- "Being a neolab means that we can create what we are missing."
- "Most of our team really just does post-training."
