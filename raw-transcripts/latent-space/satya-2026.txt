## Show Notes

Guest: Satya Nadella, Chairman and CEO of Microsoft
Published: June 3, 2026

Recorded at Microsoft Build 2026. Crossover episode between No Priors and Latent Space.

**AI as Ecosystem Platform**
Nadella emphasized that platforms should create more value outside than what's captured within them. He positioned Microsoft as a "Frontier Intelligence Platform" enabling companies to build their own AI capabilities using multi-model harnesses and enterprise context layers.

**MAI Models & Training Strategy**
Microsoft's approach to model development focuses on clean lineages, private evaluations, and hill-climbing scaffolds. Nadella: "each company will have its own private eval" as a critical competitive advantage.

**Real-World AI Value**
Coding is the most impactful use case, but emerging opportunities in workflow automation through long-running agents and proper UI/UX for agent management.

**The Harness Concept**
Enterprise AI requires coordinated ecosystems combining models, data, tools, and rich context layers. Microsoft's approach emphasizes multi-model flexibility rather than single-model dependency.

**SaaS Transformation**
AI reshapes traditional SaaS business models, unbundling and rebundling applications around new capabilities like Work IQ, which exposes previously untapped enterprise data.

**IP & Evaluation Strategy**
Private evaluations represent a new form of "Token IP" that companies can leverage to maintain control and differentiate themselves across different models.

**Future Workforce Evolution**
Engineering roles will consolidate around agent management, infrastructure, security, and specialized domains, with generalist roles gaining leverage.

**Community Impact & Data Centers**
Massive infrastructure build-out; industry responsibility to ensure communities benefit through job creation, energy efficiency, and broad economic participation.

**Societal Optimism**
Education innovation, healthcare improvements, and democratizing entrepreneurial opportunity through accessible AI tools.
