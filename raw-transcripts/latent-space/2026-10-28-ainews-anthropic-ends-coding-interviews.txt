# Anthropic ends Coding Interviews: "Hunger beats tenure"
# URL: https://latent.space/p/ainews-anthropic-ends-coding-interviews-hunger-beats-tenure/
# Date: 2026-10-28
# Source: Latent Space (AI News)

Felix Rieseberg, a hiring manager at Anthropic, says he has stopped using traditional coding interviews. He argues that Claude now handles such tasks better than candidates, and that his most impactful builders succeed regardless of how they perform on these tests. The headline, "Hunger beats tenure," frames his message for junior engineers.

**His advice for juniors:**

1. Prioritize business impact over working through the backlog.
2. Pursue a bold idea that could substantially advance your organization's goals.
3. Build a working prototype quickly instead of spending weeks on requirements documents and buy-in.
4. Focus on one strong idea rather than cramming several into one prototype.
5. Use your fresh perspective, especially your empathy for users, on user-facing work.
