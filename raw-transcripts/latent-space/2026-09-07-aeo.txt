# The Frontier AEO Tracker: What Astra Chooses (and every other frontier model, and what you can do about it)
# URL: https://www.latent.space/p/aeo
# Date: 2026-09-07
# Source: Latent Space (latent.space)

The Latent Space Frontier AEO (Agent Ecosystem Optimization) Tracker is a research tool examining how frontier AI models make recommendations across 161 product categories.

Methodology: The tracker analyzes "6 prompt variations over 7 models" testing everything from coding tools to database services to angel investors.

Major Findings:

- Models demonstrate "soft biases" favoring their own ecosystem products (Claude Code recommends Claude products, etc.)
- Notable exceptions exist where GPT models recommend Claude products
- 28 categories show unanimous agreement among all surveyed frontier models
- Astra exhibits greater confidence with fewer source citations (median of 5) compared to Sol (9 sources)
- Markdown content-negotiation practices significantly impact model recommendation patterns

Accessibility: The full dataset remains publicly inspectable with "every prompt and answer pair" available for review on the tracker platform.
