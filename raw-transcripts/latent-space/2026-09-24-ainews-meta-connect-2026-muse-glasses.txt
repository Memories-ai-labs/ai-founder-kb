## Overview

Meta's Connect 2026 conference showcased an integrated hardware-and-agent strategy centered on Muse, its personal AI assistant. The company announced voice and video capabilities, new glasses hardware, email integration, and a keychain device called Muse Charm.

## Major Announcements

**Muse Agent Capabilities**

Meta expanded Muse's functionality with voice interaction and real-time video support. The system can maintain extended conversations while managing background tasks. Computer use on Mac was demonstrated, allowing users to queue jobs that Muse processes independently. A new feature enables each Muse instance to have its own email address, which users can carbon-copy on messages or forward items for handling.

**Hardware Releases**

The company unveiled Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 glasses with improved battery life and upgraded microphones. Meta's first VR glasses delivery—distinct from traditional headsets—launched at $1,299, pitched for private cinema, workstation, and gaming use. The glasses received FDA clearance as a hearing aid. Muse Charm, a keychain device enabling interaction without phones or computers, ships in December.

**Partnership Ecosystem**

Over 1,500+ applications integrated with Muse through connectors. Retail partners include Walmart, Best Buy, Gap, Sephora, and Instacart. Productivity integrations feature Box, GitHub, Granola, and Notion. Meta indicated it may eventually capture transaction percentages.

**Muse Realtime Avatar**

Research released a new avatar system animating Muse with sub-second response times and unlimited session length, with watermarked output added without latency penalties. Meta claims superior performance versus Runway Characters and HeyGen LiveAvatar in head-to-head testing.

**Frontier Model**

Alexandr Wang teased an unreleased frontier model as "the most capable model we have ever trained," but provided no release timeline or specifications.

## Reactions and Context

Industry observers praised the VR glasses and agent capabilities. Critics noted the presentation's tone and questioned capability claims. The personal agent market includes competitors like Instinct, xAI's Grok agent, and anticipated offerings from OpenAI and Anthropic.

Meta's acquisition of WaveForms AI, led by Alexis Conneau, surfaced in real-time voice and avatar development. The company leveraged owned hardware and in-house technology as key differentiators.

Anthropic announced Claude's discovery of a previously unknown bacteriophage reverse transcriptase system.
