# Date: 2026-11-20
# Source: Latent Space (AINews)

## IPO facts

- Anthropic priced at $83/share; opened ~$111.50; closed ~$97.17 (up ~17% from IPO price)
- Raised ~$81.3B — reported as the largest IPO in history, ahead of SpaceX's June 2026 listing
- Class F shares carry ~50.1% voting power on most matters; lockup expirations expected to pressure stock over next year
- Google reportedly owns ~13.7% of Anthropic post-IPO
- Anthropic reportedly raised ~$140B total and still holds ~$132B, implying low cash burn (called "biggest surprise" in the filing)

## Dario Amodei's IPO remarks

- Framed the IPO around AI's potential to accelerate science and medicine
- Named risks: misuse, economic disruption, loss of control
- Central commitment: "taking as long as is necessary to have confidence that they are safe" on pacing training/deployment
- Reaffirmed safety-first positioning at Nasdaq opening bell

## Financial analysis

- FT Unhedged: revenue needed to earn 20% return on ~$1T compute commitments would be enormous
- Dustin Moskovitz noted as early major backer, reportedly unhappy with IPO and certain industry figures
- Bulls cite strong growth, Claude's coding leadership, and IPO ad reception
- Critics question company philosophy after going public, valuation, competitive position

## Competitive context during IPO week

- Google's Gemini 4 family (Carbon, Neon, Hydrogen) launched with aggressive pricing near the IPO
- OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra + Sol during IPO week; debated as an attempt to distract from ANT launch
- Qwen3.8-based harness adapters, Tencent Hy4-flash, and other open models released simultaneously
