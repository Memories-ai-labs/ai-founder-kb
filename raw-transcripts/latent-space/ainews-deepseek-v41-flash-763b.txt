## Summary
DeepSeek releases v4.1-Flash, a 763B parameter model using a novel causal Encoder-Decoder architecture with vision capabilities. The model has P8B-D16B parameter configuration (8B parameters active, 16B total in decoder). Authors (Sebastian Raschka et al.) argue this should have been called DeepSeek v5 given the architectural novelty.

## Key Claims
- Novel causal Encoder-Decoder (CED) architecture — departure from standard decoder-only transformers used in GPT/Claude/Llama lineage
- Vision integration at 763B scale marks return of "whale-scale" Chinese open-weight models
- Competitive with frontier Western models on coding and reasoning benchmarks

## Significance
DeepSeek's return to releasing large-scale models after the attention garnered by DeepSeek R1/V3 signals continued investment in compute-heavy pretraining by Chinese labs, despite US chip export restrictions. The novel architecture is notable — if the CED approach proves superior, it could shift the architecture race.
