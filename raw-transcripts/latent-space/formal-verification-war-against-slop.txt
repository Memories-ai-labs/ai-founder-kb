# The War Against Slop and an Emerging Weapon to Fight it: Formal Verification
# URL: https://latent.space/p/formal-verification
# Date: 2026-11-20
# Source: latent-space

Article by Richard MacManus — Latent Space, November 20, 2026

## Summary

Formal verification uses mathematical proofs and logic to check that software or hardware is correct. As AI-generated code grows, it's positioned as a defense against low-quality "slop."

**Key companies:**
- Harmonic — Aristotle product: AI writes proofs that Lean checks
- Theorem — collaborated with Anthropic on "LLM-scaled formal verification"
- Axiom Math — uses verification feedback to train math models
- &Truth — focuses on checking C code for memory safety; builds on Lean

**Core argument:** Co-founder Clark Barrett of &Truth argues that tests and scans find many bugs but can't confirm that none remain: "how do you know when you're done?"

**Lean:** Open-source proof assistant used across most of these efforts.

**Anthropic collaboration with Theorem:** Anthropic's newer model used ~40% fewer tokens to reach a 50% solve rate on 36 small verification tasks.

**Developer workflow:** "Veri-coding" — verification runs alongside AI agents. Developers would spend more time writing and reviewing specifications and less time writing code.

**Context:** Article published same day as news of two independent Riemann Hypothesis proofs (one using Lean/Claude).
