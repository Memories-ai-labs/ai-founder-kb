## AI News Summary

**Main story: Perplexity crosses $1B ARR**

CEO Aravind Srinivas confirmed at a Semafor live event. The Information reported:
- Revenue under $250M at start of 2026
- Over $750M by August 2026
- Now crossing $1B ARR (4x growth in one year)
- Computer product driving some growth

**Valuation estimate:** At multiples similar to other agent companies, Perplexity could be worth $30-50B — up from $20B valuation round in September 2025.

**Recent product launches:**
- **Computer:** Automations for recurring tasks (Sep), Teammates for ongoing jobs (Nov), fully local on supported Macs
- **Monitor API:** Watches a topic/page and sends webhook when something relevant changes
- **Frontier Research:** New research lab with 10,000+ GPU access, planning to publish papers, data, models, and code

## Significance

Perplexity's 4x revenue growth in 2026 demonstrates the AI search/answer engine category can generate massive revenue at scale. The pivot to agentic "Computer" features (automations + teammates) is their bet on the next phase of growth.
