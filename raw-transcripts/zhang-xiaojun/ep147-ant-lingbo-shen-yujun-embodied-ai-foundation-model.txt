## Overview

Host Zhang Xiaojun interviewed Shen Yujun about Ant Lingbo's approach to developing robot foundation models, focusing on the "brain" rather than the physical body. This represents an emerging trend in Chinese robotics companies moving away from hardware-first strategies.

## Guest Background

Shen Yujun (沈宇军) is Chief Scientist at Ant Lingbo (蚂蚁灵波), a subsidiary of Ant Group focused on embodied intelligence. Teacher reference: 汤晓鸥 (Tang Xiaoou), pioneering computer vision researcher.

## Key Topics

**Company Background:** Ant Lingbo is a subsidiary of Ant Group focused on embodied intelligence, having just released their second-generation embodied foundation model.

**Core Discussion Points:**
- 机器人原生基础模型 (Robot-native foundation models) — why the company prioritizes developing robot "brains" over physical bodies
- 大脑和本体的关系 — relationship between robot brains and physical platforms
- 预训练与数据scale up — pre-training strategies and data scaling as the primary bottleneck in embodied AI
- Comparisons between Silicon Valley approaches (Physical Intelligence's zero-shot capabilities) and Ant Lingbo's methodology
- The timing of embodied AI's "GPT-1 moment," predicted for mid-2026

**Technical Focus:** Pre-training strategies, sensor integration, task specification, and data processing methods essential for training universal robot models.
