## Guest Background

阳萌 (Yang Meng) is the founder and CEO of Anker Innovations (安克创新), one of China's largest consumer electronics companies. Born in 1982, he founded Anker in 2011. The company now has a market cap exceeding 60 billion RMB (~$8B). Anker is notable for building a global DTC consumer electronics brand from China, selling charging devices, audio equipment, and smart home products primarily through Amazon.
