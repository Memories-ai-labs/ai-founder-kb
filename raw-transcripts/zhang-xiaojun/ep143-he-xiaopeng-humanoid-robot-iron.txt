## About He Xiaopeng / XPeng

He Xiaopeng (何小鹏) founded XPeng Motors (小鹏汽车, NYSE: XPEV) in 2014. XPeng is a Chinese EV company that has pivoted aggressively toward AI-driven autonomous vehicles and humanoid robotics. The "Iron" humanoid robot was announced in early 2026. XPeng's ADAS system (XNGP) is considered among the most advanced in China.
