## Key Topics

**vLLM Origins and Evolution:**
游凯超 traces vLLM's journey from a Berkeley campus open-source initiative to a thriving community project and ultimately a commercial venture (Inferact). Discusses the significant sacrifices maintainers made to preserve open-source principles while transitioning to business operations.

**Inferact's Funding:**
Inferact raised $150 million seed funding round. This is one of the largest seed rounds in AI infrastructure.

**Co-Design Philosophy:**
The episode title captures a central theme — co-designing AI infrastructure with model architecture rather than treating them as separate concerns. Argues that hardware, inference systems, and model training must be developed together.

**Central Quote:**
"如果vLLM失败，我们会后悔一辈子" (If vLLM fails, we'd regret it forever) — reflects deep commitment to the project's success and the stakes of open-source AI infrastructure.
