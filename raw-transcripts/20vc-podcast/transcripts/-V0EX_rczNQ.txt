of interest around me on a map and seeing restaurants and reviews ratings and that sort of thing coming to believe that we were all going to have devices like this in our pockets and once we did that experiencing a busy coffee shop that was in my building every morning there was this Crush of people trying to get coffee at the same time and so I thought if I could get that coffee faster if it
