# #1 Mistake Early-Stage Startups Need to Avoid -- Ed Baker (Fmr. VP of Growth @ Uber)

one mistake startups sometimes make is trying to focus on growth too early I think you want to find product Market fit before you start to work on growth sometimes startups will say oh we need to hire a growth leader in order to find product Market fit but I actually think they're two different things so to me Product Market fit is just building a product that people love to use I think that's actually the the hardest thing for any entrepreneur to do is to find that product Market fed I think there's often a lot of luck involved with that um an analogy I like to use it's like drilling for oil no matter how how good your drill is if you're Drilling in the wrong place you'll never hit it right so I feel like every entrepreneur that's building their startup they're building their drill and they're drilling somewhere but they might be drilling in the wrong place you don't want to start growing if you're Drilling in the wrong place you want to make sure you've hit that oil first then you add the growth expertise
