# The Five Slide Deck Mistakes Founders Make When Fundraising 🙅

if you want to raise money for your business you will have to put a slide deck together to raise money from investors there are five big mistakes that I always see number one they're too long keep it to ten slides if you want more information on that simply put it in an appendix second the team page have fewer profiles with more context on their experience do not fill it with pictures of your team with no additional information on them it's utterly useless third advisor Pages all investors know that advisors are pretty far removed it's been so overused so don't have your advisor page in one of those core 10 slides then that is your Market slide it's a really important way to show your intelligence show how you think about both the market today be very precise in terms of how you identify your portion of the market and how it changes over time massive mistake that so many Founders make is they say this is a trillion dollar market I promise you pet grooming for Chihuahuas is not a trillion dollar market to be very precise in identifying what what your Market is how it changes over time and then also with ancillary additional products what opens up to you in terms of Market size and then the final one I'll say is exit slide a lot of people have exit slide how you will make money from this business in the end it's traditionally what bluntly older School Angel Investors wanted to see and honestly it's not the type of investor that you want we're coming into this with the vision to build an incredible enduring company that stands the test of time not to get a quick exit so do not have an exit slide I think it really attracts the wrong type of investor those are the five biggest mistakes that the most common used by founders
