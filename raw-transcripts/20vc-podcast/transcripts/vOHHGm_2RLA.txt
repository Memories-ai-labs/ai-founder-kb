# How to Start a VC Fund? 💰🤔

I have raised over 400 million dollars in the last four years with different Venture funds and vehicles that I manage and the biggest mistake that VCS make is they set their original Target Fund size too high they do not get any heat or momentum in the race and then they're not successful here's what they should do instead figure out their minimum viable fund size now this is the minimum size of fund where you can operate and still execute against your strategy so what do you need to do to figure out your minimum viable fund size you work through your portfolio construction so how many Investments do you want to make what check size do you want to write into those companies on average figure this total amount out and then add 20 for your manager fees and this will give you your minimum viable fund size if you're in an early stage do not think you can write less than 25 Investments you need a large enough level of diversification to be successful and so to summarize set the target lower you can always have a higher cap but it's really critical people in those early days that you gain that heat and momentum and LPS in particular feel like there's real progress with your fundraise
