## Show Notes

**AI and mathematics:**
- OpenAI's AI agents resolved nearly 400 long-standing open mathematical problems
- AI can now generate genuinely new knowledge, though not yet sweeping new theories
- Highlights value of insights in solutions; verifiability and data are key enablers

**Oversight and communication:**
- Techniques: debate and iterative amplification
- Goal: keep humans able to check AI work and stay in control

**Biology and drug discovery:**
- Drug discovery is a top priority for Brockman
- Data availability is the key open question
- Bottlenecks: regulatory filings and clinical trials matter as much as molecular design

**Regulation:**
- Regulators may need to update rules once AI's benefits are provable
- Cites self-driving cars as an analogy for regulatory adaptation

**Dots (proactive agent):**
- Always-on proactive agent tracking tasks, calendars, messages, and projects
- Changes AI form factor rather than underlying capability

**Work/consumer convergence:**
- Consumer and enterprise AI assistants will likely converge
- Main difference: cost and compute
- OpenAI aims to unify consumer and enterprise offerings

**UI:**
- Best interface is no interface
- Generative UI creates tailored interfaces on demand

**Cybersecurity — "Defender's Window":**
- Frontier models can help defenders find and fix vulnerabilities before similar capabilities spread widely
- OpenAI's Daybreak program and a billion-dollar commitment to frontline defenders
- Security spending should vastly outstrip software spending as software becomes cheaper

**Human-centric approach:**
- Keeping humans in the driver's seat even as models surpass people in some capabilities
- AI amplifies human agency
