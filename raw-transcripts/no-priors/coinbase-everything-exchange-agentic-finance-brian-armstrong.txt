## Key Topics Discussed

**Agentic Finance & Commerce**
Armstrong emphasized that AI agents need financial infrastructure. He noted that "76% of agentic commerce transactions are under 30 cents," making traditional payment rails economically impractical. Coinbase created tools enabling agents to hold self-custodial wallets and conduct transactions without requiring government identification.

**Everything Exchange Vision**
The company is expanding beyond cryptocurrency to tokenize real-world assets including stocks, bonds, and commodities. Armstrong highlighted that "88% of Coinbase revenue is from non-Bitcoin trading," reflecting diversification beyond digital assets alone.

**AI Integration at Coinbase**
The company developed "Toshi," an internal agent harness that helps engineers build features through recursive self-improvement. The system ingests institutional knowledge and incorporates feedback loops to improve future performance.

**New Limit: Longevity Focus**
Armstrong co-founded New Limit to pursue epigenetic reprogramming—using AI to identify compounds that restore youthful cellular function. The company is targeting liver disease, vascular dysfunction, and immune system aging, with Phase 1 human trials planned for the following year.

**Special Economic Zones**
Armstrong advocated for "freedom cities"—designated areas with reduced regulatory requirements to enable experimentation in nuclear power, drug discovery, and other sectors.
