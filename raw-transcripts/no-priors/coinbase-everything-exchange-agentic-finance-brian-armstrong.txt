## Key Topics

### Agentic Finance & Commerce
Armstrong describes how AI agents need financial infrastructure to operate independently. "76% of agentic commerce transactions are under 30 cents," making traditional payment rails (with 30-cent flat fees) impractical. Coinbase is building tools enabling agents to have self-custodial wallets and independent financial accounts using crypto rails and stablecoins, addressing what he calls the problem of keeping "AIs unbanked."

### The Everything Exchange
Coinbase is expanding beyond crypto trading to include tokenized stocks, stablecoins, prediction markets, and other assets on a single platform. Armstrong highlights that 88% of current revenue derives from non-Bitcoin trading. The company recently launched tokenized stocks outside the US, enabling access for approximately 4 billion people without brokerage accounts.

### AI Integration at Coinbase
Implementing "recursive self-improvement" systems where internal agents make code changes informed by institutional knowledge ("brains"). When humans review and correct agent work, that feedback loops back into the system, continuously improving future performance.

### New Limit: Longevity & Epigenetic Reprogramming
Armstrong co-founded New Limit, focused on cellular rejuvenation through epigenetic reprogramming. Approach targets restoring youthful cell function rather than treating individual diseases. Initial clinical trials for alcoholic liver disease begin next year, with expansion planned for vascular and immune cells.

### Cognitive Enhancement & Human Progress
Armstrong expresses interest in targeted cognitive enhancement, noting that nutritional interventions can improve developmental outcomes. Advocates for embryo editing for disease prevention; discusses broader questions about human biological advancement.

### Special Economic Zones
Discusses "freedom cities" — proposed US federal zones with reduced regulatory requirements — as sandboxes for innovation in nuclear power, drug discovery, and other sectors.
