## Guest
Stefano Ermon — Stanford Professor, Diffusion Pioneer, Co-Founder and CEO of Inception

## Key Content

**Main Topic:** Stefano Ermon discusses why diffusion models represent a superior architecture for AI inference compared to autoregressive models, particularly regarding speed, efficiency, and hardware utilization.

**Core Arguments:**

Ermon contends that diffusion models process tokens in parallel during inference, whereas autoregressive approaches remain inherently sequential. He explains: "autoregressive models are still sequential...one left to right one token at a time...that workload...maps poorly to GPUs."

**Company Status:** Inception, founded by Ermon, operates with approximately 50 employees and serves production customers. The company developed proprietary serving infrastructure since existing frameworks don't support diffusion-based language models.

**Technical Achievements:** In 2024, Ermon's research demonstrated that diffusion models could match autoregressive model quality at GPT-2 scale while generating text "10x faster compared to the auto-aggressive model."

**Customer Application:** OpenCall uses Mercury models (Inception's offering) in voice agent pipelines, achieving production speeds on standard GPUs rather than specialized hardware.

**Future Outlook:** Ermon estimates 20-30% of workloads prioritize latency sufficiently for diffusion-model advantages, emphasizing that "inference...is a key bottleneck for...RL post-training."

**Academic Perspective:** Ermon notes academia enables "contrarian bets" on novel architectures, citing diffusion models, Flash Attention, and DPO as academically-developed innovations.
