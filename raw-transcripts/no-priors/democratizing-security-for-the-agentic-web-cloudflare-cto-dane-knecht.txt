Key themes:

Security urgency — Serious vulnerability reports have risen sharply over the past 3 to 6 months, and major incidents now happen weekly rather than about once a year.

Attacker landscape — Many recent high-profile vulnerabilities were found by individuals, including high schoolers using AI agents, rather than nation-states. Knecht expects security to consume the majority of engineering team time over the next 18 months.

Zero trust vs. castle-and-moat — Companies that moved to zero trust are better positioned. Priorities: reducing attack surface, formal verification, safer languages, adversarial agent and human reviews.

Agentic web — Describes a "messy middle" where humans and agents share the same infrastructure. Expects content delivery and monetization to split for each. Points to micropayments and HTTP 402 as possible economic models.

Cloudflare products — Conference monetization gateway, Signals project with OpenAI for content discovery, and Cloudflare OS (an internal AI platform released as reference architecture).

Internal AI adoption — Cloudflare restructured around AI, rebuilt workflows (e.g., email process that once involved three teams), set token-usage notifications rather than leaderboards, hired ~1,000 interns.

Hiring philosophy — Curiosity, drive, and empathy are key criteria. Personal AI use is a strong hiring signal.

Open vs. closed models — Security capabilities will eventually become broadly available since open source tends to spread; attackers will get access regardless.

Democratizing security — Cloudflare provides major vulnerability protections to free customers as well as paying ones.
