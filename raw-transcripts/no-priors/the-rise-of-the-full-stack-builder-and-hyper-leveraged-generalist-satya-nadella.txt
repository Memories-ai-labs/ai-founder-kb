## Show Notes

Guest: Satya Nadella, Chairman and CEO of Microsoft
Hosts: Sarah Guo, Elad Gil, and swyx (Latent Space)
Published: June 4, 2026

Crossover episode recorded at Microsoft Build 2026. (Also published as Latent Space episode on June 3, 2026.)

**Ecosystem Strategy & Platform Development**
Nadella emphasizes conceptualizing AI as an ecosystem play rather than a single model. Focus on enabling companies to create their own AI, not just consume others'.

**Microsoft's Training Strategy**
Multi-model harnesses with tools access. Emphasis on clean lineage in model pre-training. Private evaluations positioned as a company's most important intellectual property.

**Real-World AI Deployment**
Challenge of converting benchmark outcomes into measurable real-world value. Token efficiency and context preparation identified as critical factors.

**IP Redefinition**
Shifts from human experience to captured traces and evals from agent deployment. Private evaluations become defensible competitive advantage.

**Engineering Role Evolution**
Full-stack builders gaining prominence. Generalist leverage identified as maximizing returns. Infrastructure and distributed systems expertise increasingly vital.

**Data Center Impact**
Microsoft built more Azure capacity in 15 months than in first 15 years. Community benefits and permission essential for continued expansion.

**Future Opportunities**
Education sector ripe for innovation through new pedagogical models. Agent-driven workflow compression creating significant value.
