## Overview

Michael Lee discusses how Sequence Holdings applies frontier engineering to transform legacy businesses rather than replace them. The conversation covers Sequence's $7.7 billion take-private acquisition of Baldwin insurance brokerage alongside the Dell Family Office, the holding company model, and lessons from operating at the intersection of incumbents and AI technology.

## Key Topics Covered

**Business Model & Philosophy**
Lee explains that Sequence operates as a permanent holding company focused on acquiring world-class businesses and applying AI transformation at scale. He argues incumbents possess distinct advantages—brand, scale, regulatory position—that startups cannot replicate.

**Why Holding Company Structure**
The permanent structure aligns capital duration with transformation needs, celebrates engineers rather than investors, and enables long-term compounding without pressures to exit within typical fund timelines.

**BankSouth Investment**
Sequence's first investment demonstrates tangible results: consumer loan underwriting decreased by 94%, loan processing timelines reduced from 30 to 11 days, and loan volumes doubled without additional headcount or compromised underwriting standards.

**Baldwin Insurance Acquisition**
Baldwin represents the ideal candidate—a scaled, centralized operation led by an ambitious management team already investing in technology adoption, within a $2+ trillion industry where incumbents maintain competitive advantages.

**Atlas Platform**
Sequence built a reusable four-layer technology platform including data ontology, agent builder, orchestration engine, and application layer designed to generalize across industries while solving industry-specific workflows.

**Management Team Criteria**
Lee emphasizes selecting leaders who already champion technological change through cloud migration, centralized data infrastructure, and AI adoption—demonstrating commitment before partnership.

**Career Perspective**
Lee's background spanning investment banking, private equity, public market investing, and frontier venture informed his conviction about marrying exceptional execution with long-term ownership and engineering excellence.
