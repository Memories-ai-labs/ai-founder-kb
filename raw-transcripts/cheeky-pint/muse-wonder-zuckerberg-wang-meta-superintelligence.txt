## Show Notes

Meta is scaling its AI infrastructure past five gigawatts. Mark Zuckerberg and Alexandr Wang discuss launching Muse Wonder and the future of personal AI agents. Topics include the creation of Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), a talent reboot, and making a $100B+ capital expenditure commitment before knowing whether the model will work. They also cover Muse Wonder's architecture (active memory, multimodal perception, and frontier coding), agentic commerce, hardware such as smart glasses, and the goal of putting superintelligence in the hands of billions.

## Key Transcript Excerpts

### Muse as a personal agent
Zuckerberg uses Muse to coach his MMA training from gym camera footage, giving feedback between rounds. Wang's Muse regularly buys obscure teas. Zuckerberg also uses it to plan weekend baking with his daughter and to order ingredients.

### Muse Wonder model
Muse Wonder is a larger model than Muse Spark, with a "Full" version for higher-end use. Key strengths: memory, persistence on long tasks, coding, and domain expertise in areas like finance and legal. Training runs through pre-training and post-training (supervised fine-tuning, then reinforcement learning). A small, talent-dense research team and very large compute investments made before the product's success was proven.

### Strategy: own the full stack
Meta wants to own the full stack, including models, devices, and products. Zuckerberg argues that a few companies will build frontier superintelligence and drive the consumer experience.

### Agentic commerce
Muse can shop and delegate purchases, and the guests expect businesses to need agent-compatible interfaces. Stripe's role in Muse's payments discussed; Stripe planning to acquire OpenRouter. Voice interaction is hard to get right because of conversational timing; visual output often more efficient. Meta's smart glasses are a planned route for multimodal agents.

### Funding the AI buildout
Zuckerberg argues Muse can scale through:
1. Subscriptions for premium Muse features
2. Commerce (small cut of savings or earnings)
3. Business-in-a-box product for small businesses
4. Enterprise API platform
Also: AI improving the core ads business already funds spending. On the bet: "The answer better be yes."

### Why no winner-takes-all
Wang cites:
- Uneven execution across labs
- Distillation spreading capabilities to smaller models
- People's desire to avoid single-company dominance
Zuckerberg: diverse ecosystem is safer; Meta focused on personal + professional agents.

### Founding Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL)
Lab began after Llama 4 release (~April 2025). Near-total reboot: talent density, flat structure, entire company pivoting to support it. Set up ~1 year before this conversation.

### Leadership and culture
Wang describes Zuckerberg as very "Bayesian" — quickly updates beliefs, acts on them, willing to make large bets and change course quickly once convinced. Both describe Meta leadership becoming more "AI-pilled." Team is consistently more self-critical than optimistic about progress.
