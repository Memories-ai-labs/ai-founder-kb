## Show Notes / Summary

Storonsky discusses how Revolut has grown to about 50 million customers worldwide and its plans to compete with Amex and Chase in the US. Topics include its "new bets" process (products judged by strict retention curves), its subscription-based business model presented as an alternative to lending, and using AI to cut compliance approval cycles that currently take 210 days. Storonsky argues that CEOs should spend less time at conferences and more time on product development.
