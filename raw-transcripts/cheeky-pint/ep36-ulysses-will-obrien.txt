## Description

Will O'Brien discusses how autonomous underwater robots are scaling to address major ocean challenges. Topics include the history of brewing at Guinness, including the statistics breakthrough behind Student's t-test; seagrass as an underrated carbon sink; engineering robots to operate thousands of meters deep; Ulysses' vertically integrated strategy; balancing environmental goals with practical tradeoffs; and selling hard tech to defense and offshore energy customers.

## Key Themes

- Ulysses makes autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) at ~$50K/unit, dramatically undercutting traditional $500K+ underwater drone costs
- Vertically integrated strategy: builds own hardware, software, and operations
- Customers: defense and offshore energy sectors
- Mission: address ocean challenges at scale (carbon sequestration via seagrass, infrastructure monitoring, scientific research)
