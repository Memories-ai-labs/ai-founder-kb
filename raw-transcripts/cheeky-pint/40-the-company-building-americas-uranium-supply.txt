## Show Notes / Summary

Scott Nolan discusses the engineering, economics, and geopolitics of uranium enrichment. He explains why advanced reactors need High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium (HALEU) to scale, and why the U.S. came to rely on outside fuel suppliers after the Cold War. He describes General Matter's plan to build a domestic enrichment facility in Paducah, Kentucky, to replace Russian imports. The conversation also covers his experience as an early SpaceX employee and how 1970s interest rates stalled nuclear power.
