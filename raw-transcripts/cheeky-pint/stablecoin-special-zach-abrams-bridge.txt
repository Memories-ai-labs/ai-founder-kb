Zach Abrams, the CEO and co-founder of Bridge, the leading stablecoin orchestration platform, and Henri Stern, CEO and co-founder of Privy, the leading crypto wallet infrastructure, sit down with John to discuss the future of stablecoins, issuing, and what it will take for crypto to become ubiquitous. Both companies recently joined Stripe, and are uniquely positioned to dissect how crypto is changing financial infrastructure.
