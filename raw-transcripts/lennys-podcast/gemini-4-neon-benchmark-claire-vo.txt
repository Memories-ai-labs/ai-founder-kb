## Overview

Claire Vo runs her "How I AI" bench — a set of 25 tasks covering coding, writing, creative work, video, prototyping, and personal-assistant work. Six frontier models were scored blind: GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, Claude Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5, and Gemini 4 Neon and Carbon.

**Headline finding:** Gemini 4 Neon ranked first overall — "the dark-horse winner surprised me — and it wasn't close."

## Key points

- **Scoring method:** Blind human scoring combined with LLM-as-judge ratings.
- **Coding:** No longer differentiates frontier models.
- **Favorite model:** Her day-to-day favorite (Opus 5.5) lost the blind test; she still calls it her least annoying daily driver.
- **Claude Fable 5.2:** Strong in some areas; she identifies tasks where she would never use it.
- **Creative and prototyping tests:** SVG generation, animation, animated storybook build, prototypes — all compared side by side.
- **Cost and speed:** A slow model has hidden costs even when its output is strong.
- **Claire Annoyance Bench:** A new eval she built from her own local agent session data to measure how annoying models are to work with.
