## Episode Overview

Adam Mosseri discusses how Instagram is restructuring its product teams, the role of AI in creator tools and platform strategy, and why he views AI-generated content as a tailwind rather than a threat to authenticity.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Product Team Structure Evolution (2026)
- Moving from specialized teams of a "baker's dozen" to lean pods of 4-6 generalists
- Flattening organizational hierarchies to increase agility
- Cross-functional collaboration becoming the default model
- Reducing role specialization to increase speed

### 2. The "Product Staff" Role
- New hybrid position blending PM, design, data science, and research responsibilities
- Generalist operators taking on multiple functional responsibilities
- Represents shift away from strict functional specialization
- Enables fewer headcount per product surface

### 3. Designer Role Future
- Mosseri remains bullish on design careers despite dissolving role boundaries
- Certain specialized roles face greater risk in restructured environments
- Design thinking becoming more valuable across functions, not less

### 4. Instagram Algorithm Insights
- Algorithm capabilities are only now catching up to what users already believed it could do
- Platform understanding user behavior more accurately than previously assumed
- Recommendation quality driving growth more than features

### 5. AI-Generated Content Strategy
- Rise of synthetic content presented as a positive development ("tailwind")
- Company rethinking creator identity in AI-generated content landscape
- Authenticity becoming the central platform differentiator as creation barriers fall
- Key thesis: when anyone can generate content, genuine identity and relationship become the moat

### 6. Product Failures Discussed
- Facebook Home
- Initial Reels version (early iteration problems before the successful launch)

## Guest Background
Mosseri has led Instagram since 2018, surpassing the founders' tenure. Originally a designer who spent 15+ years at Meta, previously led Facebook's News Feed. Under his leadership, Instagram's user base has tripled.
