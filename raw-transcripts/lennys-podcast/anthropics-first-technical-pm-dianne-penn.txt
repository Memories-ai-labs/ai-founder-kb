Dianne Penn joined Anthropic in 2023 as their inaugural technical product manager when the product team consisted of just five engineers. She has contributed to shipping models from Claude 2 through Fable and helped develop Claude Code, MCP, Skills, computer use, tool use, and reasoning capabilities.

Prior to Anthropic, Penn worked on Alexa's AI at Amazon and traded high-yield bonds at JP Morgan Chase.

## Topics Discussed

- Anthropic's foundational period and early product team (5 engineers)
- Key inflection points that transformed the company into history's fastest-growing organization
- Claude's development as a coding tool
- An eval-driven development methodology her team pioneered
- Finding fulfillment amid rapid AI advancement
- Claude's capacity to respectfully challenge users
- Areas where human judgment remains essential
- Token maxing and the jagged edge of AI capabilities
