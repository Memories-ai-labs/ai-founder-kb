## Overview

Mozilla's engineering team used an agentic bug-finding pipeline to identify security vulnerabilities in Firefox. A key insight: "the harness and pipeline did just as much of the work" as the AI model itself.

## Key Topics

- Building a basic bug-finding harness without requiring an SDK
- Using LLM judges to score and rank files before allocating compute resources
- Implementing verifier subagents to eliminate false positives
- The "goal-loop pattern" for giving agents tightly scoped problems with clear pass/fail signals
- Why existing investments in fuzzing, CI, and dev tooling provide competitive advantages
- How humans remain essential in the review process despite AI-generated patches
