Guest: Ankit Maheshwari, CTO of Innovaccer

## Episode Summary

Maheshwari describes the "Jarvis" assistant he runs on a Mac Mini. It began by handling approvals that made him a bottleneck at work and expanded to ordering lunch, calling coworkers who don't respond on Slack, negotiating restaurant reservations, and turning on the AC before he gets home. The episode covers the "soul files" and guardrails that make the system trustworthy, why voice calling is a separate subagent, and whether a local model (GLM) could replace a $500-to-$600-a-month Anthropic bill.

## Key Takeaways

1. The automation that started it all: Jarvis handles approvals to prevent Maheshwari from being a bottleneck
2. Soul files and subagents: distinct AI "personalities" for different tasks (health coach, email triage, master coordinator)
3. Why well-scoped subagents beat one global agent — global agent hallucinated on personas
4. The 1% annoyance rule: automate anything that consistently annoys you, even slightly
5. Guardrails: how to make a personal AI agent trustworthy enough to act autonomously
6. Local model cost tradeoff: GLM Flash vs. $500-600/mo Anthropic bill
7. Voice calling as a separate subagent via Bland AI + ElevenLabs + Twilio
