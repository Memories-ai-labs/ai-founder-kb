## Summary

Serrano built a paid user-generated content (UGC) program for Wispr Flow, an AI voice-to-text tool. He manages 50 creators who produce 500 to 600 paid UGC ads a month. Creators are paid a cut of ad spend, and the top earners make over $13,000 a month. He joined at 19, before the product had product-market fit. Over the past year, Wispr Flow grew revenue 30x and raised at a $2 billion valuation.

## Key topics

- Why paid UGC is an underused growth lever for AI products
