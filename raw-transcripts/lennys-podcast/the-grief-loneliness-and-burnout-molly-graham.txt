## Episode Description

Molly Graham brings 20+ years of leadership experience from Google, Facebook, Quip, and the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative. She hosts TED's WorkLife podcast and runs Glue Club, a leadership community for senior operators.

This episode addresses a wave of grief, loneliness, and burnout sweeping through the tech industry — with a particular focus on how the AI era is reshaping management, delegation, and career development.

## Key Topics Covered

1. **Evolving career advice in the AI era** — Graham revisits her famous "give away your Legos" framework and explains why it requires updating for current times. In the AI era, giving away Legos to machines is fundamentally different from giving them to human teammates.

2. **Industry mental health crisis** — Widespread grief, loneliness, and burnout affecting tech workers at all levels as AI accelerates change and creates uncertainty about roles.

3. **AI delegation differs from human delegation** — Fundamental differences in how delegating tasks to AI compares with delegating to human team members. Key insight: "delegating to a robot is nothing like delegating to a human."

4. **Job displacement narrative** — Examination of fears about AI replacing workers, suggesting these concerns may be exaggerated; the emotional and relational dimensions of work remain human.

5. **Protecting core responsibilities** — Identifying which tasks should never be outsourced to AI systems; maintaining human ownership of strategic, relational, and judgment-heavy work.

6. **Management best practices** — Strategies top managers are currently implementing to maintain team cohesion and individual development in the face of AI-driven change.
