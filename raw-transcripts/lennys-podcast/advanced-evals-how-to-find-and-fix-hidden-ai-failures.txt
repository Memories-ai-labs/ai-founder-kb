## Summary

A paid newsletter post discussing evaluation systems for AI products. The authors argue that most teams skip the critical error discovery phase and jump straight to metrics, which leads to measuring the wrong things.

## Three Steps for Effective Error Discovery

**1. Gather traces**
Collect complete records of user sessions with AI products, logging inputs, outputs, tool calls, and intermediate steps.

**2. Review and annotate**
Use a coding agent to help review diverse samples of data while maintaining human judgment. The authors emphasize that "agents miss issues requiring product judgment" and recommend annotating at least 100 traces.

**3. Identify patterns**
Cluster annotations into failure modes and prioritize based on frequency and impact.

## Key Insights

- The authors present a free plugin (evals-skills) that automates much of the bookkeeping while keeping humans in the loop.
- They illustrate concepts using a leasing assistant example.
- This process typically takes 30 minutes to learn and complete.
- Error discovery resembles product discovery—identifying which problems merit measurement before building dashboards around generic metrics.
