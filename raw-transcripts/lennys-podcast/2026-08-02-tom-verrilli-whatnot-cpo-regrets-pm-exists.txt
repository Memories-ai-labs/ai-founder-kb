## Background

Tom Verrilli is CPO at Whatnot — described as "the fastest-growing U.S. marketplace business in history" with over $8 billion in GMV. Previously CPO at Twitch and director of product growth at Twitter.

## Key Topics

1. **"We regret that product management exists"** — Whatnot's founding philosophy: lean teams with senior individual contributors doing substantive work rather than large PM org structures.

2. **AI's impact on PM role** — How AI is reshaping traditional product management responsibilities and capabilities.

3. **Hiring criteria** — What Tom prioritizes when recruiting PMs: senior ICs over managers.

4. **Senior IC model** — Shift toward fewer, more experienced individual contributors rather than managing large teams.

5. **Data science transformation** — How AI has revolutionized data science operations at Whatnot.

6. **"Play the Accordion" framework** — Tom's mental model for organizational flexibility and decision-making.

7. **Leadership nuance** — Why "hire great people and get out of their way" requires more nuance and active involvement.

8. **Twitter experience** — Key lessons from his tenure during the company's turbulent period.
