Alex Lieberman co-founded Morning Brew and now runs Tenex. Episode explores his AI-powered content workflow built around Claude — addressing how to maintain authentic voice while automating content creation.

## Topics Discussed

- The Blank Page Problem: AI "Oracle" eliminates initial friction by scanning systems and internet for content opportunities
- Six-step workflow from idea identification through publication
- Interview panels that extract genuine ideas before drafting
- Voice Preservation: Markdown files codifying personal style to prevent generic output
- The Interview Step as the critical stage (not drafting) where AI quality issues originate
- Team Leverage: Tenex Creator Cup incentivizes employee content with $5,000 prize pool
- Staff as underutilized marketing channels

## Tools Referenced

Claude, Notion, Linear, Slack, Wispr Flow
