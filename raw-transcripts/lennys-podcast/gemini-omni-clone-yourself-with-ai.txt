## Episode Summary

Claire Vo documents a real-time experiment creating an AI avatar of herself using Google Flow and Gemini Omni video generation model. The entire process — from facial scanning to completing a one-minute promotional video — takes approximately 15 minutes.

## Key Learning Points

1. **Avatar creation via Google Flow** — under 5 minutes from photo capture to usable avatar
2. **AI democratizes video production** — non-professionals can create promotional video content without cameras, crew, or editing skills
3. **AI-generated storyboarding** — Flow generates storyboards from a text prompt describing the video goal
4. **Character consistency across scenes** — Gemini Omni maintains avatar identity across seven distinct generated scenes
5. **"Uncanny valley" moments** — AI struggles with specific emotions and physics, generating occasional artifacts
6. **Browser-based video editing** — stitching generated footage into final video without desktop software
