## Key Frameworks & Insights

### "Proven, Better, New" Framework
- Copy proven solutions first
- Improve them until "10 out of 10 people say yes"
- Only then add innovation
- "Being less ambitious is the path to the most ambitious ideas"

### Instinct vs. Ideas
- "Your instincts are right 95% of the time, but your ideas are wrong 75% of the time"
- Key distinction: trust your intuition about problems and user needs, but be deeply skeptical of your initial solution ideas

### Risk Management
- Core principle: "kill hope before hope kills you"
- Don't let wishful thinking about a struggling product prevent you from cutting losses early

### Product-Market Fit Pattern
At Zynga, 8 of 10 major game launches became massive hits. The pattern: don't swing for novel ideas — start from what's already proven, make it demonstrably better for 10/10 users, then layer in differentiation.

### Raising Children in the AI Era
- Discussion on how to develop instinct and judgment in an environment of AI-augmented decisions
- Emphasis on maintaining independent thinking and avoiding "cognitive surrender"
