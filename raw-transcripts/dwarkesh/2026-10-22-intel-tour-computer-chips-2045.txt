## Episode Summary

Recorded during a two-day tour of an Intel fab, with animated explainers and interviews with Intel leadership about the future of semiconductor manufacturing.

## Key Topics

**Process Development**
- Bringing up a node like 14A takes years and roughly 2,000 manufacturing steps
- Teams track yield with inline metrology and failure cross-sections
- Avoid stacking too much innovation into one node; Intel's 10nm delays showed the cost of that approach
- Now uses maturity gates to decide when a technology is ready

**Transistor Evolution**
- Episode traces planar transistors, FinFETs, and gate-all-around
- Gate-all-around took about a decade to reach production
- Intel's view: the field has moved from "shrink it" to multi-dimensional innovation with many new materials

**High-NA EUV**
- Intel uses High-NA lithography for 14A, partly because backside power relaxes the wiring pitch requirements
- TSMC, which lacks backside power, sees less value in the tool for now

**PDKs (Process Design Kits)**
- Intel is building the formal PDKs that outside customers need
- New for Intel as it becomes a foundry

**Future Architectures**
- Topics include CFET (stacking n- and p-type transistors), 2D materials, and 3D stacking
- Thermal management flagged as a key limit on stacking, especially for logic

**Interconnects**
- Ruthenium may beat copper at very fine pitches (~20nm) because it avoids the liner needed for copper
- Copper remains the best choice for wider lines

**Hybrid Bonding and Packaging**
- Bonding chips directly with copper-to-copper connections is moving into volume production
- Covers wafer-to-wafer versus chip-to-wafer bonding, pitch scaling, and power savings for non-AI uses

**Capacity Limits**
- EUV output is capped below roughly 100 tools per year; lead times are long
- Intel's CTO: industry could roughly double leading-edge wafer output, not 10x it
- This conflicts with AI compute growth of about 3x per year

**Economics and Catching Up**
- Fabs are extremely capital-intensive; scale drives margins
- Intel says it is close to TSMC on technology, but trails on scale and execution

**Terafab Partnership**
- Intel is partnering with Elon Musk's Terafab project, aimed at securing chip supply for his companies

**Closing Insight**
- Intel says it would invest more now if it had known about the demand surge sooner
