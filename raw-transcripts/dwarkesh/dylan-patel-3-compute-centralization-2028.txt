## Key Predictions

Dylan Patel forecasts that Anthropic and OpenAI will control most of the world's usable computing power by 2028. The labs are projected to capture 40-50% of new compute next year, expanding to 70-80% by 2028's end.

## Compute Growth Trajectory

Global AI compute deployment will grow from 30 gigawatts this year to approximately 200 gigawatts by 2028. Both labs combined could reach 100 gigawatts, with each megawatt generating revenue exceeding "$50 million" currently.

## Economic Implications

A small amount of semiconductor fabrication investment generates enormous end-user revenue. "$6 billion of fab CapEx produces a gigawatt every single year" generating approximately "$100 billion of revenue."

This massive return differential creates intense competition for compute resources. Patel suggests compute prices could rise to "$25, $30, $50 million a megawatt" as labs outbid competitors.

## Sovereign Debt Risk

The podcast explores whether "$5 trillion of credit" needed through 2029 could trigger a debt crisis. Rising interest rates from AI infrastructure investment could devastate developing nations and non-AI sectors. Patel warns that if interest rates increase substantially, countries lacking AI exposure face potential default scenarios similar to the 1980s Latin American debt crisis.

## Centralization Concerns

Both speakers agree that "every force is screeching towards centralization" — from training economies of scale to compute scarcity to recursive self-improvement advantages.
