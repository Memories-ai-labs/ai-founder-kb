## Overview

Google released Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, a production-ready video generation model with enhanced creative controls for developers: studio-quality video production including scene extension, first/last frame interpolation, and crisp 4K upscaling.

## Key Features

**Scene Extension:** Analyzes up to 10 seconds of prior context to generate seamless continuations. Videos extendable in 10-second increments up to 40 seconds total.

**Keyframe Specification:** Specify starting and ending frames to generate smooth transitions — supports camera orbits, zoom transitions, seamless looping clips.

**Efficient Drafting:** 360p previews generate up to 60% faster and at 1/3 the cost of 720p. Rapid iteration during development.

**High-Resolution Output:** Upscaling to 1080p or 4K for professional-grade final products.

**Multimodal References:** Reference up to 3 seconds of video alongside images to maintain visual consistency.
