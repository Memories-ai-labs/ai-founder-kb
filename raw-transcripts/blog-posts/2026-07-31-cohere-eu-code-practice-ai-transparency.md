# Cohere Signs EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content

**Date:** July 31, 2026

## Summary

Cohere has signed the EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content, becoming one of the first companies to do so. This voluntary framework helps AI providers demonstrate compliance with Article 50 of the EU AI Act, which mandates transparent marking of AI-generated content to protect Europe's information ecosystem.

## What Was Signed

Cohere signed **Section 1** of the Code, applicable to AI system providers. This commitment follows an earlier signature of the EU AI Act Code of Practice on General Purpose AI Models.

## Key Points

- Demonstrates leadership in transparent AI content practices
- Appropriate marking of AI-generated content
- Europe is a critical market for Cohere (serving enterprise and public sector customers)
- Plans continued collaboration with EU partners:
  - Participation in AI Act Advisory Forum
  - Code of Practice Signatory Taskforce involvement

## Strategic Context

This announcement underscores Cohere's positioning as the "enterprise-safe" AI provider relative to OpenAI and Anthropic. By proactively embracing EU regulation ahead of competitors, Cohere differentiates on compliance and trust — particularly relevant for European enterprise and government customers.
