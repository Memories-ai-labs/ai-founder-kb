# An update on independent evaluators
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/an-update-on-independent-evaluators
# Date: 2026-10-15
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic says it has started giving a team from METR, a nonprofit that evaluates frontier AI models, "employee-like access" to its systems. METR will publish its risk assessments.

The announcement follows Anthropic's commitment to stronger third-party assessment and its signing of the White House Accord on Super Intelligence.

Anthropic works with other evaluators too, including SecureBio (reviewing the chemical and biological weapons chapter of the August Risk Report) and Accenture and Faculty (on a broader partnership). Early talks with Hugging Face are also underway.

**Key framework — what evaluators need:**
- Expertise
- Independence from the company they assess
- Enough access to judge risks
- Freedom to publish

Anthropic compares this to bank examiners working inside large banks.

**METR access terms:**
- Company hardware, office space, and internal tool access
- Anthropic cannot veto publication
- Anthropic can require redaction of narrow categories: customer data, privileged information, and model architectures

**Three access tiers for evaluators:**
1. Narrowly scoped investigations with Anthropic's approval over publication
2. Publication without approval, with some redaction rights
3. Employee-like access (what METR has) — matches tier 2's publication rights plus full access

Anthropic says it is embedding evaluators voluntarily and believes every frontier AI company should do the same.
