# Tiny Aya in the wild: What people are building with open multilingual AI
# URL: https://cohere.com/blog/tiny-aya-in-the-wild
# Date: 2026-07-14
# Source: Cohere Labs Blog

Author: Madeline Smith, Operations and Community Manager, Cohere Labs

Cohere Labs launched Expedition Tiny Aya, a mentor-supported research initiative distributing their lightweight, open-weight multilingual model to builders and researchers worldwide. The program demonstrates how accessible AI models drive innovation across diverse applications.

## Key Findings

### Education & Learning
Teams created math reasoning tools with transparent problem-solving steps and developed child-safe offline voice assistants. They released public datasets including a multilingual Math Olympiad benchmark and a multilingual benchmark for evaluating child-focused AI systems.

### Safety in Multilingual Contexts
Research revealed that "safety behavior shifts dramatically under code-mixed prompts," showing models respond differently when multiple languages blend together. Teams also discovered potential cross-lingual misalignment transfer and identified cultural understanding gaps.

### Accessibility & Deployment
Projects demonstrated that models can function on phones and offline systems through quantization, parameter-efficient vision approaches, and on-device tool use. Teams created synthetic data pipelines for underrepresented languages like Hindi and Turkish.

### Language Understanding
Studies showed strong cross-lingual transfer in word-sense disambiguation tasks and revealed that programming language choices affect model behavior and code generation patterns.

## Impact

The core Tiny Aya research was accepted to COLM 2026. Multiple projects won the Hugging Face Build Small Hackathon. Contributors developed open-source tools, benchmarks, and datasets advancing multilingual AI research globally.
