# Cohere advances C$4 billion commitment to strengthen Canada's AI leadership
# URL: https://cohere.com/blog/cohere-canada-commitment
# Date: 2026-10-30
# Source: Cohere

## Commitment Summary

Multi-year commitment (started 2024) to invest C$4B+ in R&D and AI infrastructure in Canada through 2031.

## Key Details

- **Jobs:** 1,500+ Canada-based roles by 2031; ~1,000 technical; 250+ paid student positions by 2029
- **Infrastructure:** C$309M GPU cloud partnership with Bell AI Fabric — sovereign Canadian infrastructure
- **Research:** National university network: U of Toronto, U of Waterloo, Amii, Vector, Mila
- **Responsible AI:** Multi-year initiative with U of Toronto on responsible AI adoption at scale

## Context

- Canada's national AI strategy targets 3% GDP increase + 250K AI jobs by 2031
- Only 12% of Canadian businesses used AI to produce goods/deliver services in 2025 (Bank of Canada cites adoption gap)
- Cohere's goal: ensure economic value from AI is "built and retained in Canada"

## Signal

National AI sovereignty strategy playing out at the company level — Cohere positioning as Canada's sovereign AI champion. C$4B commitment + Bell infrastructure partnership is a major bet on non-US AI independence.
