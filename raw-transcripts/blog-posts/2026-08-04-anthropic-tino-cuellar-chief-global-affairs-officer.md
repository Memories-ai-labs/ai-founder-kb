# Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar to join Anthropic as Chief Global Affairs Officer

**Title:** Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar to join Anthropic as Chief Global Affairs Officer
**Publisher:** Anthropic
**URL:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/tino-cuellar
**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Source:** Anthropic News

---

Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar has been appointed as Anthropic's inaugural Chief Global Affairs Officer, overseeing policy, international relations, and governmental partnerships globally.

## Background

Cuéllar brings extensive experience across law, technology, international security, and public service. Most recently, he served as President of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, a prominent independent policy research organization with international presence. His judicial background includes service as a California Supreme Court Justice, where he addressed technology, privacy, and constitutional matters.

Previously, he directed Stanford's Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies and co-directed its Center for International Security and Cooperation. He also founded Stanford's Cyber Initiative and served on the President's Intelligence Advisory Board and State Department's Foreign Affairs Policy Board across three administrations.

Additional leadership roles include co-chairing a bipartisan Nuclear Proliferation task force and leading California's Frontier AI Working Group. Currently, he holds the Cameron Schrier Family Professorship at Stanford Law School and serves as Senior Fellow at Stanford's Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence.

## Transition

Cuéllar was previously a Trustee of Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust (since January 2026) and has stepped down from that position to assume his new role.

## Leadership Perspective

According to Cuéllar, "there is no more consequential place to be shaping that work right now than Anthropic," emphasizing AI governance's importance for democratic societies and global advancement.

Daniela Amodei praised his qualifications, noting his "sound judgment and civic-mindedness" across government, law, and academia.

## Significance

This hire signals Anthropic's escalating focus on government relations and policy as AI regulation accelerates globally. Creating a dedicated CGAO role (rare among AI labs) suggests Anthropic is building a permanent institutional interface with governments — not just lobbying, but shaping international AI governance frameworks.
