# Measurements for understanding the pace of AI development inside frontier labs
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/institute/measuring-pace-of-ai-development
# Date: 2026-09-17
# Source: Anthropic Research Blog

Anthropic proposes three new measurement frameworks for tracking AI development progress, framed as transparency mechanisms that frontier labs should publish regularly.

## 1. AI-Led R&D Automation (Automation Index)

Anthropic developed an "Automation Index" rating how much of its research and development is performed by AI itself, on a scale from AL0 (no AI involvement) to AL5 (fully autonomous). As of August 2026, Claude "leads" 26% of Anthropic's AI R&D work, with over 90% operating at collaboration level or above. Claude is not operating fully autonomously for any measured subset.

## 2. AI Agent Oversight

With approximately 30,000 agents conducting research at Anthropic simultaneously, Anthropic implemented monitoring systems tracking coverage, review latency, and escalation rates:
- Online monitors block risky actions in real-time with 100% coverage
- Offline monitors review transcripts post-facto, flagging roughly 1-2 per thousand for human review

## 3. Compute Allocation Tracking

During a measured week, approximately 6% of AI R&D compute went toward safety research. The article acknowledges this is a conservative estimate, as safety work often requires less compute-intensive experimentation than capability development.

## Key Perspective

Anthropic frames these measurements as transparency mechanisms, proposing that frontier labs should regularly publish comparable metrics to help the public understand AI development pace. The company acknowledges the current lack of transparency regarding what happens inside AI labs.
