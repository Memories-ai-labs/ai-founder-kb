# Introducing Gemini 3.7 Flash
# URL: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/
# Date: 2026-08-13
# Source: Google Blog

## Overview

Google announced Gemini 3.7 Flash, described as "our most intelligent workhorse model yet for coding and agents." Released just three weeks after Gemini 3.6, this model represents algorithmic improvements informed by developer feedback.

## Key Performance Improvements

Substantial gains in software engineering tasks:
- **FrontierCode**: 43.6% accuracy vs. 34.4% for 3.6 Flash (production code quality)
- **DeepSWE benchmark**: 65.3% vs. 49.0%
- **WebDev Arena Elo**: 1588 vs. 1538 (web development)
- **GDP.pdf document comprehension**: 34.0% vs. 22.0% (finance/law knowledge domains)
- **AutomationBench**: 30.4% vs. 17.0% (business workflow automation)

## Pricing & Availability

Introductory price through December 31, 2026:
- **Input**: $0.75 per million tokens (half the cost of 3.6 Flash)
- **Output**: $3.75 per million tokens

Starting January 2027: $1.50 / $7.50 per million tokens.

Available via Google AI Studio, Android Studio, Gemini Enterprise Platform, and Spark for Google AI Pro/Ultra subscribers in 160+ countries.

## Significance

Three-week release cadence signals Google's shift to continuous algorithmic improvement on the Flash tier rather than waiting for major model generations. The 14-point DeepSWE improvement in three weeks represents significant engineering velocity in software agent benchmarks.
