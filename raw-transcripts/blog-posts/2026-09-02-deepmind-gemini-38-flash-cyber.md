# Introducing Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.8 Flash Cyber
# URL: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/
# Date: 2026-09-02
# Source: Google DeepMind Blog

## Overview

Google announced two new Gemini models designed for advanced AI applications. "Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.8 Flash Cyber deliver next-generation intelligence for agentic workflows and cybersecurity."

## Gemini 3.8 Flash

The general-purpose model represents Google's latest reasoning and coding advancement. It maintains the speed and affordability of its predecessor at "$0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens."

Key capabilities include:
- Superior performance on complex software engineering tasks
- Enhanced multi-step reasoning for specialized domains
- Autonomous agent deployment for enterprise applications

The model demonstrates particular strength in long-horizon coding challenges, outperforming larger competing models while costing significantly less.

## Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber

This specialized cybersecurity variant prioritizes vulnerability detection and automated code patching. Access is restricted through Google's Fairwind Program, limited to "trusted defenders" including government authorities and critical infrastructure operators.

Notable achievements include:
- Exceeding 70% success rate on real-world vulnerability discovery across 20 programming languages
- 2.6x more correct patches than competing commercial models in Chrome security testing
- Discovery of critical vulnerabilities in under 2 hours versus typical months-long timelines

## Availability

Both models are accessible through Google AI Studio, Google Cloud, and consumer applications like Gemini app and Google Sheets.
