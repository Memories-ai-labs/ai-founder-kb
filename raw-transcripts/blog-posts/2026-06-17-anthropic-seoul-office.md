# Anthropic Opens Seoul Office and Announces New Partnerships Across the Korean AI Ecosystem
# Source: Anthropic News
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/seoul-office-partnerships-korean-ai-ecosystem
# Date: 2026-06-17
# Publisher: Anthropic

---

Anthropic has established a Seoul office and announced partnerships across Korea's AI sector. KiYoung Choi, the Representative Director for Korea, stated: "What I see in Korea are teams who understand that innovation and safety are two sides of the same coin."

## Government Partnership

The company signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT focused on safe AI adoption, including safety evaluations in Korean and cyber threat analysis.

## Enterprise Adoption

Major Korean companies are deploying Claude extensively:
- NAVER deployed Claude Code across its engineering organization, benefiting thousands of developers
- Nexon uses the tool for game development
- LG CNS, Hanwha Solutions, and Samsung SDS are rolling out Claude to thousands of employees
- Channel Corp integrates Claude into its customer service platform serving over 230,000 companies

## Research and Nonprofit Work

Anthropic will collaborate with the National AI Research Lab, providing Claude access to 60 researchers across major Korean universities. Good Neighbors Korea, a children's rights NGO, is deploying Claude to reduce administrative burden and improve service delivery.

## Developer Community

Korea ranks among the top dozen countries for Claude.ai usage. The company launched Claude for Startups in Korea and co-hosted Claude Build Day with BASS Ventures, attracting over 100 founders and developers.
