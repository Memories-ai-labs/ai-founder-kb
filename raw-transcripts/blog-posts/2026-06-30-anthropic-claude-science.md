## Overview

Claude Science is a research application designed for life sciences professionals, available in beta for macOS and Linux. The app integrates data analysis, database access, and scientific visualization tools into a single environment. It was announced June 30, 2026 alongside Claude Sonnet 5 and the Fable 5 export control lift.

## Key Features

**Reproducibility:** Analyses include the exact code, environment, and conversation that produced them, enabling reproduction, editing, or defense months later.

**Scientific Visualization:** Built-in renderers for proteins, molecular structures, genomic data, and chemical compounds without additional installation.

**Background Reviewer:** Flags incorrect citations, untraceable numbers, and figures that don't match underlying code.

**Computational Flexibility:** Manages computing resources across personal laptops, Linux systems, and HPC clusters. Users can write batch scripts and submit jobs via SSH or Modal, with Python and R kernels maintaining persistent state.

**Domain Coverage:** Pre-configured for genomics, single-cell RNA analysis, structural biology, cheminformatics, and proteomics. Connects to 60+ scientific databases.
