# Introducing the Anthropic Cyber Mission
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission
# Date: 2026-10-08
# Source: Anthropic News

---

Anthropic launched the Cyber Mission, a long-term effort to help defenders secure the software and systems society relies on.

## Critical Infrastructure Defense Program (CIDP)

Provides operational technology (OT) security providers — covering power grid, water, and transportation controls — with:
- Access to frontier Claude models
- On-site engineers
- Threat research

Founding partners: Accenture, Booz Allen, CrowdStrike, Deloitte, Dragos, Hitachi, Nozomi Networks, Palo Alto Networks, PwC, and Rockwell Automation.

## OSS Scanner

Opt-in, free, periodic security scans of open-source projects using Anthropic's strongest models. Reports include:
- A proof of concept for each finding
- A suggested fix where possible
- Sent without human review; Anthropic expects some inaccuracies and a true-positive rate above 90%

## Context

- Vulnerabilities are easier to find than to verify and fix
- Fixing operational technology can be slow because it often cannot be taken offline
- Anthropic's June program for state, local, tribal, and territorial governments now reaches more than half of US states
- Anthropic's forecast: AI will eventually favor defense, but the near term may be harder
- Plans: expand the program, share findings (including failures), and collaborate with other developers, security companies, and governments
