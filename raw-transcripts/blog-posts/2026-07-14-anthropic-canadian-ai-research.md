# Anthropic commits $10 million to Canadian AI research
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/canadian-ai-research
# Date: 2026-07-14
# Source: Anthropic

Anthropic announced a $10 million CAD investment in Canadian AI research institutions, recognizing Canada's foundational contributions to modern artificial intelligence development.

## Key Partnership Announcements

Anthropic established partnerships with eight institutions:

- **Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute (Amii)** in Edmonton — Claude credits for reinforcement learning and AI safety research
- **Mila** in Montreal — support for responsible AI, health, sustainability, and robotics research
- **Vector Institute** in Toronto — advancing trust, safety, health, and science applications
- **CHEO and CHEO Research Institute** — developing AI approaches for children's health outcomes
- **Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH)** — computational mental health research and multilingual education
- **Université Laval** — studying LLM behavior across cultural contexts and low-resource languages
- **University of Saskatchewan** — biomedical, food security, and quantum computing research
- **University of Toronto Data Sciences Institute** — supporting research projects through peer review

## Startups Program Expansion

Anthropic is adding Amii, Mila, and Vector to its Startups program, providing hundreds of affiliated Canadian startups with minimum $5,000 USD in API credits each.

## Canada Market Context

According to the Anthropic Economic Index, Canada ranks eighth globally in Claude.ai usage. Per capita, Canadians use Claude over four times the rate predicted by population alone — second only to the United States among top-10 countries. Canada's AI community (Hinton at Toronto, Bengio at Mila, Sutton at Alberta/Amii) gave rise to the deep learning revolution that underlies modern LLMs.
