## What is EFS?

Anthropic announced Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) on September 1, 2026—a solution combining zero data retention privacy with misuse detection. The system stores data in customer-controlled cloud infrastructure rather than Anthropic's servers.

## Core Features

- Data storage in customer environments (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure)
- Customer-managed encryption keys
- Automated safety monitoring without Anthropic human review
- Pattern-based detection across sessions and accounts
- No charges from Anthropic; cloud providers bill for storage

## Design Process

Anthropic developed EFS collaboratively with over 100 enterprise customers across finance, healthcare, manufacturing, telecom, legal, retail, and government sectors. The initiative involved input from major banks' security chiefs through the Analysis and Resilience Center for Systemic Risk (ARC).

## Customer Benefits

One executive noted: "Enterprise Frontier Safeguards gives us exactly what we asked for: our logs stay in a Wells-managed environment under Wells-managed keys."

## Rollout Timeline

Phased release beginning fall 2026, with eligible customers receiving zero data retention on Claude Fable 5 and 5.1 until EFS becomes available.

## Supported Platforms

Claude Code, Claude Enterprise, Claude Platform, Amazon Bedrock, and integrations with major cloud providers.
