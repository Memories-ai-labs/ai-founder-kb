## Cohere Expands Its Sovereign AI Platform with AISLE and cubic

Published: November 13, 2026

### Summary

Cohere announced completion of its acquisition of AISLE, a company building autonomous systems for vulnerability discovery and remediation, and welcomed the team behind cubic, an AI-powered code review platform. These deals expand Cohere's sovereign AI platform into cybersecurity and software development.

### Key Points

**Cybersecurity (AISLE acquisition):**
- Ondrej Vlcek → SVP of new Cohere Cyber business
- Jaya Baloo → Chief Information Security Officer
- Stanislav Fort → VP of AI Security
- Cohere plans long-term investment in Prague as a global center for AI cybersecurity

**Secure coding (cubic):**
- Team led by CEO Paul Sanglé-Ferrière
- Will establish a secure coding vertical
- Tools designed for on-premises and air-gapped deployments

**Broader strategy:** Organizations want AI they can deploy on their own terms, with control over their data, infrastructure, and operations.

### Significance

Cohere is making a significant bet on AI-native cybersecurity, combining vulnerability discovery automation (AISLE) with secure coding tools (cubic). This positions Cohere's sovereign AI platform as an end-to-end offering for security-conscious enterprises and governments — going beyond language model APIs into critical security infrastructure. The Prague investment signals a European hub strategy.
