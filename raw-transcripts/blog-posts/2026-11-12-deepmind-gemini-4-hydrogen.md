## Introducing Gemini 4 Hydrogen

Published: November 12, 2026

### Summary

Google introduces Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a faster model aimed at everyday coding and agentic workflows, positioned below the frontier Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon models.

### Key Points

**Positioning:** Below frontier Gemini 4 Carbon/Argon (deep, long-horizon reasoning). Hydrogen targets everyday coding and agentic workflows with speed.

**Performance:** Scores slightly above Gemini 4 Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default settings.

**Pricing:** $1.50 per million input tokens, $7.50 per million output tokens.

**Availability:** Google AI Studio, Gemini API, Gemini for business, Gemini app, Google Search, and Google Antigravity (paid subscribers).

**New feature — Orchestrator:** Antigravity feature that automatically switches between Carbon and Hydrogen within a session based on task complexity.

**Improvements over Gemini 3.8 Flash:** Speed, coding performance, and safety behavior.

### Significance

The Gemini 4 Hydrogen launch fills out Google's model lineup with a fast, cost-efficient option for agentic workflows. The Orchestrator feature in Antigravity — automatically routing between frontier and fast models — is a notable UX innovation for AI assistants. The pricing ($1.50/$7.50 per million tokens) positions it competitively against other fast models.
