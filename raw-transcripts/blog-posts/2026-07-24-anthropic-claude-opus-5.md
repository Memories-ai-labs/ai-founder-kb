# Introducing Claude Opus 5
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5
# Date: 2026-07-24
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic announced Claude Opus 5 on July 24, 2026, described as "a thoughtful and proactive model that comes close to the frontier intelligence of Claude Fable 5 at half the price." Available immediately across all platforms.

## Key Performance Highlights

- On Frontier-Bench v0.1: surpasses all other models, more than doubles Opus 4.8's performance at lower cost per task
- On ARC-AGI 3 (novel problem-solving): score is three times as high as the next-best model
- On OSWorld 2.0 (computer use): outperforms every other model at any given cost
- Life sciences improvement: 10.2 percentage points higher than Opus 4.8 on organic chemistry benchmarks
- Remains behind Mythos 5 on cybersecurity tasks

## Working Capabilities (Agentic Behavior)

Notable examples of autonomous problem-solving:
- Wrote custom computer vision code to solve tasks without direct access to visual data
- Identified and fixed root causes in open-source bugs without being prompted
- Built complete market data feeds independently

## Safety and Alignment

- Scored lowest on automated behavioral audits for misaligned behavior among recent models
- Adheres to Claude's Constitution better than Opus 4.8, Sonnet 5, or Fable 5
- Does not advance risky dual-use capabilities; remains substantially behind Mythos 5 in vulnerability exploitation

## Pricing and Access

- $5 per million input tokens, $25 per million output tokens (same as Opus 4.8)
- Available via Claude API and all platforms
- 2.5x faster mode available at double the base price
- HN reception: 1,680 points; #1 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Leaderboard within hours of release
