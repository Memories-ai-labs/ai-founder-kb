OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra on September 3, 2026, initially to Daybreak cybersecurity program users, with broad API and paid plan access the following week.

## Key Capabilities

- Advanced computer and browser automation capabilities
- "Best model for software engineering to date" — superior at finding bugs, executing terminal tasks, and answering codebase queries
- Can identify and develop zero-day exploits to help security teams patch vulnerabilities
- Outperforms prior OpenAI models (Sol) and Anthropic's Fable on agentic tasks

## Controversy: Opaque Recurrence

The model employs "opaque recurrence," a reasoning technique that obscures chain-of-thought monitoring — a critical research process for auditing AI decision-making. OpenAI's chief scientist acknowledged this creates monitoring challenges as models become more capable, noting that advanced models can "perform harder tasks using fewer language tokens" or none at all, limiting oversight ability.

## AGI Claim

Greg Brockman (OpenAI president) stated: "For me personally, I do think we're there" regarding AGI achievement. He also noted the AGI contractual obligation with Microsoft no longer exists.

Official description: "Most intelligent and, also very importantly, our most aligned model yet." — Greg Brockman
