# Anthropic launches roadshow for proposed initial public offering
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch
# Date: 2026-11-10
# Source: Anthropic

## Summary

Anthropic, PBC has launched the roadshow for a proposed IPO of its Class A common stock.

## Key Details

- **Offering size:** 825,000,000 shares. Anthropic offering 801,351,354; selling stockholders 23,648,646.
- **Price range:** $70.00–$78.00 per share (implied valuation ~$1.4 trillion).
- **Exchange:** Applied to list on Nasdaq Global Select Market under ticker "ANT."
- **Underwriter option:** 30-day option for underwriters to buy up to 123,750,000 additional shares.
- **Underwriters:** Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC.
- **Status:** Registration statement filed with SEC but not yet effective.

This marks a major inflection point for the AI industry — Anthropic going public at ~$1.4T valuation validates the AI lab business model.
