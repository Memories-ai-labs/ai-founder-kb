# 2026 Usage Policy Update
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update
# Date: 2026-10-08
# Source: Anthropic News

Effective date: November 12, 2026

---

Anthropic published a new Usage Policy version. The changes mostly clarify existing rules and add examples for Claude's longer, more autonomous work.

## Key changes

**New deceptive activity section:** Rules against fake account networks, fabricated news sites, and influence operations were consolidated into one section covering political and commercial deception.

**Elections section refocused:** Now centers on preventing voter deception and election disruption. The blanket ban on personalized vote and campaign targeting was removed; deceptive or privacy-violating targeting remains prohibited.

**Weapons:** Prohibitions now explicitly cover weapons software and components, and arming drones or autonomous vehicles.

**Surveillance and law enforcement:** Covert tracking of people is prohibited; Claude cannot be used to decide who to investigate, arrest, or charge. Consensual tracking, content moderation, journalism, and legal research remain permitted.

**High-risk uses:** Requirements for a qualified human in the loop and notice to affected individuals were clarified; new requirements cover Claude connected to hardware that takes autonomous physical actions.

**Abusive behavior toward models:** A prohibition covers sustained, purposeless cruelty toward Claude — aimed at extreme cases. Ordinary frustration, dark creative themes, and model testing are excluded.

**Supported Regions:** Enforcement details for the policy on companies majority-owned by entities in unsupported regions were clarified.
