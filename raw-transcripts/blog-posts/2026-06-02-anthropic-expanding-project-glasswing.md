## What is Project Glasswing?

Project Glasswing is Anthropic's collaborative initiative to enhance global software security. The program provides advanced AI capabilities to organizations whose codebases, if compromised, could have catastrophic consequences affecting critical infrastructure and potentially hundreds of millions of people.

## Initial Results

Starting with approximately 50 partners in early April 2026, organizations deployed Claude Mythos Preview to scan codebases for vulnerabilities. According to Anthropic's announcement, "These partners have identified more than 10,000 high- or critical-severity security flaws" through their collaborative efforts.

## Expansion Details

The program is now extending to roughly 150 additional organizations across more than 15 countries. New partner categories include critical infrastructure sectors previously underrepresented:
- Power
- Water
- Healthcare
- Communications
- Hardware

Many new participants are vendors whose software reaches numerous organizations globally, including governmental entities.

**Selection criteria:** Organizations where successful cyberattacks could affect over 100 million people and carry significant national security implications.

## Strategic Purpose

Anthropic emphasizes two primary objectives:
1. Helping the software industry safely access advanced models and tools to adapt to the emerging threat landscape
2. Gradually shifting focus from merely identifying vulnerabilities to disclosing, fixing, and deploying patched software at scale

The announcement reflects Anthropic's expectation that within 6-12 months, competitors will develop comparable AI models, potentially without equivalent safety measures, necessitating accelerated industry adaptation.

## Model Used

Claude Mythos Preview — Anthropic's advanced vulnerability-scanning model deployed for this security research initiative.
