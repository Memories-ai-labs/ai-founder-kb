# The state of sovereign AI adoption in 2026
# URL: https://cohere.com/blog/state-of-sovereign-ai-adoption-2026
# Date: 2026-08-25
# Source: Cohere Blog

Author: Cohere Team (IDC InfoBrief commissioned by Cohere)

Survey of 500+ senior decision-makers across Canada, US, UK, and Germany in regulated industries on sovereign AI adoption.

## Key Findings

**Definition gap:** One in three leaders had difficulty describing sovereign AI in their own words.

**Sovereign AI defined:** The ability for organizations to maintain control over AI system design, deployment, and governance across their chosen infrastructure.

**Industry concerns:** Data leakage and compliance identified as paramount:
- 70% in energy
- 82% in financial services

**Adoption blockers:**
1. Defining operational needs
2. Bridging awareness gaps (only 13% report being "very widely" aware)
3. Developing holistic strategy

**Emerging motivation:** Competitive advantage gaining traction alongside risk management — telecoms leading at 37%, manufacturing at 32%.

**IDC projection:** By 2028, CIOs at multinational organizations will boost investments in modular, sovereign-ready cloud and data localization environments by 65%.

## Relevance for AI Founders

Enterprise AI founders selling into regulated industries (finance, energy, healthcare) face a market where 1/3 of buyers can't articulate what they want. Framing around data sovereignty and control rather than model capabilities may improve sales conversations. The 65% projected increase in sovereign AI investment is a large TAM signal.
