# Anthropic files S-1 with the SEC for proposed initial public offering
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec
# Date: 2026-10-26
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic, PBC filed a registration statement on Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on October 26, 2026, for a proposed initial public offering of its Class A common stock.

## Key Details

- **Exchange and ticker:** Anthropic plans to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT"
- **Offering terms:** The number of shares and the price range have not yet been determined
- **Underwriters:** Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC are named lead underwriters
- **Status:** The registration statement has been filed but is not yet effective; no securities may be sold until the registration statement becomes effective

## Context

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers Dario Amodei (CEO) and Daniela Amodei (President), along with other co-founders. The company is known for its Claude family of AI models and has pursued a public benefit corporation structure, reflecting its safety-focused mission.

The IPO marks a major milestone for the AI lab, which has raised significant venture capital from investors including Google, Amazon, and Spark Capital. The S-1 filing initiates the formal public disclosure process under U.S. securities law.
