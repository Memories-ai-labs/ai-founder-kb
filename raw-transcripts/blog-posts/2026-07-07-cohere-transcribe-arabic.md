# Meet Cohere Transcribe Arabic: The world's most accurate open-source model for Arabic speech recognition

**Publisher:** Cohere
**Date:** 2026-07-07
**URL:** https://cohere.com/blog/transcribe-arabic
**Author:** Cohere Team

---

Cohere released an open-source Arabic speech recognition model achieving the lowest average word error rate (WER) of any open-source model: 25.87, surpassing competitors like Meta's OmniASR and OpenAI's Whisper.

## What it solves

Addresses unique challenges in Arabic language processing:
- Dialect variation across 30+ regional varieties
- Code-switching between Arabic and English
- Domain-specific vocabulary common in enterprise settings

## Performance

- Preferred over Whisper in 95.8% of human evaluation tests
- Preserves dialectal authenticity rather than standardizing toward Modern Standard Arabic
- ~525 RTFx throughput vs. 146 for Whisper Large V3

## Availability

- Apache 2.0 license; weights on Hugging Face
- API access and managed deployment via Cohere's platform
