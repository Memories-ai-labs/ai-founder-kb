Mistral announced three major initiatives at the AI Now Summit 2026.

**Mistral for Industrial Engineering**

The company unveiled an integrated AI stack designed for mission-critical industrial operations. This solution combines advanced physics models, engineering expertise, and robotics to help industrial engineers accelerate design work and optimize asset performance while maintaining control over proprietary data.

Key partnerships include Airbus, where AI will support "initial design to on-board capabilities" across commercial aircraft and defense activities. BMW Group selected Mistral as a partner for their "Large Industry Model" initiative focused on engineering data and complex development scenarios like crash simulation. ASML is collaborating on engineering optimization tasks including "design of high-performance parts, surrogate models and control loops."

Mistral acquired Emmi on May 22 to enhance physics AI capabilities for aerospace, automotive, and semiconductor manufacturers.

**Vibe: Unified Agent for Productivity**

Vibe now handles long-running, multi-step work including "inbox and calendar management, deep research, drafts, and recurring processes." The agent also handles coding work—building features, fixing bugs, and creating pull requests across web apps, editors, and terminals.

**Les Ulis Data Center**

A new 10 MW facility in Essonne, France will open in Q3 2026, dedicated to inference operations and addressing "compute supply chain risks by providing direct control."
