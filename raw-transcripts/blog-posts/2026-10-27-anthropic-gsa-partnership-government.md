# Expanding our partnership with GSA to bring Claude to all levels of government
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-partnership-gsa
# Date: 2026-10-27
# Source: Anthropic News

**Category:** Partnerships

---

Anthropic and the General Services Administration (GSA) signed a new agreement expanding Claude's availability across U.S. government.

## Key Details

- **Discount:** 40% off Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government, and the Claude API for government partners
- **Scope:** Covers federal, state, local, tribal, and territorial governments; applicable through cloud providers or direct purchase
- **Estimated savings:** $1 billion to the government; no minimum spend commitment
- **Adoption:** ~500 US federal, state, and local organizations now use Claude; weekly Claude for Government active users up ~8x since February 2026
- **Security:** Claude for Government has FedRAMP High authorization (GA since September 30); Claude Enterprise pursuing FedRAMP Moderate

## Use Cases

- Utah pilot with Claude Code across 10 state agencies
- Faster ransomware response for a state CIO
- Legacy code documentation and modernization projects

## Availability

Available through:
- Amazon Bedrock
- Google Cloud's Vertex AI
- GSA's Multiple Award Schedule (OneGov discount)

Anthropic is also launching a support program to help agencies implement Claude.
