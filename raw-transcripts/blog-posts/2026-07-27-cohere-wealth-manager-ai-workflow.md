# A Day in the Life of a Wealth Manager, With and Without AI

**Published:** July 27, 2026
**Author:** Yael Malamatinas, Staff Product Marketing Manager

## Overview

Contrasts traditional wealth management workflows with AI-enhanced processes using Cohere's North AI platform.

## Key Points

**Without AI:** Wealth managers juggle disconnected tools including CRM systems, spreadsheets, email, PDFs, and compliance platforms, creating significant productivity losses.

**With AI (North platform):** Three primary agents:

1. **Client Intelligence Agent** — Provides analytics-grounded portfolio views and risk assessments
2. **Research Synthesis Agent** — Aggregates market data from multiple sources into investment theses
3. **Communications Agent** — Generates compliant client-facing documents

## Additional AI Agents

- **Regulatory Compliance Agent** — Handles MiFID II and Dodd-Frank reporting with trade surveillance support
- **Operations Intelligence Agent** — Streamlines settlement reconciliation and exception handling

## Business Impact

"AI isn't a shortcut. It's how business gets ahead." Improved client connections, more complete risk views, stronger research-backed recommendations, and enhanced regulatory confidence.

## Implementation Guidance

Cohere recommends piloting the Regulatory Compliance Agent first, then scaling after establishing data access patterns and review workflows.
