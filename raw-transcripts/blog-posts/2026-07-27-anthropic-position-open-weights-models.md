# Anthropic's Position on Open-Weights Models
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models
# Date: 2026-07-27
# source: Anthropic News
# Author: Dario Amodei

## Summary

Anthropic explicitly denies advocating for bans on open-weights models, despite accusations to the contrary. The post distinguishes between what Anthropic opposes (categorical bans) and what it supports (targeted security measures).

## Key Arguments

**Primary Concern:**
Authoritarian governments developing more powerful AI than the US, enabling military superiority or domestic repression. "My primary concern is the risk that authoritarian governments...build AI models that are more powerful."

**Secondary Concern:**
Powerful AI models enabling cyberattacks, biological attacks, or misuse due to alignment problems. Open-weights models present higher risk because "guardrails" become difficult to apply and weights cannot be withdrawn once released.

## Supported Policy Measures

1. **Chip Restrictions:** Prevent advanced semiconductor sales to China and crack down on smuggling operations. This directly addresses capability development since "China has limited domestic production capacity."

2. **Distillation Controls:** Target industrial-scale distillation operations that allow China to build better models than chip availability would normally permit.

3. **Safety Testing:** Implement mandatory pre-release testing for all sufficiently capable models — both open and closed — examining cyber, biological, and alignment risks.

## Position on the Open Letter

Amodei agrees open-weights models expand access and enable competition but disputes claims they automatically enhance safety. He expresses concern about asymmetric risks, particularly regarding biological threats where "sufficiently capable models may be able to quickly weaponize pandemic-level viruses."

## Context

This post was published in response to an open letter signed by AI researchers and entrepreneurs advocating for fewer restrictions on open-weight AI models. TechCrunch covered Amodei's response under "Anthropic's Dario Amodei responds to concerns about open-weight models."
