# The Total Cost of AI Ownership
# URL: https://cohere.com/blog/the-total-cost-of-ai-ownership
# Date: 2026-07-15
# Source: Cohere Blog

Author: Ariana Milligan, Product Marketing Manager

This article examines the comprehensive expenses enterprises face when deploying artificial intelligence systems at scale. While token pricing represents the most visible cost, the true expense encompasses infrastructure, operations, security, and scaling considerations.

## Key Cost Components

Tokens are the fragments of text that a model both reads and generates, occurring across prompts, responses, document retrieval, agent steps, and retries. However, token price alone misrepresents actual expenses.

Research highlights widespread cost challenges: 96% of organizations deploying generative AI experienced higher-than-expected expenses, while 80% of companies missed infrastructure forecasts by over 25%. AI maturity results in more inference, longer context windows, more agentic steps, increased retrieval, and demand for speed.

## Ownership vs. Renting Strategy

The analysis compares capital expenditure (owned assets like servers and chips) against operating expenditure (rented resources like cloud instances). Utilization emerges as the critical factor determining cost-effectiveness.

Concrete figures from multiple sources demonstrate significant savings through ownership:
- Owned inference: ~$0.11 per million tokens over five years (Lenovo analysis)
- Cloud instances: ~$0.89 per million tokens
- Frontier APIs: ~$2.00 per million tokens

## Efficiency Strategies

Model efficiency techniques including mixture-of-experts routing and low-bit quantization reduce costs without sacrificing capability. Raw hardware price alone is a poor guide to cost — throughput and utilization determine actual economics.

## Strategic Implications

Successful AI scaling involves determining which capabilities to own versus rent, maintaining visibility into cost drivers, and building predictable expense models rather than accepting volatile usage-based billing.
