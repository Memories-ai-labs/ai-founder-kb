# Introducing Mistral Large 4
# URL: https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/
# Date: 2026-10-06
# Source: Mistral AI News

Mistral has launched a public preview of **Mistral Large 4** (informally called "le Chonk"), a 1 trillion-parameter multimodal model with 49 billion active parameters. The company describes it as their largest and most capable model to date.

## Performance Highlights

- **Cybersecurity:** Ranks among the top five globally on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index, with an 82% score on vulnerability reproduction tests
- **Coding:** Scores 61.7% on DeepSWE v1.1 and ranks second in human evaluations (3.74/5 scale)
- **Agentic workflows:** Achieves 59.9% on AutomationBench business automation tasks
- **Multimodal capabilities:** Surpasses GPT-6-Astra on visual grounding benchmarks (42% vs 41% on Dense 200)
- **Scientific tasks:** State-of-the-art on SciCode-Verified among open-weight models

## Infrastructure & Sovereignty

The model was trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's European datacenters and will be deployed regionally, including an EU-operated endpoint. Training data spans over 160 languages, including all official EU languages.

## Availability

A public preview API is available now on Mistral Studio, with weights releasing by month's end.

**Pricing:** $1.36 per million input tokens, $4.18 per million output tokens.
