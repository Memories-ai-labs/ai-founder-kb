# Granite Switch 4.1: Chat, Decisions, and Speech from One Endpoint

*Published October 15, 2026 on Hugging Face Blog (IBM Granite)*

## Summary

IBM releases Granite Switch 4.1, a unified model endpoint that handles conversational AI, decision-making tasks, and speech processing through a single API.

## Key Features

- **Unified endpoint**: Chat, decisions, and speech from one model access point
- Simplifies enterprise AI deployment by reducing model management overhead
- Part of IBM's Granite model family targeting enterprise production deployments

## Significance

- Trend toward unified multi-modal/multi-task models reducing operational complexity
- IBM's continued open-source AI push via Hugging Face
- Decision/routing capability integrated with speech adds ambient computing use cases
