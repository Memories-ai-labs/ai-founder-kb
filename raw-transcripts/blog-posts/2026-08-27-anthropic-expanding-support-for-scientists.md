# Expanding Our Support for Scientists
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-support-for-scientists
# Date: 2026-08-27
# Source: Anthropic News

## Overview

Anthropic is significantly expanding its support for the scientific research community through new subscription plans and enhanced funding opportunities.

## Key Announcements

**Claude Team Plan for Scientists**
The company is opening 10,000 seats for researchers worldwide to access Claude subscriptions at reduced costs for one year. Standard seats will be free and premium seats with 5x usage limits will be available for $15 per month. Anthropic plans to expand this program beyond the initial allocation.

**Expanded AI for Science Program**
Beyond biology, which received prior focus, the company now aims to support researchers across multiple scientific disciplines. This includes projects involving complex computational work, such as investigations into mathematical problems and protein design.

## Access Requirements and Details

To qualify for the team plan, applicants must be a principal investigator or equivalent at an academic or nonprofit research institution. Individual researchers can apply for up to $50,000 in credits per project through the broader AI for Science program.

## Safety Considerations

The company maintains restrictions on model access for certain applications. Biology and chemistry researchers currently have limited access to Opus-class models, while Claude's Fable models continue to block professional biology and drug development queries due to dual-use concerns.
