# Anthropic is Donating Another $20 Million to Public First Action
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/donation-public-first-action
# Date: 2026-07-21
# Source: Anthropic News

## Overview
Anthropic announced an additional $20 million contribution to Public First Action, a nonpartisan organization focused on AI education and policy. Total support now reaches $40 million.

## Key Points

**Purpose:** Supports Public First Action's "public education and policy mission" — funds cannot be used to influence elections. First $20M (February 2026) promoted AI safeguards, American AI leadership, and developer transparency.

**Why now:** Anthropic cited recent AI capability advances. Their Claude Mythos Preview model "discovered thousands of high-severity software vulnerabilities" across major operating systems and browsers earlier this year. Released through Project Glasswing to help cybersecurity experts address weaknesses before exploitation.

**Policy recommendations Anthropic advocates for:**
- Government verification of safety claims
- Civil penalty enforcement mechanisms
- Authority to slow or block risky AI deployments
- Mandatory model testing and independent evaluation
- Stronger export controls on advanced semiconductors

**Core message:** Governments must act quickly to build capacity for managing AI risks while capturing benefits. American technological leadership remains advantageous but fragile in global AI competition.

## Context for AI Founders
This announcement comes one day after court approval of Anthropic's landmark $1.5B copyright settlement — Anthropic's most significant legal liability resolution. The $20M policy donation signals Anthropic positioning as a safety-forward, policy-engaged company at a time when Chinese open-weight models are dominating developer mindshare. The mention of Claude Mythos discovering thousands of software vulnerabilities is notable — first public reference to this model capability.
