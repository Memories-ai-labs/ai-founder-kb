## Welcoming Jaya Baloo as Cohere's First CISO

Published: November 12, 2026

### Summary

Cohere announced that Jaya Baloo, co-founder of AI-native cybersecurity company AISLE, is joining as its first Chief Information Security Officer.

### Key Points

- **Role:** First CISO in Cohere's history
- **Background:** Former CISO at Rapid7, Avast, and KPN Telecom; World Economic Forum advisor; Europol EC3 advisor
- **AISLE work:** Building autonomous systems for finding and fixing previously unknown software vulnerabilities
- **Framing:** Trust is central to enterprise and government AI adoption; she will shape Cohere's security strategy for its sovereign AI platform
- **Quote:** Security and trust "need to be built in from the start, not added later"

### Significance

Hiring a prominent CISO from both enterprise security (Rapid7, KPN) and AI-native security (AISLE) signals Cohere's intent to make security a first-class product differentiator, particularly for government and regulated industry customers. This is part of the broader AISLE acquisition announced the following day.
