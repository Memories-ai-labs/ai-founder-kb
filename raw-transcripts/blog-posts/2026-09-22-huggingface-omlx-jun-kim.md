# Jun Kim, oMLX creator and maintainer, joins Hugging Face to support the MLX community
# URL: https://huggingface.co/blog/omlx
# Date: 2026-09-22
# Source: Hugging Face Blog
# Authors: Pedro Cuenca, Lysandre, Victor Mustar, Julien Chaumond, Jun Kim

## Summary

Hugging Face announced that Jun Kim, creator of oMLX, has joined as a team member to support the MLX community on Apple Silicon. The move transitions oMLX from a side project to a fully-funded initiative.

## Key Points

- oMLX maintains its Apache 2.0 license with Kim continuing as lead
- Hugging Face aims to streamline converting transformer model definitions into MLX implementations
- Plans for closer collaboration with mlx-lm, mlx-vlm, and LMStudio
- Goal: advance open, local AI accessibility for developers building on Apple platforms
- Faster development pace and better contributor guidance now possible with full-time focus

## Significance

Signals Hugging Face's continued investment in local/on-device inference infrastructure, particularly for the Apple Silicon ecosystem. Community-built tooling (oMLX) getting institutional backing is a recurring pattern in the open-source AI tooling space.
