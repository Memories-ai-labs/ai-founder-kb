# Welcome Beam: a 501B open model you can own, customize, and scale

*Published October 16, 2026 on Hugging Face Blog*

## Summary

Introduction of Reflection Beam 1.0, a 501B total parameter (23B active) Mixture of Experts open-weight model under Apache 2.0 license. Includes a safety report and Mirror CLI harness for deployment.

## Key Details

- **Architecture**: MoE (Mixture of Experts) — 501B total, 23B active parameters
- **License**: Apache 2.0 (fully open, commercial use allowed without special agreements)
- **Benchmark score**: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score: 30
- **Context**: Positioned as an open, ownable alternative to proprietary frontier models
- **Limitations noted**: High hallucination rate on AA-Omniscience benchmark

## Tooling

- Mirror CLI harness included for deployment
- Safety report published alongside the model weights

## Significance

- One of the largest Apache 2.0 licensed models released
- Contrasts with Meta Muse Spark Open's more restrictive license
- Part of trend toward truly open frontier-class models in late 2026
