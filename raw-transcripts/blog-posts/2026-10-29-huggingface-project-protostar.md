# Announcing Project ProtoStar: Making Publicly Funded Science AI-Ready
# URL: https://huggingface.co/blog/announcing-project-protostar
# Date: 2026-10-29
# Source: Hugging Face Blog

Authors: Avijit Ghosh (evijit), Sayeed Choudhury (sayeedmd), Greg Tananbaum (gtananbaum), Emma Scharfmann, Georgia Channing, Eunice Mercado-Lara, Irene Solaiman — plus Hugging Face Policy Team, CMU-OSPO, and ORCA-Open.

**Purpose:** ProtoStar is an initiative to make publicly funded research datasets, models, and code easier to discover, reuse, and build on, especially for AI-driven research. Encourages sharing in AI-ready, machine-actionable formats.

**Core problem:** "Making research publicly available does not automatically make it usable" — resources are often scattered, poorly documented, or in legacy formats.

**Partners:** Carnegie Mellon, Purdue, Georgia Tech, Arizona State, and George Washington University (via Open Forum for AI), Astera Institute, Hugging Face (infrastructure), and ORCA (community). Organizations join through a memorandum of understanding.

**Growth figures:** Hugging Face hosts over 17,000 public datasets in core scientific domains. Researchers shared more than 3x as many new science datasets in the first 9 months of 2026 vs. same period of 2025.

**First challenge:** Hosted by Carnegie Mellon from November 2-13, 2026 — the Climate Risk Intelligence Challenge: forecast urban climate indicators for 210 U.S. cities. Teams also propose a short climate adaptation policy idea.

**Contact:** protostar@orcaopen.org
