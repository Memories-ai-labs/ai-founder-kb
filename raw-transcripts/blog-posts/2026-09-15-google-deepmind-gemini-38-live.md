# Introducing Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking
# URL: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-live-gemini-3-8-live-extended-thinking/
# Date: 2026-09-15 (updated 2026-09-17)
# Source: Google DeepMind Blog

Google has released two advanced voice-based AI models designed to make conversational interactions with AI feel more natural and intuitive. Both models process visual information in near real-time, support 97 languages with automatic detection, and can execute background tasks without interrupting dialogue.

## Gemini 3.8 Live

A cost-efficient model optimized for scale, combining conversational ability with visual grounding and fluid dialogue capabilities. Designed for high-throughput deployments where cost-per-session matters.

## Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking

Built for complex tasks requiring deeper reasoning, this variant maintains uninterrupted conversation while handling multi-step workflows. Achieved the top spot on Artificial Analysis' Speech to Speech Quality Index in independent benchmarks.

## Key Details

- Both models are available immediately via Gemini API and Google AI Studio
- Enterprise users get private preview access through Gemini Enterprise and Google Workspace
- All generated audio includes SynthID watermarking for transparency
- Near real-time visual processing enables grounded multimodal conversation

## Significance

Gemini 3.8 Live represents Google's push to make real-time voice AI competitive at scale. The Extended Thinking variant is a direct bid for the enterprise agentic workflow market where voice + reasoning + background task execution is increasingly a differentiator.
