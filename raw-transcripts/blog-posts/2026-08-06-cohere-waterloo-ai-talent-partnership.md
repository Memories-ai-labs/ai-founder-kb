# Cohere and University of Waterloo Launch AI Talent Partnership

**Title:** Cohere and the University of Waterloo launch partnership to strengthen Canada's AI talent pipeline
**Publisher:** Cohere
**URL:** https://cohere.com/blog/cohere-university-of-waterloo-announcement
**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Source:** Cohere Blog

---

Cohere and the University of Waterloo have announced a collaboration to develop Canada's AI workforce, creating "a new AI Transformation and Change Management certificate led by Waterloo's Future of Work Institute."

## Program Details

Launching in Fall 2026, this non-credit certificate combines theoretical knowledge with practical experience. Students engage in hands-on co-op opportunities working directly with organizations to identify where AI can solve real business problems.

The curriculum integrates AI literacy, human-centered design, ethics, business strategy, and change management. Rather than jumping to technology solutions, the program teaches students to "examine the people, processes and operational challenges behind a problem before determining where AI can help."

## Impact

Creates a "living lab for AI transformation" where students gain workplace experience while partners learn how to make organizational AI adoption "more effective, responsible and useful."

Cohere positions this as addressing a critical enterprise gap: as organizations move beyond experimentation to widespread AI implementation, professionals who bridge technology and business strategy are essential.
