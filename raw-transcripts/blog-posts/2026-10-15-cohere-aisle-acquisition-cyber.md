# Cohere to acquire AISLE to advance cyber-resilient sovereign AI
# URL: https://cohere.com/blog/cohere-and-aisle-sign-letter-of-intent-to-advance-cyber-resilient-sovereign-ai
# Date: 2026-10-15
# Source: Cohere Blog

---

Cohere announced a proposed acquisition of AISLE, a cybersecurity AI company founded 2024. The deal is not yet final as of publication date.

## What AISLE Does

AISLE builds AI systems to find, validate, and help fix previously unknown vulnerabilities in production software. In its first year out of stealth, it reported:
- 500+ CVE-assigned vulnerabilities across 100+ codebases
- All 12 flaws in OpenSSL's January security release
- 18 of 24 CVEs in curl's latest release

**Founders joining Cohere:**
- Ondrej Vlcek → SVP, Cohere Cyber
- Jaya Baloo → CISO
- Stanislav Fort → VP, AI Security

## What This Means for Cohere

**Cohere Cyber:** New cybersecurity business unit launching as part of this deal — targets enterprises and governments securing AI systems.

**Full-stack sovereign AI:** Cohere combines models + agentic applications + secure deployment + security tooling into one sovereign platform. Third acquisition after Reliant AI and Aleph Alpha.

**Prague hub:** Cohere announces major long-term investment in Prague as global AI cybersecurity hub.

**Strategic rationale:**
- Deployment in private cloud, on-prem, and fully air-gapped environments
- AISLE security research sits closer to frontier model training — verifiable security problems may improve model evaluation
- Agent governance: monitoring and governance for AI agents and autonomous workflows
- Continuous, scalable vulnerability discovery and remediation
