## Overview
General availability announcement for Gemini 4 Carbon (introduced Oct 28, 2026). Now accessible to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers. Positioned as Google's strongest model for hard reasoning tasks; Argon remains better for most software engineering and knowledge work.

## Key Capabilities

**Knowledge work**: Strong at dense document/chart/table analysis for legal, finance, operations. Ranks #2 on Multimodal Knowledge Agent benchmark at 6X lower cost than #1.

**Agentic coding**: Ranks #1 on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity WANDR, and Zapier AutomationBench at 2X lower cost than next-best external model. New `/swarm` feature in Google Antigravity coordinates teams of agents.

**Cybersecurity**: Available to vetted defenders through Fairwind Program. Leads SEC-bench Pro, ExploitGym, and CWE-bench v1.

**Scientific reasoning**: Used for formal verification, lab data analysis, protein homolog discovery. ~40x increase in retrieved homologs across 7 low-homolog protein families.

**Safety metrics**: 
- Per-attempt breach rate: 0.8% (down from 3.1% on Argon)
- Agentic honesty benchmark: 92% pass rate (up from 70%)
- Prompt injection robustness: noted as area for improvement

## Pricing
- Introductory: $2/M input, $10/M output tokens (matches Argon)
- After November 30, 2026: $4/M input, $20/M output

## Availability
- API: Google AI Studio, Gemini for Business (paying developers/enterprises)
- Consumer: Google AI Ultra subscribers via Antigravity, Gemini app, AI Mode in Search
