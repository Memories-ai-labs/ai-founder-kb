# Anthropic raises $65B in Series H funding at $965B post-money valuation
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h
# Date: 2026-05-28
# source: Anthropic News

Anthropic has raised $65 billion in Series H funding led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital, valuing the company at $965 billion post-money.

Global enterprises across industries are deploying Claude in their core operations, and a growing number of people around the world use it for their everyday work. Since the Series G in February, adoption has continued to grow across global enterprise customers, and the run-rate revenue crossed $47 billion earlier this month. This latest funding is expected to advance safety and interpretability research, expand compute to meet growing demand for Claude, and scale the products and partnerships customers rely on.

Krishna Rao, Chief Financial Officer of Anthropic, stated: "Claude is increasingly indispensable to our growing global community," adding that funding will help serve historic demand and keep the company at the research frontier.

The round was co-led by Capital Group, Coatue, D1 Capital Partners, GIC, ICONIQ, and XN. Significant investors include AMP PBC, Baillie Gifford, Blackstone, Brookfield, D.E. Shaw Ventures, DST Global, Fidelity, General Catalyst, Insight Partners, Jane Street, Lightspeed Venture Partners, MGX, NTTVC, NX1 Capital, Situational Awareness LP, T. Rowe Price entities, and Temasek. The round includes $15 billion of previously committed investments from hyperscalers, including $5 billion from Amazon.

Strategic infrastructure partners Micron, Samsung, and SK hynix joined the funding round, recognizing their critical role in supplying memory, storage, and logic chips.

Anthropic has significantly expanded compute capacity with agreements with Amazon for five gigawatts, Google and Broadcom for five gigawatts of next-generation TPU capacity, and SpaceX for GPU capacity access. Claude remains available on Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure.

## Key Stats
- Series H size: $65B
- Post-money valuation: $965B
- Run-rate revenue (as of May 2026): $47B ARR
- Compute deals: Amazon (5GW), Google/Broadcom (5GW TPU), SpaceX (GPU)
- Lead investors: Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia Capital
