# Investing in multi-agent AI safety research

**Published:** June 11, 2026
**Source:** https://deepmind.google/blog/investing-in-multi-agent-ai-safety-research/
**Partners:** Google DeepMind, Schmidt Sciences, the Cooperative AI Foundation, ARIA, Google.org

## Overview

Google DeepMind and partners announced a $10 million funding initiative for multi-agent AI safety research. The initiative addresses the emerging challenge that "millions of AI agents — built by different organizations — will interact across digital environments, communicating, negotiating and transacting with one another."

## Four Research Priorities

1. **Sandboxes and testbeds** — Creating evaluation environments including "virtual marketplaces, simulated ecosystems and multi-organisation workflows"

2. **Agent network science** — Studying how collective capabilities emerge and detecting dangerous population-level properties

3. **Agent infrastructure** — Stress-testing protocols for identity, reputation and cross-platform interactions

4. **Oversight and control** — Developing monitoring methods for deployed agent populations

## Key Insight

Interacting autonomous agents can produce complex "emergent" behaviors that are difficult to anticipate — a fundamentally different safety challenge from single-agent systems.
