# Mistral raises €3B to make sovereign, open-weight AI the technology frontier
# URL: https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-makes-sovereign-open-weight-ai-to-frontier/
# Date: 2026-09-08
# Source: Mistral AI News

Mistral announced a €3 billion Series D funding round at a valuation exceeding €21 billion, representing "the largest equity fundraising round ever completed by a European technology company" just three years after launch.

Samsung Electronics spearheaded the investment, alongside co-leads Scaleup Europe Fund (managed by EQT) and PSG Equity. The capital will strengthen frontier research capabilities and expand computing infrastructure for model training while boosting commercial expansion across 20 countries.

The company now supports over 125 global enterprises including Airbus, ASML, and HSBC. Mistral emphasizes addressing a shifting industry question: how organizations can leverage AI "without surrendering control over the infrastructure and intelligence loop."

The firm positions itself as the sole AI company building "the full stack required" to deliver performance alongside sovereignty—encompassing open-weight models, proprietary infrastructure, computing capacity, and production systems. This approach enables organizations to maintain data control, customize models, ensure private compute resources, and retain auditable production systems.

The syndicate includes new investors Advent, BlackRock-managed funds, and Luxembourg's government, alongside existing backers including a16z, ASML, Bpifrance, and Salesforce Ventures.
