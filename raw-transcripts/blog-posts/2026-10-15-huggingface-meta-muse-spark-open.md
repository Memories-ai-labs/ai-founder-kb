# Welcome Muse Spark Open: open-weight frontier multimodal mixture-of-experts model from Meta

*Published October 15, 2026 on Hugging Face Blog*

## Summary

Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B total parameter (42B active) multimodal MoE open-weight model, topping Artificial Analysis's US open-weight Intelligence Index with a score of 39.

## Key Details

- **Architecture**: Mixture of Experts — 816B total, 42B active parameters
- **Context window**: 1M tokens
- **Modalities**: Text, image, video, and audio input
- **License**: Attribution required; large model-as-a-service businesses must sign a separate agreement
- **Benchmark**: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score: 39 (top US open-weight model)
- **Limitation**: Ranks last among top 9 open models on AA's agentic evaluations

## License Controversy

The licensing terms drew community criticism — the requirement for MaaS businesses to sign a separate commercial agreement makes it less freely usable than Apache 2.0 models like Reflection Beam.

## Significance

- Largest multimodal open-weight model from Meta to date
- Reinforces Meta's strategy of open-weight releases to drive ecosystem adoption
- Sets a new bar for US open-weight model capability on general benchmarks, though agentic performance lags
