Cohere and OpenText announced a strategic collaboration designed to assist governments and regulated industries in transitioning agentic AI from pilot stages to full production deployment.

## Partnership Details

The collaboration combines OpenText's enterprise data management capabilities with Cohere's North platform, described as a "secure agentic AI platform." Together, these companies aim to equip AI agents with the necessary context and intelligence to "reason over enterprise data and act across systems while maintaining control over security, deployment, and data location."

## Key Integration

A significant component integrates Cohere's North platform and models directly into OpenText Aviator AI agents. This enables organizations to deploy across various environments — on-premises or within private, public, or sovereign cloud infrastructures.

## Significance for AI Founders

Cohere's rapid partnership cadence (Aleph Alpha merger + OpenText + Schwarz Group STACKIT all announced within 2026-09-16) signals a deliberate platform strategy: wrap sovereign AI capabilities around legacy enterprise data platforms to enable regulated industries to run agentic AI without sending data to US hyperscalers. AI founders targeting government/regulated verticals should study this playbook.
