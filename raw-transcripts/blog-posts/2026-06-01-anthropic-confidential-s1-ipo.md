Title: Anthropic confidentially submits draft S-1 to the SEC

Anthropic, PBC submitted a draft S-1 registration statement to the SEC for a potential initial public offering of common stock. The company stated this submission "gives us the option to go public after the SEC completes its review."

The filing is conditional on market conditions and other factors. Neither the share count nor pricing have been determined yet.

Per Securities Act Rule 135, this announcement is not an offer to sell securities or solicitation to buy them. Any actual securities transactions will follow standard registration requirements.

**Context:**
- Announced alongside recent $65 billion Series H funding round at $965 billion post-money valuation
- Annualized revenue run rate reportedly hit $47 billion in May 2026
- Claude Opus 4.8 recently introduced with enhanced coding and agentic capabilities
- Anthropic surpassed OpenAI to become the most valuable private AI startup following Series H
- IPO would be a landmark event for the AI industry — at near-$1T valuation, would enter top tier of S&P 500
