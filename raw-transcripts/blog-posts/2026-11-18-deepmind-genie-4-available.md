# Genie 4 is now available
# URL: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/
# Date: 2026-11-18
# Source: Google DeepMind Blog

**Authors:** Diego Rivas (Group Product Manager, Google DeepMind), Pavan Kumar (Group Product Manager, Google Labs)

## Summary

Google is rolling out Project Genie with the Genie 4 world model to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers, enabling interactive AI-generated world simulation with real-time editing.

## Key Features

**Genie 4 (new):**
- Edit worlds mid-session with text prompts (change weather, add characters, redirect action)
- Real-time native audio
- Available to Google AI Ultra subscribers

**Genie 3 (upgraded):**
- Sessions up to 3 minutes per generation
- Linked worlds
- 360-degree panorama seeding
- Real-time in-world events

**New Project Genie Agent:**
- Builds worlds from natural-language conversation
- Can bring Gemini into a session to offer suggestions or trigger events

**Community Gallery:**
- Explore curated and community-made worlds
- Remix and publish worlds
- Example worlds: "Living Memory," "Dino Field Guide," "The Life of Ants"

**Safety:** All generated video and audio includes SynthID watermarking; inputs and outputs go through safety screening.

**Access:** Google AI Ultra subscribers; Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist.
