Anthropic launched a new connector for Claude that provides direct access to the Anthropic Economic Index, enabling users to explore how AI is being utilized across the economy.

## Key Points

The tool allows people to ask Claude questions about AI adoption, such as: "Which occupations use AI the most?" and "What are the most common ways people in Colorado use Claude?"

Getting started: Users can access the connector through claude.ai's menu, enable it, and begin querying the Index without installation.

The announcement notes that "the Index reflects patterns in Claude usage rather than the labor market as a whole," and Claude will reference source data and acknowledge limitations during exploration.

## Availability

The connector is available immediately in claude.ai across all Claude models, with complete datasets remaining freely accessible on Anthropic's website for researchers, journalists, and policymakers seeking to understand AI's economic impact.
