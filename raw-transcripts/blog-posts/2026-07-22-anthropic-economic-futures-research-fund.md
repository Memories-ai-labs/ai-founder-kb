Anthropic announced a $200 million Economic Futures Research Fund to support external research on preparing society for AI's economic impacts. The initiative focuses on studying interventions that enhance economic flexibility, ensure equitable AI benefits distribution, and mitigate disruption-related harms.

## Five Research Priorities

1. **Workplace AI Integration** — Field experiments examining collaborative patterns, organizational design choices, and worker participation in AI deployment decisions at firm levels.

2. **Transition Support** — Evaluations of skill retraining, job placement, apprenticeship models, and educational curricula designed for an AI-transformed labor market.

3. **Income Support Modernization** — Testing unemployment insurance reforms, basic needs relief, and longer-duration income pilots addressing persistent AI-driven displacement.

4. **Worker Wealth Building** — Pilots testing pre-distributive capital accounts, equity-sharing mechanisms, and revenue-generation approaches like compute or automation taxes.

5. **Public Investment Evidence** — Large-scale pilots funding human-facing services, AI-enabled public services access, guaranteed employment programs, and place-based community interventions.

## Funding Details

The fund primarily supports projects between $5–30 million from accredited universities, research institutes, and nonprofits with field experiment experience. Proposals must emphasize ambitious scope and early public knowledge-sharing.
