# Hallo, Deutschland! — Mistral Opens Munich Hub for Industrial AI
# URL: https://mistral.ai/news/hallo-deutschland/
# Date: 2026-09-28
# Source: Mistral AI News

Mistral has established a new hub in Munich to advance industrial AI applications in Germany's leading industrial economy. The announcement emphasizes that the company is positioning itself as a "long-term technological partner" rather than merely a software vendor.

## Key Strategic Focus Areas

The Munich facility will house specialized research teams concentrating on Physics AI and Industrial AI, supported by applied engineers serving enterprise clients directly.

## Sovereignty and Infrastructure

Mistral commits to building one gigawatt of European compute capacity by 2030. The company highlights its open-weight model architecture, allowing customers to run AI systems on their own infrastructure using European infrastructure and law compliance, ensuring "the intelligence stays where it was built."

## Physics AI Development

Following the May 2026 acquisition of Emmi AI, Mistral now employs over 30 physicists and engineers specializing in computational fluid dynamics and multi-physics simulations. The company is collaborating with BMW on crash simulations and with Siemens Energy on industrial applications.

## Academic Partnerships

A research partnership with Technical University Munich focuses on developing digital twins for automotive aerodynamics, combining real-time experimental data with computational simulations.

## Significance

This move reinforces Mistral's European sovereignty strategy — competing with US hyperscalers on the basis of open-weight models, European data residency, and industrial domain expertise. Germany's industrial economy (BMW, Siemens, etc.) represents a large and underserved enterprise AI market.
