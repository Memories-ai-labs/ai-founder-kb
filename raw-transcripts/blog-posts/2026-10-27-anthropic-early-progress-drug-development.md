# Early progress on accelerating drug development with Claude
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development
# Date: 2026-10-27
# Source: Anthropic Research Blog

**Team:** Anthropic Life Sciences

---

Anthropic's life sciences team reports early results from deep research partnerships with six drug developers: BigHat Biosciences, Bristol Myers Squibb, Divergence Labs, Enveda, Generate Biomedicines, and The Column Group.

## Key Partnership Results

**Generate Biomedicines:** Scientists used Claude Code to design a shorter version of a CAR T-cell therapy component. In lab tests, one design performed better than the standard version.

**Divergence Labs:** Claude helped turn a food allergy treatment goal into a design plan and generated approximately 35,000 antibody designs in roughly a month. Several candidates showed promising early binding or dislodging activity.

**BigHat Biosciences:** Claude worked with an automated lab to improve two drug leads. The best versions bound much more tightly and showed better stability and less clumping.

**Enveda:** Claude reviewed 1,061 pages of manufacturing documents, caught all 11 issues human reviewers found, and flagged seven more. Cut human review time by 89% and drafted regulatory documents far faster than manual work.

**The Column Group:** A new company, Pleiades, aims to use Claude to run more and more complex drug programs with a small team. Its first Claude skill, for assessing drug targets, was tested on 45 target-disease pairs.

**Bristol Myers Squibb:** Claude is being used to assess clinical trial plans and estimate their chance of success, with plans to expand in 2027.

## Anthropic's Own Programs

Anthropic acquired Mithril Medicine, whose founder used Claude to coordinate work with 11 external labs. A possible muscular dystrophy treatment moved from idea to promising mouse results in under 18 months, with human trials possibly starting as early as late 2028.
