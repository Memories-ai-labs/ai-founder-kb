# Improving Fable 5's Biology Safeguards
# URL: https://anthropic.com/news/improving-fable-5-s-biology-safeguards
# Date: 2026-08-07
# Source: Anthropic News

## Summary

Anthropic announced updates to Claude Fable 5's biology safeguards designed to reduce false positives. The company reports that "this update reduced biology-related fallbacks by about 85%," enabling the system to assist with a broader range of biological tasks.

## Key Points

**Improved Accessibility:**
Users will encounter substantially fewer instances where the system reverts to a less capable model when addressing health and educational biology questions. Healthcare professionals can now receive enhanced support on clinical matters.

**Dual-Use Concerns:**
Despite improvements, Fable 5 still restricts requests involving virology, toxicology, and molecular design. The organization emphasizes that capability assessments demonstrate "Fable 5 could provide significant uplift to" malicious actors seeking to develop biological weapons.

**Technical Approach:**
Anthropic refined safety classifiers — automated systems that identify potentially harmful biology requests. The classifier's foundational rules were rewritten after consulting diverse experts, then retrained using updated data to better distinguish between harmful and benign uses.

**Safety Philosophy:**
The company launched Fable 5 with extremely restrictive biology controls initially, accepting high false positives to mitigate potential catastrophic risks while continuing refinement work.

**Next Steps:**
Anthropic commits to developing trusted access pathways enabling legitimate researchers to utilize frontier biology capabilities responsibly.
