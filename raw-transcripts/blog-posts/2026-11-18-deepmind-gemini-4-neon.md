**Author:** Koray Kavukcuoglu, SVP, Google DeepMind and Chief AI Architect

## Summary

Google DeepMind introduces Gemini 4 Neon, described as "Bringing frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion" for software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows.

## Key Details

**Positioning:** Neon sits between Carbon (hardest long-horizon projects) and Hydrogen (fast everyday execution) in the Gemini 4 family.

**Benchmarks:**
- #3 overall on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (score 61 at xhigh thinking level)
- #1 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (58.6)
- #2 on the Vals Index

**Pricing:**
- $3 per million input tokens
- $0.15 per million cached input tokens
- $15 per million output tokens
- 1-million-token context window

**Availability:**
- Rolling out in the Gemini API via Google AI Studio
- Gemini for business
- Google Antigravity (paid AI Pro and Ultra subscribers)
- Gemini app (Pro and Ultra subscribers)

**Safety:**
- Neon for cyber going to trusted defenders through the Fairwind Program
- Builds on Gemini 4 safety stack
- 0.0% attack success rate on Gray Swan's indirect prompt injection benchmark at highest thinking level
