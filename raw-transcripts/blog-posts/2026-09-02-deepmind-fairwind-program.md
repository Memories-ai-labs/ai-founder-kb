# Proactive Cyber Defense for Governments and Enterprises: The Fairwind Program
# URL: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/safety-security/fairwind-program/
# Date: 2026-09-02
# Source: Google DeepMind / Google Security Blog
# Author: Four Flynn, VP Security and Privacy

## Program Overview

Google launched the Fairwind Program as "a limited access program for governments and trusted partners to use our most advanced cyber defense capabilities." The initiative aims to deliver autonomous vulnerability detection and remediation at scale for critical infrastructure protection.

## Key Capabilities

The program combines Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber with CodeMender to enable defenders to "find, verify, and fix vulnerabilities at agentic scale." Rather than weeks of manual patching, organizations can now generate "verified, deployment-ready patches in minutes — within an organization's secure cloud environment."

## Target Organizations

Initial access prioritizes three sectors:
- Government agencies and national cyber authorities
- Critical infrastructure operators (healthcare, telecommunications, energy, financial)
- Core technology platforms serving downstream users

## Security Safeguards

Participating organizations must implement strict operational standards, including limiting access to internal cybersecurity and incident response teams, with protections like multi-factor authentication required.

## Broader Impact

Google.org has committed over $100 million globally to cybersecurity initiatives, including $36 million funding for 35 cyber clinics providing free support to 1,250+ U.S. hospitals, school districts, and municipal utilities.

## Partner Participation

The program includes more than 650 global partners, with testimonials from organizations including Armadin, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Snowflake, and Wiz.
