# Cloudera and Mistral Partner to Bring Specialized, Sovereign Intelligence to Enterprise Data
# URL: https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-x-cloudera/
# Date: 2026-09-10
# Source: Mistral AI News

Mistral has partnered with Cloudera to support enterprises in regulated industries seeking to transform mission-critical processes with AI while maintaining complete control over their data and intelligence.

The partnership announcement includes two key components:

**Inference in Customer Environments:** Mistral's models will integrate with Cloudera's hybrid data platform, enabling enterprises to deploy AI across private and public clouds, on-premises, and air-gapped environments while retaining full control.

**Custom Model Development:** Mistral allows enterprises to train AI models on proprietary data within controlled settings, transforming decades of institutional information into customized models while preserving ownership of both data and resulting intelligence.

According to Abhas Ricky, CBM of Applied AI at Cloudera: "General-purpose models are the starting point, not the finish line... models trained on proprietary data... tuned to their business and governed inside their own environment" represent the real competitive advantage.

Kamal Brar, SVP of Partnerships at Mistral, noted the opportunity to bring sovereign AI capabilities to Cloudera's extensive customer-managed data infrastructure.

The collaboration addresses growing demand for sovereign AI—systems where data, intelligence, compute, and operations remain under customer control, including data residency, model ownership, infrastructure selection, and governance without external platform dependency.
