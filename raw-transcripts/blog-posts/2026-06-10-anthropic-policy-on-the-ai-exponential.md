# Policy on the AI Exponential
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/policy-on-the-ai-exponential
# Date: 2026-06-10
# Source: Anthropic

Anthropic presents two complementary policy frameworks addressing AI's rapid advancement. The organization states: "AI is advancing at exponential speed, and the policymaking process was built for a slower world."

## Advanced AI Framework

Targets governance of increasingly capable AI systems.

**Scope:** Applies to models trained with over 10²⁵ floating-point operations, developed by companies with $500M+ AI revenue or $1B+ AI R&D spending.

**Four Catastrophic Risks Addressed:**
- Biological threats from weaponized AI capabilities
- Cybersecurity vulnerabilities in critical infrastructure
- Loss of control over autonomous AI systems
- Automated AI research amplifying other risks

**Developer Requirements:**

Transparency obligations mandate that frontier developers test their models and publish a summary of results. Companies must disclose safety frameworks and conduct regular independent evaluations.

Security measures require protection of model weights and training infrastructure against state-actor threats.

**Government Authority:**

The proposal grants governments power to block or deter the deployment of models that pose a significant risk of catastrophic harm, with penalties tied to global revenue.

## Economic Policy Framework

A second framework addresses workforce preparation and equitable distribution of AI's financial benefits.

## Implementation Path

Anthropic emphasizes federal-level action while opposing broad congressional preemption of state laws addressing issues like child safety outside the framework's scope.
