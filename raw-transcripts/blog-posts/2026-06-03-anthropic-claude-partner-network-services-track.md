# Introducing the Services Track and Partner Hub of the Claude Partner Network
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub
# Date: 2026-06-03
# Source: Anthropic News

## Summary

Anthropic expands its Claude Partner Network (launched March 2026 with $100M investment) with two new initiatives: the Services Track (tiered certification program for consulting firms) and the Claude Partner Hub (daily-updated portal for partners and customers).

## Key Details

**Scale of Claude partner ecosystem:**
- 40,000+ firms have applied to the Partner Network
- 10,000+ consultants have earned Claude certification credentials
- Major consulting firms deploying at scale:
  - Accenture: training 30,000 professionals
  - Cognizant: rolled out to ~350,000 associates
  - Deloitte, KPMG, Infosys, PwC have similarly extensive deployments

**Services Track (three-tier structure):**
1. Select
2. Preferred
3. Global Premier

Each tier measures: certified practitioners + production customer deployments + published customer endorsements. Identical requirements regardless of firm size.

**Claude Partner Hub:**
- Partners track standing against program metrics (updated daily)
- Customers identify qualified implementation firms
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) connector for conversational access to partnership data

**Program features:**
- No entry fees; Registered status requires commitment to develop 10 certified practitioners
- Transparent advancement schedules
- Separate tracking of practice building vs. business referrals
- $100M in training and support resources

## Significance for Founders

Shows Anthropic building a large enterprise consulting ecosystem similar to Salesforce/Salesforce Partners or AWS Partner Network. The MCP integration into the Partner Hub demonstrates Anthropic's broader strategy of making Claude natively accessible across business workflows. For founders selling to enterprises, understanding which consulting partners are Claude-certified will matter for co-sell and implementation support.
