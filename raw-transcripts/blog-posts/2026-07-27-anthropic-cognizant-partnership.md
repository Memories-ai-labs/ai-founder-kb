# Cognizant and Anthropic Expand Their Partnership to Bring Claude to Enterprise Clients

**Title:** Cognizant and Anthropic Expand Their Partnership to Bring Claude to Enterprise Clients
**Source:** Anthropic News
**URL:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/cognizant-anthropic
**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Publisher:** Anthropic

---

Anthropic announced an expansion of its partnership with Cognizant, a major technology services firm. The collaboration focuses on integrating Claude AI into enterprise operations across multiple industries.

## Key Developments

Cognizant is embedding Claude across internal platforms including Flowsource, Neuro AI Engineering, and Neuro IT Ops. The company has trained over 30,000 associates on Claude usage and is becoming a Global Premier Partner in the Claude Partner Network.

## Client Applications

Cognizant has already deployed Claude-powered solutions for enterprises, including:

- A customer experience portal built for a manufacturer within six months
- A contract-intelligence system that "cut contract review time by up to 40 percent while lifting extraction accuracy above 88 percent"
- A risk-navigation tool enabling underwriters to complete evaluations "in minutes" rather than hours of manual research

## Strategic Vision

CEO Ravi Kumar S noted that "AI capability is rising faster than enterprises can absorb it," positioning Cognizant as a bridge between advanced AI and enterprise needs. Anthropic's Daniela Amodei emphasized the value of deploying Claude "in real, practical ways" across demanding industries like manufacturing and life sciences.

The partnership signals continued Anthropic enterprise expansion through systems integrator / professional services channels — a go-to-market model distinct from direct enterprise sales.
