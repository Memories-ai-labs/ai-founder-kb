# Anthropic closes IPO at $83/share including full underwriter option
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/ipo-close
# Date: 2026-11-20
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic, PBC closed its initial public offering on November 20, 2026.

## Key details

- **Shares offered:** 1,127,000,000 Class A common shares at $83.00 per share
- **Underwriter option:** Underwriters exercised their full option to purchase 147,000,000 additional shares
- **Sellers:** Anthropic sold 1,103,351,354 shares; certain selling stockholders sold 23,648,646
- **Gross proceeds (IPO):** ~$93.5 billion total; ~$91.6 billion to Anthropic (before underwriting discounts and expenses)
- **Trading:** Class A shares began trading on Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026, ticker: ANT
- **Concurrent private placement:** 313,253,007 Class N (non-voting) shares sold for ~$26.0 billion to: AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and TSMC Partners
- **Total combined gross proceeds:** ~$119.5 billion (IPO + private placement)
- **Underwriters:** Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley (lead book-runners)
- **SEC:** Registration statement declared effective November 18, 2026

## Context

Anthropic's shares opened trading at $111 on November 19, approximately 33% above the $83 IPO price, making it one of the most significant AI IPOs in history.

The seven Anthropic founders collectively sold ~23.6 million shares, with each founder receiving approximately $280 million before taxes. All seven have pledged (voluntarily, not legally binding) to dedicate 80% of personal equity to philanthropy, potentially directing ~$156 billion to charitable causes based on post-IPO valuations.

The Class N private placement locked in commitments from key AI infrastructure partners (AMD, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, etc.), each receiving non-voting shares — suggesting Anthropic structured the deal to maintain operational independence while securing deep ecosystem backing.
