# Our approach to bioresilience

**Publication Date:** July 16, 2026

**Authors:** Isomorphic Labs and Google DeepMind

**URL:** https://deepmind.google/blog/our-approach-to-bioresilience/

---

## Summary

Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs outline their three-pillar strategy for addressing AI-era biosecurity risks: prevention, detection, and response. The initiative includes 15+ partnerships with government bodies and biosecurity organizations.

---

## Three-Pillar Strategy

### 1. Prevention

- Threat modeling and safety evaluations for AI-generated biological sequences
- Adapting SynthID watermarking technology to biology: enables DNA synthesis providers to identify potentially dangerous AI-generated DNA sequences
- Pre-synthesis screening systems

### 2. Detection

- AlphaEvolve optimizes metagenomic sequencing algorithms → reduces costs for disease surveillance
- AlphaGenome and protein annotation tools → faster identification of novel pathogens from sequence data
- Focus on natural pandemic surveillance as well as AI-related risks

### 3. Response

- Researchers gain access to latest AI systems for vaccine and countermeasure design
- Isomorphic Labs runs a specialized unit that deploys its drug design engine during outbreaks
- Targeting both natural pandemics and AI-generated biological risks

---

## Context

This is part of DeepMind's CBRN (Chemical, Biological, Radiological and Nuclear) risk framework. The collaborative approach emphasizes government and scientific partnerships over proprietary solutions.

---

## Relevance for AI Founders

- SynthID expanding from media watermarking to DNA sequences signals that AI lab safety technology is converging with biosecurity infrastructure
- The "15+ government partnerships" model illustrates how frontier AI labs are positioning safety work as a policy/partnership lever
- Isomorphic Labs deploying drug design AI during actual outbreaks = first real-world signal of AI-accelerated biodefense at deployment scale
