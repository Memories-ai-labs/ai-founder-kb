# Investing in the public benefit
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/public-benefit-fund
# Date: 2026-10-18
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic is committing a portion of its shares to the new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund. Subject to stockholder approval, 5% of the company's shares will be committed now, with an additional 2% added at the board's discretion for every 50% increase in company value above a set baseline. The Fund is a wholly-owned LLC, and its equity will not be used for lobbying or political giving.

The Fund will give Anthropic more flexibility than its in-company programs, such as large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations. One example is labor market impacts from AI, which Anthropic already committed $200 million to studying.

This builds on earlier pledges: co-founders' commitment to donate 80% of their equity, and an employee equity donation match program. Together, Anthropic says, roughly 20% of its shares will be dedicated to the public good, plus any additional amount tied to valuation milestones.

Anthropic plans to share more about the Fund's governance and milestones in coming months, along with a public benefit report.
