# Video intelligence: Make every moment in your video library searchable
# URL: https://cohere.com/blog/video-intelligence-preview
# Date: 2026-11-16
# Source: cohere-blog

Cohere has launched Video Intelligence in Compass, its intelligent search product. It lets AI applications and agents search video collections, find relevant moments, and return timestamped evidence alongside documents.

## Key features

- **Moment-level retrieval:** Start and end times are determined at search time, not from fixed predefined segments.
- **Unified search:** Video, documents, slides, and spreadsheets are searched in one permission-aware index.
- **Verifiable results:** Each result links to the original footage with timestamps and transcripts.
- **Enterprise controls:** Configurable fields, model choices, and deployment options.

## Use cases

Meeting and knowledge retrieval, education and training, sports and media archives, and legal and compliance review.

## Performance

In Cohere's evaluation across 11 datasets and 11,133 queries, Compass ranked first on average nDCG@10, 4.4 points ahead of the strongest alternative of seven video embedding models tested.

## Availability

Cohere is recruiting design partners to test the product on their own footage.
