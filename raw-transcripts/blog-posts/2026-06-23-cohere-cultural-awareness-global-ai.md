# Why Cultural Awareness is Essential for Global AI
# URL: https://cohere.com/blog/why-cultural-awareness-is-essential-for-global-ai
# Date: 2026-06-23
# Source: Cohere Blog
# Authors: Ananya Sahu (Research Scholar), Julia Kreutzer (Senior Research Scientist), Marzieh Fadaee (Head of Cohere Labs)

The piece argues that while modern language models increasingly support multiple languages, language coverage alone doesn't ensure cultural competence. The authors contend that "language is simply not an adequate proxy for culture" and note research showing underrepresented cultures face exclusion and bias risks.

## Key Survey Findings

The authors conducted a survey with 81 participants from over 22 countries:

**Language Switching Problem**: Among non-English primary speakers (83% of respondents), 89.5% reported switching to English when using AI, primarily seeking "higher quality and accuracy of responses."

**Cultural Gaps**: Over one-third felt AI inadequately understood their cultures. Users reported failures in creative writing, translation, local information lookup, and culturally-specific guidance.

**Norm Violations**: Approximately 63% reported instances where AI violated cultural norms, defaulting to Western-centric perspectives and overlooking region-specific practices and traditions.

**Marginalization Concerns**: About 67% expressed worry that AI would "further marginalize their culture in the future or reduce it to stereotypes."

## Recommendations

Culturally aware AI should:
- Provide region-specific localization
- Calibrate communication styles appropriately
- Cite diverse sources
- Handle code-switched language naturally
- Deliver culturally contextual guidance

The authors conclude that "cultural awareness needs to be a core design requirement" to build equitable global technologies.
