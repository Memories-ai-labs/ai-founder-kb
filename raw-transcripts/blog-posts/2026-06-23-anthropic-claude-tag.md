# Introducing Claude Tag
# URL: https://anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-tag
# Date: 2026-06-23
# Source: Anthropic News

Claude Tag represents a new collaborative approach enabling teams to work with Claude through Slack. The system allows Claude to join as a team member with access to selected channels, tools, data, and codebases.

## Key Features

**Multiplayer Functionality**
"Within a given Slack channel, there's one Claude that interacts with everyone." This enables team members to observe ongoing work and continue conversations seamlessly, functioning like a collaborative teammate rather than isolated single-user interactions.

**Contextual Learning**
The system builds understanding by observing channel activity over time. Users need not repeatedly explain contexts, and Claude can automatically learn from authorized Slack channels and data sources to develop tacit knowledge.

**Proactive Assistance**
When ambient behavior is enabled, Claude "will proactively keep you updated about whatever it thinks you might need to know," flagging relevant information and following up on unresolved tasks.

**Asynchronous Operations**
Teams can delegate tasks and focus elsewhere while Claude works independently. The system can schedule its own tasks and pursue projects autonomously across hours or days.

## Availability and Setup

Claude Tag launches in beta for Claude Enterprise and Team customers. Implementation involves four steps: pairing with Slack workspaces, granting tool access, establishing spending limits, and testing functionality. The platform replaces the existing Claude in Slack app, with administrators having 30 days to migrate. Launch credits are available for eligible organizations.
