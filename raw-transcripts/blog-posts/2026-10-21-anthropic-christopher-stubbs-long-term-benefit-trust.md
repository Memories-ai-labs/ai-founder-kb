# Physicist Christopher Stubbs joins Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/christopher-stubbs-joins-long-term-benefit-trust
# Date: 2026-10-21
# Source: Anthropic News

## Summary

Christopher Stubbs, experimental physicist and former Harvard Dean of Science (2018–2024), joins Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust. He is also an advisor on national security and arms control, a member of JASON (independent scientific advisory group to the US government), and part of the team that discovered the accelerating expansion of the universe (1998 High-Z Supernova Search Team, later Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics).

## Key Details

- **The Long-Term Benefit Trust:** Can appoint a majority of Anthropic's board and advises on decisions involving AI risks and societal impacts.
- **Other trustees:** Neil Buddy Shah, Richard Fontaine, Ben Bernanke
- **Stubbs's background:** Inaugural project scientist for the Vera C. Rubin Observatory; has written about keeping AI-assisted research reproducible.
- **Quote (Stubbs):** Wants to help ensure AI is developed for humanity's long-term benefit.
- **Quote (Daniela Amodei):** Praised his "intellectual honesty, rigor, and commitment to the truth."
- **Context:** Announcement accompanies reference to Anthropic's first Public Benefit Report and a planned Anthropic Public Benefit Fund (subject to stockholder approval).

## Significance
Anthropic is building out the scientific and governance credibility of its oversight structure. Adding a world-class experimental physicist with national security experience signals an attempt to strengthen the Trust's ability to evaluate catastrophic-risk scenarios from first principles.
