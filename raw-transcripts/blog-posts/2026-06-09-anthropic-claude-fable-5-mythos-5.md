# Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5

**Published:** June 9, 2026
**Source:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5

## Model Overview

Anthropic launched two new models on June 9, 2026:

**Claude Fable 5** is a Mythos-class model designed for general use with integrated safeguards. It achieves state-of-the-art performance across nearly all capability benchmarks, excelling in software engineering, knowledge work, vision, and scientific research.

**Claude Mythos 5** is the same underlying model as Fable 5 but with safeguards lifted in certain areas. It is initially available only through Project Glasswing for cybersecurity professionals and critical infrastructure providers.

## Key Capabilities

- **Software Engineering**: Stripe reported Fable 5 "compressed months of engineering into days," completing a Ruby codebase migration in one day that would have taken teams two months
- **Vision**: State-of-the-art performance extracting data from scientific figures and completing complex visual tasks
- **Long-context**: Maintains focus across millions of tokens; improved performance with persistent memory access
- **Drug Design**: Mythos 5 accelerated protein design processes approximately tenfold
- **Novel Research**: Generated scientific hypotheses preferred by researchers 80% of the time versus Opus-class models

## Safety Measures

Fable 5 implements three main safeguards via AI classifiers:

1. **Cybersecurity protection**: Blocks responses to exploitation and offensive cyber tasks; fallback to Claude Opus 4.8
2. **Biology/Chemistry limits**: Prevents misuse of dual-use biological research capabilities
3. **Distillation defense**: Prevents extraction attempts targeting competing systems

The system triggers safeguards in less than 5% of sessions on average.

## Pricing & Availability

- **Cost**: $10 per million input tokens, $50 per million output tokens
- **Fable 5**: Available immediately via Claude API and platforms
- **Mythos 5**: Restricted to authorized partners; broader access program coming
- **Subscription rollout**: Free inclusion through June 22; usage credits required after

## Early Partner Feedback

Partners including Cursor, GitHub, and leading research institutions highlighted Fable 5's superior performance on complex, long-horizon tasks with increased autonomy and reliability compared to previous models.
