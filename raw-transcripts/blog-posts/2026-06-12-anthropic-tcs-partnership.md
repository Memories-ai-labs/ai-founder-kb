# TCS and Anthropic Partner to Bring Claude to Regulated Industries

**Title**: TCS and Anthropic partner to bring Claude to regulated industries
**Publisher**: Anthropic
**URL**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/tcs-anthropic-partnership
**Date**: 2026-06-12
**Source**: Anthropic News

---

Anthropic announced a strategic partnership with Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), a major global technology services provider. Under this agreement, TCS will deploy Claude to approximately 50,000 employees across 56 countries and develop Claude-powered solutions for clients in financial services, healthcare, public sector, and other heavily regulated sectors.

TCS joins Anthropic's Claude Partner Network, positioning itself to assist enterprise clients in adopting the AI technology. The partnership emphasizes Claude's suitability for regulated industries where accuracy and auditability are paramount requirements.

## How TCS is Implementing Claude

TCS will function as an early adopter, integrating Claude across engineering, finance, legal, marketing, and sales divisions. The company is establishing a dedicated practice featuring consultants, engineers, and industry specialists who will architect and deploy Claude-based systems tailored to specific sectors.

Notable implementations include:

- **Diligenta** (TCS's UK life and pensions division) plans to enhance customer experience for over 22 million policyholders
- TCS banking teams will utilize Claude Code to improve software engineering and IT operations
- Engineering divisions will develop reusable plugins for claims processing and lending advisory
- **TCS iON** will deliver Claude training and certification across its 75 million annual assessments

K. Krithivasan, TCS CEO: "Enterprise AI value comes from understanding business context, orchestrating complex systems, and applying deep AI engineering talent."

Dario Amodei, Anthropic Co-founder and CEO, noted the partnership strengthens Anthropic's commitment to India, described as the company's second-largest market.
