# Results from the First Anthropic Public Record

**Title**: Results from the first Anthropic Public Record
**Publisher**: Anthropic
**URL**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-public-record
**Date**: 2026-06-12
**Source**: Anthropic News

---

Anthropic released findings from its inaugural Public Record survey, examining American public opinion on artificial intelligence. The research involved nearly 52,000 Americans surveyed between November and December 2025.

## Key Findings on Public Hopes

Nearly half of respondents (48%) identified disease curing as a top-three hope for AI advancement. Supporting individuals with disabilities ranked second at 36%, followed by technological progress and quality-of-life improvements, both at 23%.

## Primary Concerns

Job displacement emerged as the dominant fear, with 64% of Americans expressing worry about AI-induced job loss. This concern appeared consistent across political affiliation and geography. Cognitive dependency ranked second at 56% of respondents, with misinformation concerns at 52%.

## Notable Pattern: Education and Job Anxiety

Anxiety about job displacement increased with educational attainment. Those holding postgraduate degrees showed approximately 10 percentage points higher concern than those with high school education or less.

## Hands-On Experience Reduces Anxiety

Daily AI users at work demonstrated notably lower job-loss anxiety (54%) compared to non-users (70%), suggesting practical experience may alleviate concerns.

## Governance Preferences

Over 70% of Americans supported government involvement in AI regulation on a bipartisan basis. When identifying specific priorities, respondents emphasized privacy (56%), child safety (52%), and liability for harm (49%).

## Trust Deficit

Only 15% of Americans expressed trust in AI companies regarding development decisions — lower than trust in federal government (20%) or independent experts (43%).

## Recommendations from Public

When asked about ensuring beneficial AI development, respondents prioritized legal liability for companies (47%) and prioritizing safety over growth (44%).
