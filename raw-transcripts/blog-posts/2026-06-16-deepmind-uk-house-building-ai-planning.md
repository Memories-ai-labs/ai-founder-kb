# Unlocking UK House-Building with AI-Accelerated Planning

Google DeepMind is partnering with the UK government to develop an AI-powered planning prototype designed to accelerate housing development. The initiative aims to reduce the time required to process householder planning applications by 50%, supporting the UK's goal of constructing 1.5 million new homes by 2029.

## Co-Creating the AI Planning Tool

Building on the success of Extract—a tool that converts legacy planning documents into usable digital data—Google DeepMind is collaborating with the UK government's Incubator for AI, Google Cloud, Faculty, and local authorities in Barnet, Dorset, and Camden to develop an advanced planning assistant.

The prototype functions as "a highly skilled assistant for planning officers, handling the heavy lifting of data extraction and case analysis." Following early trials, the government plans national rollout to all councils in 2027.

## Key Capabilities

The AI planning tool streamlines work by:

- **Consolidating data** from multiple sources onto a single screen
- **Identifying policies** relevant to each application with exact citations
- **Summarizing feedback** from consultation letters
- **Drafting assessments** including reasoning and proposed conditions

Critically, planning officers retain decision-making authority and review all AI-generated content.

## Impact and Scale

Extract has already demonstrated significant value across 20+ local planning authorities, expected to save councils approximately 255 hours annually. The new prototype addresses the reality that householder applications comprise nearly 70% of annual planning submissions.
