# Crunchbase in North: Private market signals for growth-stage companies
# URL: https://cohere.com/blog/crunchbase-in-north
# Date: 2026-11-17
# Source: cohere-blog

Cohere announced a Crunchbase Connector for its North platform, giving North access to data on over five million companies, including funding and M&A history, competitors, growth signals, and Crunchbase's predictive market models.

## Use cases

**Investors and M&A:** Spotting opportunities and risks in a pipeline, and generating memos or target lists.

**Go-to-market and market research:** Identifying companies and contacts, and connecting to tools like Salesforce.

**Analysts:** Mapping markets and tracking new entrants.

## Details

The connector respects existing Crunchbase and North permissions. Figures come with citations, and forecasts are labeled separately from reported or estimated valuations. Available starting November 17, 2026, to North customers with an active Crunchbase license and SSO enabled. Admins can turn it on in their organization's connector settings.
