# Introducing Claude for Teachers
# URL: https://anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers
# Date: 2026-07-14
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic has launched Claude for Teachers, offering verified K-12 educators in the US complimentary access to premium Claude capabilities, a curated teaching skills library, and connections to evidence-based curricula aligned with academic standards across all 50 states.

## Why This Initiative Exists

Educational research spanning decades demonstrates that differentiation, mastery-based learning, and small group instruction significantly enhance student achievement. However, teachers frequently lack the time and resources to implement these practices effectively. Budget constraints, oversized classrooms, and planning that extends beyond school hours create particular strain in under-resourced schools. Claude for Teachers aims to bridge the gap between what research shows works and what fits into a teacher's realistic weekly schedule.

## Integration with Educational Ecosystems

The platform connects with Learning Commons, providing access to academic standards and their underlying learning competencies across all states. It incorporates trusted curricular resources including OpenSciEd and Illustrative Mathematics. Teachers can also integrate Claude across nine existing K-12 educational tools, including ASSISTments for math problems, Canva Education for design, and Eedi for diagnostic questioning.

## Core Capabilities

The platform includes three primary functions: drafting standards-aligned lesson plans, creating differentiated materials for students at varying readiness levels, and analyzing classroom data to inform instruction. Claude Code and Cowork features enable automated task management, such as scheduling daily exit ticket reviews at 4 PM.

## Privacy and Safety

Claude for Teachers restricts access to verified educators only, maintaining separate terms aligned with K-12 privacy requirements. Student data remains excluded from model training and receives protection under FERPA compliance through a K-12 Data Processing Addendum.

## Professional Development

Educators gain access to AI Fluency for K-12 Teachers, a course developed with Teach for America providing model-agnostic guidance on appropriate classroom AI applications.

## Availability

Verified educators can access Claude for Teachers free through June 30, 2027. School and district offerings are forthcoming.
