## Key Announcements

Anthropic introduced two new AI models designed for advanced coding and knowledge work. These versions represent the same underlying model with different safeguard levels—Fable 5.1 is publicly available while Mythos 5.1 requires vetted access for cybersecurity and life sciences professionals.

## Performance Improvements

The models demonstrate substantial capability gains across multiple benchmarks:

- **Scientific Research:** On Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, Fable 5.1 achieved 52.6% accuracy compared to Fable 5's 24.7%
- **Coding Tasks:** Performance on Terminal-Bench 4.0 reached 55.8% for Fable 5.1 versus 42.0% for its predecessor
- **Cost Efficiency:** "Fable 5.1 will cost an estimated 25% less than Fable 5 for typical workloads"

## Notable Features

**Pricing:** Cache read operations now cost 75% less ($0.25 per million tokens), reducing overall expenses by approximately 25% for standard usage and up to 45% for complex agentic work.

**Data Privacy:** Enterprise Frontier Safeguards enable zero-data-retention policies while maintaining security protections through customer-controlled cloud infrastructure.

**Scientific Capabilities:** The models demonstrated breakthrough results in protein design, achieving 10x higher binding affinities than previous competition benchmarks and enabling GPU optimization for genomic analyses.

## Safety Enhancements

Safeguards became more precise, reducing false positives by 60% in cybersecurity applications while maintaining protective measures against genuine threats.
