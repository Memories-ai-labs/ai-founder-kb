# AI for Science expands to foundations and research institutions
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/ai-for-science-expands-foundations-research-institutions
# Date: 2026-10-28
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic announced an expansion of its AI for Science program, which provides free Claude credits to researchers on high-impact projects. The program began in May 2025 and has supported hundreds of projects in biology, medicine, physics, chemistry, climate science, and mathematics.

The expansion adds three groups of partners:

**Science funders and societies:** Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, Simons Foundation, Schmidt Sciences, Open Source for Science Fund, US National Science Foundation, and CAS (American Chemical Society division). These organizations will distribute credits to researchers.

**Research institutions:** Space Telescope Science Institute, Wellcome Sanger Institute, Francis Crick Institute, Imperial College London Department of Life Sciences, EMBL, and Gladstone Institutes.

**Specific research projects:** FlyWire (fruit fly brain mapping), LEAP (Earth system modeling), AstroPath (cancer imaging), gnomAD (human genetic variation), Tau Ceti (shared Lean mathematics library), and Centre for Population Genomics.

A collaboration with HHMI Janelia Research Campus on the Model Hardware Standard — a specification that lets AI agents safely operate lab instruments — is currently in research preview.

Many projects will produce public goods like open datasets and software. Anthropic describes the collaborations as early-stage.
