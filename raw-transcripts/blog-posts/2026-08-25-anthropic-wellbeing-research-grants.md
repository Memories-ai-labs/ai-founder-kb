# Funding better evaluations of AI's impact on wellbeing
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/wellbeing-research-grants
# Date: 2026-08-25
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic announced a $5 million grant initiative designed to support independent researchers examining how AI systems influence user wellbeing. The program provides direct funding, model access, and technical assistance to grantees developing open-source evaluation tools.

## Overview

As AI has become integral to work, learning, and problem-solving, it also functions as a conversational companion and emotional support resource. However, the field still lacks clear behavioral standards for these interactions, particularly during mental health crises or when users seek companionship.

Wellbeing evaluation presents unique challenges. As Anthropic notes, assessing impact "requires much more context" than typical model evaluation. A response appropriate in one situation might prove harmful in another—for instance, fitness advice could be counterproductive for someone with disordered eating history.

## Evaluation Criteria

Anthropic seeks evaluations that:

- Clearly define what constitutes success or failure
- Involve clinical experts in design and validation
- Examine both safeguards and potential harms
- Reflect authentic multi-turn conversation patterns
- Validate graders against subject-matter experts

## Application Timeline

Interested researchers can apply through Anthropic's online form, with a September 21 deadline. Selected applicants will receive full-proposal notification by October 5.
