# Introducing Claude Corps

**Published:** June 11, 2026
**Source:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-corps

## Overview

Anthropic launched Claude Corps, a fellowship initiative designed to extend AI benefits to communities nationwide. The program will train 1,000 fellows to use Claude effectively, pair them with nonprofits across America, and provide full-time, in-person employment for one year. Anthropic committed an initial $150 million to this effort.

## Program Structure

Claude Corps operates as a partnership among three organizations:
- **Anthropic** — funding and strategy
- **CodePath** — employer of record and programming
- **Social Finance** — measurement and financial scaling

## Fellowship Details

- **Duration:** 12 months
- **Salary:** $85,000 annually plus benefits
- **Training:** Intensive initial training, plus five weekly hours of ongoing instruction
- **Support:** Weekly mentorship, office hours with Anthropic technical staff, and professional guidance from host managers
- **Eligibility:** Anyone over 18 with less than two years of full-time work experience; U.S. work authorization required; relocation support available

## Host Organization Examples (Inaugural Cohort)

- **Braven** — teaches first-generation students job skills
- **Code the Dream** — provides coding education and apprenticeships
- **Montgomery County Food Bank** — serves Houston-area families
- **Team Red, White & Blue** — supports veteran wellness
- **REEF** — conducts marine conservation research

## Application Timeline

- Fellowship applications: Open through July 17, 2026 (first cohort of 100)
- Host organization applications: Open immediately
- Cohort start dates: October 2026, January 2027, and August 2027

## Key Goals

The program aims to equip nonprofits with valuable AI tools while developing fellows' technical skills for future careers. Anthropic plans to measure outcomes, open-source enabling technology, and potentially expand the model internationally.
