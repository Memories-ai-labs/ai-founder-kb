# Claude Frontier Academy: $100M to Train 10,000 Engineers
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-frontier-academy
# Date: 2026-10-02
# Source: anthropic

Anthropic has launched the Claude Frontier Academy, a $100 million initiative designed to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027. The program addresses a critical gap in AI talent within enterprises seeking to implement AI solutions effectively.

The Academy's first program, the Frontier Deployed Engineer Residency, follows a medical training model. Engineers learn from experienced practitioners, work through realistic scenarios, and undergo assessments before earning credentials. The curriculum covers enterprise deployment processes, from identifying appropriate use cases through security reviews to final implementation.

Participants must be hands-on software engineers with solid fundamentals, prior experience building with large language models, and a track record of helping others adopt AI technologies. Organizations nominate their strongest engineers, each bringing a specific Claude project to lead upon returning to their companies.

The program begins with a multi-day in-person component in San Francisco, New York, or London, where participants work through a simulated enterprise scenario and complete a graded practical assessment. Those who pass advance to a 12-week residency period, during which they lead actual Claude implementations at their organizations while receiving mentorship from Anthropic engineers.

Initial cohorts include participants from leading firms: Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, and Novo Nordisk. These organizations recognize the program's focus on practical, business-driven problem-solving rather than theoretical certification.
