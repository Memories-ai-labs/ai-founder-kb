# Barclays Scales Claude to Upgrade Operations and Improve Client Experience
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/barclays-scales-claude
# Date: 2026-10-01
# Source: anthropic

Barclays, a major British universal bank, is significantly expanding its partnership with Anthropic to deploy Claude AI systems across its global operations. The expansion focuses on accelerating software development, modernizing outdated systems, and enhancing operational efficiency.

The bank expects Claude Code adoption to reach 50% of its developer workforce by year-end 2026, with majority adoption among software engineers anticipated in 2027.

## Key Applications

**Customer Service Support:** Barclays' Colleague Knowledge Assistant, powered by Claude and active since 2025, helps over 16,000 UK employees serve the bank's 20 million retail customers more effectively. The system has processed more than one million searches.

**Email Processing:** Within Global Markets operations, Claude models classify and route approximately 120,000 incoming emails daily, reducing manual work while improving efficiency and information prioritization.

## Responsible Deployment

Anne Marie Darling, Group Co-Chief Operating Officer, emphasized that expanding Claude usage means "transforming how work gets done," enabling colleagues to concentrate on complex problem-solving while automating routine tasks.

Craig Bright, also Group Co-Chief Operating Officer, noted that "AI as an increasingly agentic capability embedded within how we build, test, secure, and operate technology" represents a fundamental shift in enterprise development practices.
