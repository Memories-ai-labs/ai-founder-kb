# Cohere Triples UK Footprint with New London Office to Support R&D Growth

**Title**: Cohere triples UK footprint with new London office to support R&D growth
**Publisher**: Cohere
**URL**: https://cohere.com/blog/cohere-triples-uk-footprint-with-new-london-office-to-support-r-and-d-growth
**Date**: 2026-06-15
**Source**: Cohere Blog

---

Cohere is significantly expanding its London presence by relocating to 100 New Oxford Street, nearly tripling its current office space to over 14,000 square feet with capacity for approximately 100 employees.

This expansion marks a strategic milestone in the company's European growth strategy, building on recent developments including an intended merger with Germany's Aleph Alpha, the acquisition of Reliant AI, and partnerships with Spain's Indra Group and the UK Government focused on sovereign AI initiatives.

## Key Leadership Perspectives

**CEO Aidan Gomez** stated the move represents "an investment in London's R&D ecosystem" and positions Cohere "at the heart of the UK's sovereign AI revolution." He emphasized that Britain combines "unparalleled research talent with forward-thinking regulatory frameworks."

**Mayor of London Sadiq Khan** called it "a strong vote of confidence in our capital," while **Minister for UK AI and Online Safety Kanishka Narayan** noted it demonstrates "Britain is the place to build, hire and grow" and highlights the nation's focus on secure AI deployment.

## Strategic Context

London has served as a crucial hub for Cohere's engineering, research, and commercial operations, with both the CEO and CTO based in the UK. The company generates strong revenue from contracts across regulated sectors including finance, healthcare, manufacturing, telecommunications, energy, and public administration.

The new workspace, designed by London-based Thirdway Design, will consolidate operations on a single floor and is expected to complete later in 2026.

## Strategic Significance for AI Founders

Cohere's UK expansion reflects several key trends:
- Enterprise AI companies are building sovereign AI infrastructure in Europe to serve regulated industries
- The UK is emerging as a key hub for AI R&D outside the US, competing with France (Mistral) and Germany (Aleph Alpha)
- The merger with Aleph Alpha (announced earlier in 2026) would create a significant European challenger to US AI labs in enterprise markets
