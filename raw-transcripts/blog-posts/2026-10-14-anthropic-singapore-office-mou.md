# Anthropic opens Singapore office and signs MOU with the Government of Singapore
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/singapore-office-opening
# Date: 2026-10-14
# Source: Anthropic

## Key points

- **New office:** Anthropic's fifth Asia-Pacific office opens in Singapore; signed MOU with the Singapore Ministry of Digital Development and Information.
- **MOU scope:** Cooperation on AI safety, security, and model evaluation; research on AI adoption impact; responsible adoption across public and private sectors. Planned technical exchanges with Singapore's AI safety and assurance institutions.
- **Background:** Anthropic has worked with Singapore since 2023 (joined Generative AI Evaluation Sandbox). Later ran red-teaming project in English, Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil.
- **Adoption:** Singapore ranks #2 of 121 countries in Claude.ai usage relative to population. Enterprise users include GovTech Singapore (1,500+ technical staff use Claude Code), DBS, GIC, Grab, Sea, and Supabase.
- **Community:** Hosted Builder Summit (300+ developers) and community meetup (700+ local builders). Singapore office is open and hiring.
