# Claude Duet App Is Anthropic's Bet on Agents
# URL: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/
# Date: 2026-10-22
# Source: TechCrunch (Ivan Mehta)

## Summary

Anthropic launches Claude Duet, a new app targeting professionals and small businesses for agentic AI tasks. It runs autonomously in background on a virtual machine, connects to 6,000+ plugins, and can make purchases on users' behalf. Research preview limited to Pro and Max customers (waitlist). Built on Claude Opus 5.5.

## Key Details

### What Claude Duet Does
- Builds daily briefings, creates dashboards, and suggests actions based on user data
- Runs tasks in the background on its own virtual machine; returns when it needs user approval
- Proactively delivers recommendations while monitoring ongoing tasks
- Connects to 6,000+ connectors and plugins in the Claude Marketplace
- Can make online purchases via Stripe's Link wallet or Shopify's Shop Pay (user approval required for sensitive steps)
- Cannot trade securities or move money between accounts
- Does NOT show live browser use (distinguishing it from personal agents like Instinct)
- Not designed for errands or ticket booking

### Safety
- New classifier screens messages to prevent sensitive information leakage
- By default declines requests for credit card numbers and government ID

### Availability
- Research preview: iOS, Android apps + web (claude.ai)
- Limited to Pro and Max customers; join waitlist

### Competitive Landscape
- Rivals named: Meta Muse, OpenAI Dots, Hark, Instinct, Grok Bot, Fo
- Anthropic positioning: professionals and small businesses (vs. consumer agents)

## Strategic Significance
Claude Duet marks Anthropic's formal entry into the agentic AI assistant market. The 6,000+ connector ecosystem suggests Anthropic is building platform leverage similar to Salesforce or Zapier but at the AI layer. Background VM execution + purchase capability represents a significant step toward AI acting with real-world economic agency.
