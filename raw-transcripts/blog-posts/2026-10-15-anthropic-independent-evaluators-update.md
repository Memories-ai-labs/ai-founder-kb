# An Update on Independent Evaluators

*Published October 15, 2026 by Anthropic*

## Summary

Anthropic announces expanded commitments to independent safety evaluation, including giving METR employees "employee-like access" to assess its safety practices and risk levels.

---

## METR Access (Tier 3 Arrangement)

- Six METR (nonprofit AI evaluation) employees receive company hardware, office space, and internal tools roughly on par with Anthropic's own risk assessment teams
- METR will publish its analysis of Anthropic's risk levels, incidents, or practices
- Anthropic cannot veto publication but can require redaction of predefined categories: customer data, privileged information, and model architectures
- Background: Anthropic committed to stronger independent assessment last month and signed the White House Accord on Superintelligence (includes pledges to work with external auditors/evaluators)

---

## Other Evaluator Partners

- **SecureBio**: reviewing the chemical and biological weapons chapter of Anthropic's August 2026 Risk Report; previously piloted on the preceding report
- **Accenture and Faculty**: working toward broader partnership covering red-teaming, alignment assessments, safeguard testing, and capability building
- **Hugging Face**: early-stage discussions underway

---

## Why Independent Evaluators

Anthropic outlines four criteria for evaluators:
1. **Expertise** — must understand frontier AI risks
2. **Independence** — no equity or financial/operational dependence on Anthropic
3. **Sufficient access** — enough to form real judgments
4. **Freedom to publish** — public accountability requires publication rights

Analogy used: bank supervision, where regulators place resident examiners inside the largest banks.

---

## Three Tiers of Evaluator Access

1. **Tier 1**: Narrowly scoped investigations; Anthropic approves access and publication
2. **Tier 2**: Broader access; publication without Anthropic's approval; Anthropic can require redactions; evaluators can comment on whether redactions were justified
3. **Tier 3 (METR arrangement)**: Employee-like access + publication rights similar to Tier 2

---

## Leak Caution and Architecture Access

- Evaluators may work with multiple AI companies — inadvertent leak risk is real
- Model architecture information: high leak risk, often low relevance → shared only when highly relevant, with explicit internal approval

---

## Anthropic's Stance

"We are embedding evaluators voluntarily and believe every frontier AI company should do the same."
