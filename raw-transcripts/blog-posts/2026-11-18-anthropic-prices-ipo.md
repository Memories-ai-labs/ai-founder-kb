# Anthropic Prices Initial Public Offering at $83 Per Share
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering
# Date: 2026-11-18
# Source: Anthropic News

## Summary

Anthropic, PBC priced its initial public offering (IPO) of Class A common stock at $83.00 per share — above the projected price range. Shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026, under the ticker symbol "ANT."

## Key Details

**Pricing:** $83.00 per share (above projected range)

**Size:**
- Total offering: 980,000,000 shares
- Anthropic selling: 956,351,354 shares
- Selling stockholders: 23,648,646 shares
- Anthropic receives no proceeds from selling stockholders' shares

**Trading:**
- Exchange: Nasdaq Global Select Market
- Ticker: ANT
- Expected trading start: November 19, 2026
- Expected closing: November 20, 2026 (subject to customary closing conditions)

**Underwriter option:** 30-day option to buy up to 147,000,000 additional shares

**SEC:** Registration statement declared effective November 18, 2026

**Lead underwriters:** Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC

## Significance

This marks Anthropic's transition to a public company, one of the most significant AI IPOs in history. The pricing above the projected range signals strong institutional demand for the leading AI safety company.
