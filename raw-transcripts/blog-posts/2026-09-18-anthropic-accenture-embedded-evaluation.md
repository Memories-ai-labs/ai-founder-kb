# Partnering with Accenture on Embedded Evaluation
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation
# Date: 2026-09-18
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic has announced a partnership with Accenture to conduct independent evaluation of frontier AI systems. This collaboration represents progress toward the commitment outlined in CEO Dario Amodei's essay to "embed evaluators within Anthropic."

The initiative, spearheaded by Faculty (Accenture's specialist AI division), will encompass model evaluation, red-teaming, alignment assessments, and safeguard testing. Both organizations plan to invest at least $1 billion over five years to build capacity in this domain.

Embedded evaluators will operate within Anthropic with employee-level access, enabling them to observe model development, monitor deployment decisions, and engage directly with staff. This positioning allows evaluators to assess company operations, verify safety commitments, and identify potential oversight gaps while reporting findings to the public.

The arrangement does not diminish Anthropic's accountability; rather, it makes responsibility more transparent. The company acknowledges that standards for evaluator access and reporting protocols remain underdeveloped. Long-term solutions should involve "pooled or government sources," as Anthropic previously advocated.

Since neither funding mechanism currently exists, Anthropic will directly finance Accenture's work while exploring pilot programs with organizations like METR using independent funding sources.

The partnership remains non-exclusive, with additional evaluators expected to be announced shortly.
