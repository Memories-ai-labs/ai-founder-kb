# Building on Our Commitment to American Scientific Discovery
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/genesis-mission-commitment
# Date: 2026-10-08
# Source: Anthropic News

---

Anthropic is committing $150 million over three years to the Genesis Mission, a federal initiative that uses AI to accelerate scientific and technological discovery.

## Key details

- Claude available to more than 15 participating agencies, including NASA, NIH, and NSF
- Builds on a December partnership with the U.S. Department of Energy, including work at national laboratories
- Announced at the Science: A New Golden Age Summit hosted by the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy

## Commitments over three years

- Claude, Claude Code, and API credits to several hundred Genesis Mission projects
- Agency partnerships on priorities like fusion energy and quantum computing
- Training and technical support for scientists

## Related offerings

- **Claude Science workbench:** Specialized environment for scientific research workflows
- **10,000 free or discounted seats** for academic scientists
- **Expanded AI for Science credits**
- **Model Hardware Standard (research preview):** Shared specification for AI agents to safely operate lab instruments — connects to Periodic Labs-style physical-world AI use cases
