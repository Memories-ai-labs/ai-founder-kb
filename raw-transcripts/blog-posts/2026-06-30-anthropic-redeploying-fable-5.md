# Redeploying Claude Fable 5
# Publisher: Anthropic
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5
# Date: 2026-06-30
# Source: Anthropic News

## Overview

Anthropic announced the restoration of access to Claude Fable 5 following the lifting of export controls that had been imposed on June 12, 2026. The model becomes available globally beginning July 1, 2026, across Claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork.

## Background on the Export Control

The U.S. government applied export controls to both Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 after Amazon researchers discovered a method to bypass Fable 5's safety mechanisms. Specifically, the technique involved "prompting it so that it identified a number of software vulnerabilities," with at least one case producing exploitable code.

Anthropic's testing revealed that less capable models could replicate the same behavior, indicating the jailbreak didn't expose unique capabilities inherent to Fable 5 alone.

## Response and Safeguards

Anthropic deployed an improved safety classifier targeting the specific bypass technique, blocking it in over 99% of cases. The company emphasized that "Fable 5 launched with a variety of safety mechanisms" designed to work together.

## Industry Framework Initiative

Recognizing the lack of consensus standards for assessing AI jailbreak severity, Anthropic partnered with Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and other firms to develop a framework evaluating jailbreaks on four criteria:

- Capability gain
- Breadth of capability gain
- Ease of weaponization
- Discoverability

## Government Collaboration

Anthropic committed to deeper collaboration with U.S. government partners through:

- Pre-release government access and evaluation for frontier models
- Rapid information sharing on safeguards and jailbreaks
- Dedicated joint research resources
- Working toward shared security standards across the industry
