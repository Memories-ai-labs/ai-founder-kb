# DXC Will Integrate Claude Into Systems Banks, Airlines, and Other Regulated Industries Rely On
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/dxc-anthropic-alliance
# Date: 2026-06-11
# Source: Anthropic

Anthropic announced a multi-year partnership with DXC Technology, a major IT services provider, to deploy Claude into mission-critical systems across heavily regulated sectors.

## Key Partnership Details

DXC plans to train tens of thousands of Claude-certified forward-deployed engineers who will embed the AI directly into customer environments. These systems currently serve "the world's largest banks, airlines, insurers, manufacturers, and government agencies."

## Internal Implementation First

Before rolling out to clients, DXC tested Claude within its own operations spanning 115,000 employees across 70 countries. The company launched DXC OASIS, an AI-native orchestration platform where "Claude sped up software development by a factor of 10." More than 95% of the code was generated by Claude, with engineers providing review and validation.

## Four Priority Areas

1. **Insurance** — Agentic solutions and system modernization
2. **Modernization as a Service** — Legacy codebase analysis and refactoring
3. **Cybersecurity** — Always-on security engineer subagents
4. **Application Services** — Maintenance and management agents

DXC CEO Raul Fernandez stated this represents "a defining moment for DXC and for the industry," combining trust with advanced AI capabilities.
