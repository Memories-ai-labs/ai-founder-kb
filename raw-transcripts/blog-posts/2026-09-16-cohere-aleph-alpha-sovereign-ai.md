Cohere announced a definitive business combination agreement with Aleph Alpha, creating what the companies describe as "the first transatlantic sovereign AI solution." The merged entity will operate globally as Cohere while maintaining deep roots in both Canada and Germany.

## Key Details

**Headquarters & Operations:**
The combined company plans dual headquarters in Berlin and Toronto, with Heidelberg retained as a research center. The transaction is expected to increase headcount to over 1,000 employees across both continents.

**Leadership Changes:**
- Ilhan Scheer (Aleph Alpha Co-CEO) becomes Chief Operating Officer
- Samuel Weinbach (Aleph Alpha Co-Chief Research Officer) becomes Chief Research Officer

**Strategic Focus:**
The partnership emphasizes sovereignty and data control. As CEO Aidan Gomez stated, organizations shouldn't have to "choose between capable AI and control over their technology." The combined entity will operate within legal frameworks of both jurisdictions with integrated safeguards.

**Notable Partnership:**
Cohere will advance its collaboration with Schwarz Group to deliver sovereign AI solutions on STACKIT, Europe's sovereign cloud service.

**Status:** Transaction remains subject to regulatory approvals, expected closure later in 2026.

## Significance for AI Founders

Cohere is executing a clear sovereign AI GTM: acquire a European lab (Aleph Alpha), gain regulatory legitimacy in Germany and the EU, and position against US hyperscalers for government and regulated enterprise contracts. This is the first formal transatlantic AI merger of this scale — signals sovereign AI has matured from positioning to M&A strategy.
