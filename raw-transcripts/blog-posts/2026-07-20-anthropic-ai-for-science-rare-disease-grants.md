# Apply for Anthropic's AI for Science Rare Disease Research Grants

**Date:** July 20, 2026

## Overview

Anthropic launched a focused grants program supporting rare genetic disease research through its AI for Science initiative. Successful applicants receive up to **$50,000 in Claude credits** over six months. Application deadline: August 2, 2026.

## Program Structure

**Track One: Basic Science Partnerships**
Collaboration among clinical researchers, patient organizations, and data scientists. Grantees work with the Monarch Initiative — an international consortium developing computational frameworks for rare disease understanding. Resources: Mondo Disease Ontology and DisMech ("an agent-friendly mechanistic disease classification library").

**Track Two: Biotech Partnerships**
Early-stage biotechnology companies accelerating drug development for rare diseases. Focus: streamlining regulatory documentation, therapeutic strategy selection, and identifying shared mechanisms across genetic therapies.

## Scale of Problem

~400 million people globally live with one of thousands of rare diseases. Small patient populations, limited clinical data, and difficulty identifying shared disease mechanisms make these uniquely hard research problems.

## Competitive Project Examples
- Mechanism discovery
- Biomarker identification
- Regulatory documentation drafting
