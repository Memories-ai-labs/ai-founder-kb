# Expanding the Cyber Verification Program
# URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/cyber-verification-program
# Date: 2026-10-06
# Source: Anthropic News

Anthropic launched an expanded Cyber Verification Program (CVP) with three access tiers for security professionals. The program provides "advanced cyber capabilities and reduced blocking classifiers" to qualified defenders while maintaining safeguards against misuse.

## Three Access Tiers

**Defense Access** targets defensive security work including incident response, malware analysis, and vulnerability assessment. The company expects "many organizations conducting defensive cybersecurity work to qualify" and aims for responses within days.

**Red Team Access** adds authorized penetration testing for organizations like in-house red teams and security firms. This tier requires "a few weeks to review" and is limited to organizations, not individuals.

**Specialized Access** has minimal cyber blocks for verified organizations testing critical infrastructure systems like power grids and flight systems, reviewed "in collaboration with the US government."

## Impact Metrics

Through Project Glasswing, partners uncovered at least 129,000 verified vulnerabilities between April–July 2026, with over 33,000 rated as critical or high-severity. Several partners reported Claude models "increased their rate of vulnerability finding by months or even years."

## Availability

The program is accessible through Claude Platform, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. Interested organizations can apply at the CVP portal.
