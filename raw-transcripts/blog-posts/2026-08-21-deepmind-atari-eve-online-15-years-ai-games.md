## Overview

Google DeepMind chronicles its evolution in AI gaming research, spanning from early achievements like Deep Q-Network's mastery of Atari games to contemporary work with sophisticated game environments.

## Key Milestones

- **Deep Q-Network (DQN)**: Early mastery of Atari games — demonstrated reinforcement learning at scale
- **AlphaGo (2016)**: Triumph over Lee Sedol
- **AlphaZero**: Chess breakthroughs
- **AlphaStar**: Grandmaster status in StarCraft II

These achievements demonstrated AI's capacity to navigate increasingly complex gaming scenarios.

## SIMA 2

The company's newer focus involves SIMA 2, an AI agent that "acts as an interactive companion capable of real-time reasoning" by observing screens and interpreting natural language instructions without requiring game code access.

## EVE Online Partnership

A significant announcement involves partnering with Fenris Creations on the EVE Universe — a persistent multiplayer space simulation spanning over two decades. This collaboration addresses frontier AI challenges including:
- Continuous learning
- Long-horizon planning
- Complex multi-agent dynamics across evolving virtual worlds

The partnership extends across EVE Online, EVE Vanguard (first-person perspective gameplay), and EVE Frontier (programmable environments with adaptable mechanics).

DeepMind emphasizes maintaining AI as "a catalyst, not a replacement" while advancing both gaming experiences and broader scientific discovery applications.
