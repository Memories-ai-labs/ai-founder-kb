## Episode Description

Examines Seinfeld as a business. The show has been watched by 72 million Americans so far this year (decades after ending). Credits success to: obsessive creators, well-timed market conditions, and an ownership structure that aligned incentives for the long term.

## Key Themes

- Seinfeld as a startup/business case study
- Content ownership and long-term incentive alignment in entertainment
- Syndication economics and media licensing
- Creator control vs. network control
