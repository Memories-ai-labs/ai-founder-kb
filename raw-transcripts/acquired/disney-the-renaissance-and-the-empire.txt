## Episode Overview

This episode covers Disney's dramatic turnaround from near-collapse in 1984 through its emergence as a dominant media empire. The narrative spans from Michael Eisner and Frank Wells' leadership during the animation renaissance through Bob Iger's era acquiring Pixar, Marvel, and Lucasfilm — before facing new challenges from tech companies.

## Key Topics Covered

- Disney's animation stagnation and corporate raider threats in 1984
- The Eisner-Wells turnaround strategy
- Home video and theatrical distribution innovations
- ESPN acquisition as transformative media asset
- Animation renaissance: Beauty and the Beast, The Lion King
- Animation struggles post-Wells era (Chicken Little era)
- Comcast's hostile takeover attempt
- Bob Iger's acquisitions creating a 21st-century media empire (Pixar, Marvel, Lucasfilm)
- Streaming era challenges
