## Sally Kornbluth

### ★★★ [Strategy] · Sequoia podcast (2026)

Crisis response playbook: (1) calm exterior no matter how on fire things are inside, (2) clarify remarks quickly (don't sit on it), (3) the board publicly backs you immediately, (4) propagate the correct message — "When you explain, you lose."

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_2opghuq88)

### ★★★ [Hiring] · Sequoia podcast (2026)

"If you take a lick of the lollipop of mediocrity, you suck forever" — worse than not hiring is hiring mediocre, because it sets a precedent. CEOs going from 100 → 500 people, 80% of hires are made by other people, so the bar inevitably slides — "it's easier to stop the slide than to recover."

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_2opghuq88)

### ★★★ [Engineering & Management] · Sequoia podcast (2026)

"Don't solve problems with processes. Solve with DRIs + operating control" — the moment you add process, you start managing process instead of outcome. "Push decisions down, you can't punish mistakes — leadership corrects quickly, not prevents."

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_2opghuq88)
