## Logan Kilpatrick + Tulsee Doshi

### ★★★ [Strategy] · Cognitive Revolution (2026-05)

Google's differentiation strategy is cost-adjusted efficiency, not raw capability. Real users "aren't willing to wait" — latency hurts retention more than quality does. The Flash series is optimized for billion-user scale, the opposite philosophy from Anthropic/OpenAI's frontier push.

[Source](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-model-eats-the-scaffolding-deepmind-s-logan-kilpatrick-tulsee-doshi-on-3-5-flash-omni-more/)

### ★★★ [Strategy] · Cognitive Revolution (2026-05)

Selective openness: list Anthropic models on Cloud Marketplace and use Exa for search grounding even though Google Search exists. Give enterprises choice, but vertically integrate your own product — a two-layer ecosystem.

[Source](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-model-eats-the-scaffolding-deepmind-s-logan-kilpatrick-tulsee-doshi-on-3-5-flash-omni-more/)

### ★★★ [Engineering & Management] · Cognitive Revolution (2026-05)

"The model eats the scaffolding. Every crank of the model flywheel, the model eats a bunch of scaffolding." DeepMind now ships a complete agent harness instead of a bare model — and simultaneously trains for "harness diversity" so the model generalizes outside Google.

[Source](https://www.cognitiverevolution.ai/the-model-eats-the-scaffolding-deepmind-s-logan-kilpatrick-tulsee-doshi-on-3-5-flash-omni-more/)
