### ★★★ [Strategy] · Sequoia Founder Spotlight (2026)

Refounding in practice: first time (Kyoto, 2015) — laid everyone off and restarted; second time (Cancun, 2023, post-GPT-4) — pivoted immediately. "Only a founder has the moral high ground to refound — non-founder-led companies struggle to do this." Notion deliberately acquires founders; they now have 50–60 ex-founders on staff.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)

### ★★★ [Strategy] · Sequoia Founder Spotlight (2026)

"Steel for organizations" metaphor: steel turned NYC from six-story buildings into skyscrapers; LLMs + software are the "steel" of org structure. The real shape is small hierarchical clusters with AI passing information between them — not Dorsey's totally flat model.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)

### ★★★ [Hiring] · Sequoia Founder Spotlight (2026)

Talent = Capability(experience) × Taste(value system) × Agency(will). LLMs commoditize capability, but taste and agency can't be retrofitted → barbell shape: super-junior + super-senior. One senior architect manages 2–3 juniors, each junior manages 4–6 coding agents.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)

### ★★★ [Hiring] · Sequoia Founder Spotlight (2026)

Sales hiring doesn't look at resumes — first round is "build something for us." "Don't reinvent the sales motion — the modern enterprise sales playbook is mature; you only get to put your innovation in 1 or 2 places."

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)

### ★★★ [Product] · Sequoia Founder Spotlight (2026)

In 2025 Notion eliminated the CMO role, splitting it into Storytelling (next to product) and Demand Gen (next to sales), each plugged directly into product and GTM.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)

### ★★★ [Engineering & Management] · Sequoia Founder Spotlight (2026)

"Building with LLMs is brewing beer, not engineering bridges" — you can't tell the yeast which flavor profile to hit. Designer + engineer + PM all sit around one cask, working through evals and experience, not PM→designer→engineer waterfall.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)
