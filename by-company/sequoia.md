## Pat Grady + Sonya Huang + Konstantine Buhler

### ★★★ [Strategy] · AI Ascent 2026 Keynote (2026)

The "MAD" framework: Moats (reason from the customer, not from technology — customers change slower than tech) + Affordance (the product has to be self-explanatory the way a hammer is to a two-year-old — Claude Code is powerful but Fortune 500 employees can't open a terminal = affordance gap) + Diffusion gap (frontier capabilities are absorbed far slower than they're produced — that gap is the startup window).

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: The Memories.ai public API is a textbook "powerful but low-affordance" case — 77 fragmented endpoints, no unified upload, Lucy Desktop forced to build its own BFF. The next step has to be a redesign around affordance.

## Pat Grady

### ★★★ [Strategy] · AI Ascent 2026 Keynote (2026)

"AI is a revolution in computation, not communication" — Internet/Cloud/Mobile were all information-distribution revolutions; AI is an information-processing revolution. "The floor keeps moving — no lead is safe, but anybody can win."

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: don't position as "another information-distribution platform" (video search). Reposition as "information-processing infrastructure" (personal memory = an external brain).

## Sonya Huang

### ★★★ [Strategy] · AI Ascent 2026 Keynote (2026)

"Services is the new software" — global software TAM is ~$650B, but legal services in the US alone is $400B. There's a $10T services market for agents to eat. Agentic apps drive 10–40× productivity gains (vs. the prior 10–40%).

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: don't just sell APIs — sell "personal memory as a service." The API should evolve toward service-shaped endpoints (not an SDK, but "hire an agent").

### ★★★ [Engineering & Management] · AI Ascent 2026 Keynote (2026)

The agenticness spectrum: tab autocomplete → agentic dev → background / async agents → dark factories (no human review, straight to prod). Bret Taylor rebuilt Sierra over a weekend; Notion rewrote 8M lines in 6 weeks; "what you imagined over 100 years is now possible in 100 days."

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: for the infrastructure team — can agents auto-generate SDKs / docs / the multi-tenant layer / billing for xvu's ~454 endpoints?

## Konstantine Buhler

### ★★ [Strategy] · AI Ascent 2026 Keynote (2026)

The "aluminum/electrolysis" analogy: PhD-level skills are like aluminum in the 1880s (precious). AI = electrolysis, making aluminum dirt cheap. "Skills that took decades to earn are so instantly invoked you can crumple them."

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: The "high-end capabilities" in the Memories.ai public API (VLM, vector search, lifelog segmentation) are exactly the PhD-skills being rapidly commoditized. The moat is in affordance + customer wrapping.
