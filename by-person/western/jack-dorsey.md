## Sequoia Founder Spotlight

Date: 2026 · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTVSwOY19Qs)

### ★★★ [Strategy]

A company is a mini-AGI. Hierarchy exists only because information flow is too slow. Put all artifacts (Slack / PRs / Docs) into an intelligence layer and let every employee query the company — not just the CEO. This is "Dorsey mode" — intelligence as the center of the circle.

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: our ~454-endpoint infrastructure (xvu + xvu-infrastructure) is currently a hierarchy. It should be turned into organizational AGI so both Lucy teams and Memories.ai's own product teams query the same brain.

### ★★★ [Strategy]

"The customer's roadmap is the company's limiting factor." 4 layers: capabilities (cards/lending) → interfaces (Square/Cash) → proactive intelligence (money is the most honest signal) → world model.

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: We should be 4-layered too: capabilities (vector/VLM/ASR primitives) → interfaces (Lucy App/Desktop) → proactive (what to highlight today) → world model (this user's portrait).

### ★★★ [Strategy]

Layoff of 40% at Block (decided Dec 2024 — everyone played with new tools over the holidays and came back saying "we wouldn't build a company this size today"). Decision process: ① minimum headcount to keep services up ② minimum to satisfy regulators ③ minimum to grow and rebuild as an intelligence company, plus buffer. Three weeks total.

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: do we need a similar exercise? "If we were starting Memories.ai's public API + Lucy infra from scratch today, what size would it be?" The answer is almost certainly much smaller than where we are now.

### ★★★ [Hiring]

A board = a hire you can never fire (and they can fire you). Treat board members as a recruiting decision, not a financing decision. "A founder has a different relationship with independent vs. investor board members." Build the board before a crisis tests it.

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: stress-test our board composition against this framework — is the truly independent / mentor seat actually filled?

### ★★★ [Engineering & Management]

Block's reorg: only 3 roles — IC (builder, "judgment + taste + creativity") + DRI (customer outcomes, "ownership + accountability") + Player Coach ("capability building, shown not told"). One person can be all three at once. Max depth 5 → target 2-3 → ideally 1 (all 6,000 people reporting up to Jack).

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: At Memories.ai's thousand-person scale, try the 3-role experiment in 1-2 BUs — e.g., have the mavi-agent team operate fully as IC/DRI/Player Coach.

### ★★★ [Engineering & Management]

"Meetings 2 months ago = slide deck review. Now = everyone brings a prototype, simulated or real data, modifiable in real time. Cost of being wrong is approaching zero — explore widely, then judgment pulls a focused thread."

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: ban pure decks in internal product reviews — require a working prototype + data. mintlify-docs should be a live playground, not static text.
