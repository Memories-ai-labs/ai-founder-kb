Date: 2026 · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRo33rnv6rQ)

### ★★★ [Strategy]

The "MAD" framework: Moats (reason from the customer, not from technology — customers change slower than tech) + Affordance (the product has to be self-explanatory the way a hammer is to a two-year-old — Claude Code is powerful but Fortune 500 employees can't open a terminal = affordance gap) + Diffusion gap (frontier capabilities are absorbed far slower than they're produced — that gap is the startup window).

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: The Memories.ai public API is a textbook "powerful but low-affordance" case — 77 fragmented endpoints, no unified upload, Lucy Desktop forced to build its own BFF. The next step has to be a redesign around affordance.
