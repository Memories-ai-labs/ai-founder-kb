## Sequoia Training Data

Date: 2026 · [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jq3BIGz4vXQ)

### ★★★ [Product]

"Music is not a scale problem like LLM. Models are small. Scale is less helpful. There are no benchmarks for music." Suno deliberately threw out structured priors like Western 12-tone, letting the model learn raw 48kHz sound waves — because telling it "there are only 12 tones" forever caps it.

### ★★★ [Product]

"90% of Suno DAU create — creation IS the entertainment." Parallels gaming and Claude Code: people enjoy building the thing more than consuming it. This is a new "creative entertainment" category — fundamentally different from Spotify (passive consumption).

### ★★ [Strategy]

"The Warner Music partnership turned out friendlier than expected — the industry isn't cooked, it's a new collaboration surface. The AI vs. non-AI music dichotomy is meaningless; most music will end up with 'some AI in it.'"

### ★★ [Product]

"I picked auto-regression over diffusion not for tech reasons but product reasons — a full 3-minute song > a crisp 12-second clip. Story matters more than fidelity."
