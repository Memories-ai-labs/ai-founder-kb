## Sequoia Training Data

Date: 2026 · [Source](https://sequoiacap.com/podcast/harvey-ceo-winston-weinberg-why-you-should-reinvent-yourself-every-4-months/)

### ★★★ [Strategy]

Attack the hardest customers: Fortune 100 in-house legal + elite law firms. Their brutal requirements become the whole industry's design partner; mid-market is easy but yields little strategic learning.

### ★★★ [Hiring]

First test for an exec hire: can they map a 3/6/12-month roadmap for their org, including specific hires? Most leaders fail — it exposes their ability to think in terms of leverage.

### ★★★ [Hiring]

Title design: "Head of" > "Director" — titles signal ownership accountability. One DRI per initiative to prevent "plant-watering" responsibility diffusion.

### ★★★ [Product]

"The times I'm best at product are when I'm selling the most" — sell and build in parallel to prevent ivory-tower roadmap mistakes.

### ★★★ [Engineering & Management]

"Penalize inaction, not failure": "I would much rather people just try and make a decision and then it's wrong, and a week later they adjust." Speed beats decision accuracy.

### ★★★ [Engineering & Management]

The four-month cycle: "Every four months, I'd say, I get this mental block that there's too many things going wrong." Every 4 months you need to reinvent yourself — different leadership hires, structural changes.
