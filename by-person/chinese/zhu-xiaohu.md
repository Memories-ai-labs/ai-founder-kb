## Zhang Xiaojun Business Interview PodcastEP122

Date: 2025-12-09 · [Source](https://www.xiaoyuzhoufm.com/episode/693834013fec3166cf262bd0)

### ★★★ [Strategy]

"Leaving a big tech company meant crossing one street in the mobile internet era; in the AI era you need to cross three." "We tilt 15 degrees off — wherever consensus clusters, we don't touch."

### ★★★ [Strategy]

"AI application founders today have to be brave enough to admit they have no moat. Anyone claiming technical defensibility is fooling you." The moat lies outside AI: editing capability, complex workflow integration, ecosystem.

### ★★★ [Strategy]

"In China, everything's basically priced like a discount vegetable; you have to earn your margins overseas." Japan, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East "have no real competitors."

### ★★★ [Strategy]

Bullish on Doubao, Qwen, Lavart (designer-ecosystem moat), Bambu Lab, and Plaud. Deeply pessimistic about LLM startup monetization ("big tech is giving tokens away too cheap").

### ★★★ [Fundraising]

PMF/ARR yardstick: hitting $2M ARR in 3 months is the passing bar; if you can't hit $5M in 12 months, no one will pay attention. For B2B SaaS, at least 1B RMB of revenue per year.
