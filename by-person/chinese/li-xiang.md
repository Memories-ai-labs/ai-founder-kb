## Luo Yonghao's Crossroads EP1

Date: 2025-08-19 · [Source](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1941583210623505759)

### ★★ [Strategy]

Three traits of top-tier founders: pick the right thing + pick something durable + iterate at extreme frequency. "Don't die chasing perfection."

### ★★ [Hiring]

Time management: every day, focus on only the three most important things — a common trait Li Xiang distills from Jensen Huang, Wang Xing, and Zhang Yiming.

### ★★ [Product]

Tried "one-person company" for half a year — completely unworkable. You need 3–5 people, or a dozen-plus + AI, to actually build a big company.
