**3. HF/Ai2: Where scientists draw the line on AI agents (Nov 25)**
AllenAI study of 17 scientists on what responsible AI agents must do: five dimensions of scientific trust (validation, privacy, objectivity, accountability, transparency). Key finding: scientists who delegate still must verify — they can't outsource trust. Proposes participatory benchmarks that scientists co-define. Important counterweight to hype around autonomous scientific AI.
