Mechanical Turk shutting its doors to new customers marks a formal end-of-era for human-crowdsourced AI training data infrastructure. The backstory is revealing: by 2023, 33-46% of MTurk workers were using LLMs to complete their own tasks — the crowdsourced human data collection layer was already quietly automated. For AI founders building data pipelines, the lesson is clear: the entire pre-LLM training data supply chain is obsolete. Synthetic data and model self-improvement are the replacement.
