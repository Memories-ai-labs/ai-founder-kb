## Highlights

**1. GLM-5.2 — strongest Chinese open-source model for coding agents (Jun 17)**
Z.AI (Zhipu AI) released GLM-5.2 via Hugging Face: an open-source model with 1M reliable context, MIT license (no regional restrictions), and benchmark performance within 1% of Claude Opus 4.8 on FrontierSWE. Technical innovations include IndexShare (2.9× FLOP reduction at 1M context) and anti-reward-hacking measures for agentic RL. For AI founders: a credible open-source alternative to closed frontier models for coding/agentic pipelines — especially relevant given the ongoing US government restrictions on Anthropic Fable/Mythos access.

**2. Pramaana Labs raises $27M from Khosla for formal verification + AI (Jun 17)**
Pramaana Labs is combining LLMs with LEAN mathematical verification to guarantee reliability in high-stakes domains (law, drug discovery, tax). Khosla-led $27M seed. This represents a structural approach to the hallucination problem: rather than prompting models to be accurate, mathematically verifying their outputs against formal proofs. Signals growing investor thesis that enterprise AI reliability requires deterministic verification layers, not just better models.

**3. Strands Agents + LeRobot: Hub-to-hardware robotics pipeline (Jun 17)**
AWS published an integration guide showing how to connect Hugging Face Hub models directly to physical robots using Strands Robots SDK + LeRobot — recording demos in MuJoCo simulation, pushing datasets to Hub, and deploying identical code to real SO-101 robots with one parameter change. Supports GR00T, π0, SmolVLA, Cosmos 3. Signals that the "model hub → physical deployment" pipeline for robotics is becoming productized infrastructure, not research-only.
