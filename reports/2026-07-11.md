**2. GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra proves the Cycle Double Cover Conjecture (488 HN pts)**
OpenAI released a formal proof of the Cycle Double Cover Conjecture — an unsolved open problem in graph theory — generated by GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra. This is one of the first credible instances of a frontier AI model producing an original proof of a previously-unsolved mathematical conjecture (not just verifying known results). For founders: this shifts the evaluation frame for AI reasoning from "impressive demos" to "demonstrable scientific progress," with implications for research automation, deep-tech AI products, and how to position AI capability claims to technical audiences.
