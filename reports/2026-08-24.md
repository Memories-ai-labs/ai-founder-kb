## Highlights

**Latent Space — Dan McAteer "The Evolution of the Agent Harness" (Aug 22)**: The most analytically rigorous essay to date on how agent harnesses and model capabilities co-evolved. Key insight: the "harness curve" and "model capability curve" were misaligned for most of 2023–24 (harness too ambitious, then too conservative), and only crossed at the right moment when Claude Code launched in Feb 2025. Harness-Bench data shows a 23.8-point accuracy spread across harnesses with identical model weights — half the agent is the harness. The essay's prediction: models keep absorbing harness capabilities into weights, and what remains becomes a "harness for human attention" rather than model scaffolding. Directly actionable for AI product builders.
