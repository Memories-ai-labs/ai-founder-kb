It cannot generate free-form text. Claims 20-200x speed advantage and 40-400x cost reduction vs frontier models. Topped HN on launch. Represents a significant architectural shift in agentic system design: separating fast, cheap classification (System One) from slower, expensive reasoning (System Two). Directly actionable for founders building multi-step agentic pipelines where classification/routing is the bottleneck.

**OpenAI, Anthropic, Google coordinating on safety standards for weeks (TechCrunch, Sept 15)**: Three frontier labs confirmed to be in talks to establish industry-wide safety standards, partly in response to Dario Amodei's public call to slow AI development. Faces antitrust scrutiny and Trump administration opposition. If successful, this creates a de facto safety cartel that smaller labs and open-weight developers will have to respond to — Cohere's "AI safety cartel" warning from Sept 13 now looks prescient. Relevant for AI founders thinking about regulatory capture risk.
