**2. Lenny's Podcast — Annual AI Sentiment Survey 2026 (July 12): Workforce bifurcation hardening**
Second-year survey reveals four archetypes (Energized, Conflicted, Disoriented, Resentful) with burnout up 11 points year-over-year. No surveyed tech worker would recommend entering the industry to newcomers. Key for founders: managers are "the single biggest lever for employee well-being" — human leadership is becoming a competitive advantage as AI handles more execution; also, job displacement ranks lower than burnout and mismanagement in worker concerns, suggesting AI talent anxiety is more about culture/quality than raw displacement fear.

**3. HN: Nvidia/CoreWeave/Nebius circular GPU financing (312 pts)**
Analysis exposing the circular capital structure underlying the GPU boom: Nvidia sells on credit to CoreWeave/Nebius → they rent compute to AI startups → startups pay Nvidia API fees, closing the loop. For founders: a must-read on the structural fragility of current AI compute infrastructure. If AI revenue growth slows, the circular structure unwinds and compute costs rise. Informs decisions on compute vendor diversification and build-vs-buy for inference.
