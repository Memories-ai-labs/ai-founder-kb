## Ivan Zhao (Notion) — 2026

## Ivan Zhao (Notion) — 2026

**★★★** [Strategy]: Refounding in practice: first time (Kyoto, 2015) — laid everyone off and restarted; second time (Cancun, 2023, post-GPT-4) — pivoted immediately. "Only a founder has the moral high ground to refound — non-founder-led companies struggle to do this." Notion deliberately acquires founders; they now have 50–60 ex-founders on staff.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)

## Jack Dorsey (Block) — 2026

**★★★** [Strategy]: A company is a mini-AGI. Hierarchy exists only because information flow is too slow. Put all artifacts (Slack / PRs / Docs) into an intelligence layer and let every employee query the company — not just the CEO. This is "Dorsey mode" — intelligence as the center of the circle.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTVSwOY19Qs)

## Jack Dorsey (Block) — 2026

Decision process: ① minimum headcount to keep services up ② minimum to satisfy regulators ③ minimum to grow and rebuild as an intelligence company, plus buffer. Three weeks total.

## Ivan Zhao (Notion) — 2026

**★★★** [Hiring]: Talent = Capability(experience) × Taste(value system) × Agency(will). LLMs commoditize capability, but taste and agency can't be retrofitted → barbell shape: super-junior + super-senior. One senior architect manages 2–3 juniors, each junior manages 4–6 coding agents.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ill76IbVuM8)
