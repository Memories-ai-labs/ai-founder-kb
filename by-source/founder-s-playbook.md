## Anthropic Team (Anthropic) — 2026-05-14

**★★★** [Strategy]: Four-stage map: Idea = expand the search space; MVP = compress time-to-implementation; Launch = accelerate messaging/distribution experiments; Scale = turn the process into reusable workflows.

**Memories.ai implication**: Memories.ai implication: platform companies win at "Scale = process templating" but actually move slower than single-app companies at "expanding the search space" in the Idea stage.
