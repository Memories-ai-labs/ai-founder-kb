# #06 — Bottom-Up Adoption, Not Enterprise-First

## One-Line Summary

n8n abandoned lead-generation goals in favor of "large-org adoption" and grew revenue 4× in 8 months through community-driven distribution. "Nobody went enterprise-first to own a space." Replit hires from hackathons / crypto communities / 16-year-olds.

## Supporting Evidence (Independent Sources)

n8n on Sequoia / Replit on Sequoia / Cohere explicitly refusing hyperscaler money / Pieter Levels on Cheeky Pint / Manus mass-market pricing vs Devin at $500/mo

## Action Implication for Memories.ai

The current enterprise-first sales path is an anti-pattern in the AI era. GTM should: (1) generous free tier (enough to build a full demo), (2) hackathons + SDK example repos instead of enterprise pilots, (3) developer community before sales team.
