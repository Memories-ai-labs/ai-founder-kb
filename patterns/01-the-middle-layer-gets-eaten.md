# #01 — The Middle Layer Gets Eaten

## One-Line Summary

The defining storyline of 2025-26: application-layer and primitive-layer both have a future, but the middle layer (model wrapper / light orchestration) is being squeezed to death from both sides — by frontier labs from below and by incumbents from above. Cursor, Manus, and Monica are all case studies.

## Supporting Evidence (Independent Sources)

Cursor ($30B valuation but squeezed by Claude Code) / Manus (sold to Meta, citing inability to absorb complexity alone) / Monica (founder admits tool-shaped products are easily replaced) / DeepMind's "The model eats the scaffolding" / Zhang Xiaojun Business Interview EP128
